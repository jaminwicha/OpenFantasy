# Roadmap, progress and documentation audit

The project has substantial durable simulation and increasingly real shared gameplay, but **P02 is
not close to a defensible complete-phase acceptance**. P00/P01 also retain foundation/export/catalog
obligations. The full production game remains much larger than the recent control/UI queue. The
[current roadmap](../CURRENT_ROADMAP.md) now gives one living phase ledger, complete P02 closure table,
binding later decisions and ordered next work. No frozen requirement or acceptance gate was removed.

## What the audit establishes

Two native critics independently examined complete requirements/phase obligations and measured
progress/workflow cost. A separate authorized Fable review completed and was critically integrated.
The [requirements critic](ROADMAP_CRITIC.md), [all 181 mapping](requirement-matrix.json),
[progress audit](PROGRESS_AND_EFFICIENCY.md), [metrics](metrics.json) and
[Fable disposition](FABLE_DISPOSITION.md) retain evidence, disagreement and limits. This is not a
new full-runtime or whole-game playtest. The matrix preserves exact source text/hash/phase mappings;
it does not fabricate181 implementation-completion judgments.

Nothing found here justifies restarting the engine or reversing the pure-C# decision. Reuse the
retained authority and actual composed systems, then prove their common player journey and the
remaining hard scale/history cases. The main correction is execution focus and honest closure.

The most important gaps are the integrated NPC motivation/memory/offscreen-work and recruitment loop,
meaningful changed-place return, missing-cargo investigation/cooperative puzzle, environmental and
ecological examples, native art/motion/audio, earned/remappable interfaces and human acceptance.
The ordinary launcher still selects an older construction farm. Advanced admissions do compose real
systems, but a new player does not encounter the breadth suggested by recent packet summaries.

The broad roadmap is accurate in intended scope. Its frozen choices require explicit later overlays
(stable runtime names and pure C# especially), and its execution status was dispersed across lengthy
handoffs. The old next-task queue underrepresented entire P02 obligations. New work should serve a
single discoverable, persistent journey while retaining the complete phase exit, not keep extending
one selected-character subsystem indefinitely. Unmet native/recovery requirements are not waived.

The complete game still requires P03's exact-time/mixed-clock/history/package feasibility, P04–P10's
domain breadth, P11's full semantic migration and finite production catalog, P12's campaign/scale/
quality, P13's actual three-desktop release and P14's later mobile acceptance. All 12 inspected catalog
shipping targets remain unresolved. That prevents an honest completed-content denominator or ETA.
Preserved predecessor baselines remain fixed; later predecessor deltas need separate reconciliation.

## Progress and efficiency judgment

Recent actual progress spans authenticated four-human play and shared gifts, production, recipes,
construction, trade, care and History with durable consequences. Earlier work established finite
physical/material systems, trading/cargo/danger and the portable authority. These are valuable retained
foundations. Their rate cannot be extrapolated to content, ecology, narrative, world history or art.

The measured record includes 274 Beta-path commits from September 8 through the audit's accepted
checkpoint, 132 P02 packets, 67 packet check/finalize scripts and 5.92GB of logical implementation-file
contents. These are activity/maintenance indicators, not features, active work hours, compressed disk
size or model credits. Six identical-source Core sweeps cost 11.4minutes for 11,310 executions; actual
stamped binary differences justified the current reruns, so blanket skipping would be invalid.

Keep correctness/feasibility gates. Improve selection and reuse: accepted player journeys as the
progress unit, explicit complete phase exits, exact dependency reuse, one maintained checker/runner,
short linked status and failure-cause classification. A future artifact consolidation must prove
retrieval/integrity and positive/negative oracle equivalence before changing retained evidence.
Avoid a large new governance/tooling project in response to this audit.

Fable supplied useful prioritization criticism but also overclaimed absent systems, source-only test
reuse and permission to declare control sufficient. Lead review rejected these. Its38-profile count
was correct; native audit prose originally miscounted the listed ranges as 37 and was corrected. The
cargo route has 14 regions; the complete admitted root retains 15. No critic is an automatic authority.
The external run took 456.468seconds and reported $5.7991455 list-price estimate; neither that number
nor native parallelism proves subscription-credit savings. No provider retry or fallback occurred.
