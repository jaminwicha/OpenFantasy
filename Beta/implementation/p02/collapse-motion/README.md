## Policy ownership and archive

`ConstructionLoadPolicy` owns exact scope, complete bearing templates, stable source/material anchors
and body/item mass calibration. Identity roles cannot alias, a source cannot back duplicate foundations,
and every template requires an acyclic load order even before its definition is installed. Weak or
unsupported acyclic assemblies remain load-resolution inputs; rejecting cycles does not declare them
safe. Binding checks the complete admitted construction catalog before selecting installed assemblies.

The six-field v1 archive has an expected content hash, canonical ordering, exact re-encoding, checked
numbers and bounded rows. Maximum fixtures use 16 definitions, 32 members and 496 legal directed edges
per template, 4,096 foundations, 64 bodies and 256 supplemental items. That archive bound does not promise
a 4,096-foundation graph plus additional actors/members: the graph shares one 4,096-element allowance.
The policy hash covers inactive definitions and unused calibration. The existing assessment's `InputPin`
is narrower; a durable pending owner must separately include the complete policy hash.

## Descent and real-world composition

`VerticalDescentQuery` accepts copied bounded candidate geometry without prematurely demanding a stable
world. It searches every solid cell top, material collider top and stationary actor head, then proves the
entire swept body prefix to the first contact. Partial ledges, nonbearing colliders and unknown terrain
cannot be skipped to obtain a lower, more convenient landing. Same-plane full-footprint footing is
required. A clear finite horizon is explicitly not a landing; actor contact is not a floor.

The hosted fixture uses actual quest-funded NPC construction and ordinary player/NPC movement in fresh
physical profile thirteen. Supplied obstacle omissions model candidate moving closures only; they are
not terrain edits, member retirement or publication. Its retained observations show:

| Candidate query | Checked result |
|---|---|
| Player with only deck omitted | Floor at Z256, 576-subunit descent |
| Deck with its two legs still present | Incomplete support at zero drop; assembly material is not globally ignored |
| Deck with exact deck/leg moving closure omitted | Floor at Z256, 512-subunit descent |
| Player after exact deck material is reintroduced at that endpoint | Deck top Z320, 512-subunit descent |
| Player above an actual NPC moved under the deck | Actor contact at head Z720, 112-subunit descent |
| Deck above that NPC | Actor contact at head Z720, 48-subunit descent |

Both the source world and closed SQLite history remain unchanged by these queries. The separate policy
fixture decodes an archive against its expected hash, binds the actual occupied world and recovers the
exact graph. Its authored weak-right-leg scenario remains stable intact; a supplied left-foundation
failure cascades through deck/player load with conserved mass. Neither fixture publishes its scenario.
