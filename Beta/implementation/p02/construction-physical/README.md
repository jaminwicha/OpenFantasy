# Durable physical construction ownership

Scope: separate retained fabrication from each installed member's physical lifecycle in a fresh dry
world profile. This is a dependency for coupled collapse, not live collapse or a closed P02 gate.

## Implemented contract

- `ConstructionPhysicalSnapshot` covers all installed members exactly once, pinned to actual construction.
  Stable identities, revisions, integer condition, signed displacement and disposition retain original
  material provenance and conserved mass/volume. One member is not a newly spendable installed lot.
- Atomic member updates preserve complete collision material; zero condition and falling material do not
  disappear. Bearing surfaces exclude failed/falling material. Exact interpenetration, complete coverage,
  original assembly grouping and existing finite bounds remain enforced.
- Stable-world admission checks every exact face-connected component's lowest members, not only the
  original assembly minimum. Floating components and `Falling` publications are rejected. Existing
  outward subunit quantization is explicit. Supplied displaced poses prove geometry representation,
  not an executed fall, landing, impact or structural safety.
- Fresh unplayed profile-twelve admission creates profile thirteen. Its direct eighteen-field root decodes
  construction and physical state before reconstructed geometry and actor admission. Old profiles keep
  their bytes; no save migration or default-farm switch occurs.
- Real funded completion creates all twelve fixture member identities atomically with fabrication,
  production, reserved output and a receipt. Historical identity collision rolls the whole action back.
  Partial labor updates the fabrication pin without inventing early members or physical revisions.
- The ordinary action gate validates candidate component support before publication. Excavation checks its
  complete proposed hole before spending partial labor, yielding a refusal when support cannot be kept.
  The selected advancing step still contributes its ordinary time/effort recovery once.
- The load binder consumes retained member condition; diagnostic overrides and detached placements needing
  a new contact interpretation are refused. Host inspections expose original material backing and physical
  state. Actual damage/displacement commands and universal force admission remain separate work.

See [verification summary](qualification/verification-summary.json) for final counts, process evidence,
fixture recreation, preservation checks and remaining acceptance boundaries. Early reports are retained

The retained fixture earns its quest funding, NPC work, installation, player stair travel and recorded

mass allocation, full raw-source retirement, sole installed output and exact SQLite identity/receipt/
journal rows. Real Host processes qualify ordinary completion, restart, retry, reverse recorded history
and external kills immediately before and after transaction commit.

Review found two related admission hazards. First, a detached member could float when only the original
assembly's minimum was checked. Exact component support and separate grounded/floating/Falling tests
resolve that gap. Second, legacy excavation geometry could accept a hole beneath a higher detached
component, deferring failure to final world construction. Pre-work and candidate-action checks turn that
into an atomic ordinary refusal. These checks do not lower support requirements or pretend to resolve
collapse. A bounded native critic rechecked the contact contract; no external Claude/Fable call was used.

## Next dependency and retained limits

Physical lifecycle ownership does not close support-removal consequences. Next work must bind durable
load policy, prepare the full affected closure and resolve pending work without awarding extra world time.
Excavation must retain its final swing until terrain, conserved material, members, occupants, damage and
receipts can publish atomically. Recompute complete actor contact and clear falling/landing paths;
missing terrain is not empty space. Preserve source identities and avoid duplicate future reservations.

Detached members retain the original installed output; fracture/debris transformation and widely separated
collision groups need explicit conserved ownership. Pending-job dependency pins exclude their own cursor.
Movement and carried-goods changes also affect loads; mining alone is not a universal gate. Resting load
paths, natural strata, wet containment/displacement, production strength/latency, multi-floor presentation,
art, devices and all full P00–P14 acceptance remain open. No source design or baseline requirement is removed.
