# Consented custody: complete integration path

This work supplies the missing agreement boundary for gifts and later exchanges. It does not
replace the original continued-world personal-medicine objective, mark an unsupported source
as usable, or close P01/P02. The retained world remains unchanged until an explicitly admitted
runtime operation can change it legally.

## Authority boundary

An outgoing proposal belongs to the source owner; acceptance belongs to the destination owner.
Both bind the same exact transaction. Host-verified deliveries and current actor control provide
authority; copying principal/session identifiers into a payload does not. Treatment permission,
friendship, party membership and NPC control supply no implicit inventory access.

The first component exercises real raw counted-source movement, preserving source intervals,
capacity, protection, identity and revisions. It joins two independently authenticated approvals
at one decision boundary. It does not claim an asynchronous invitation, a saved offer, spatial
access, elapsed time, production provenance or durable publication. Existing unilateral transfer
keeps its rules and byte contracts. The implementation assignment owns this first component;
the remaining bindings below are required work.

## Remaining bindings and evidence

| Binding | Required behavior and evidence |
|---|---|
| Produced goods | Extend the actual production owner with exact transformed-output transfer and original producer/consumption provenance. Do not manufacture a raw-source claim for a crafted item. Mixed ownership, output splitting and reserved construction stock must remain coherent. |
| Continuing material history | Record the actual agreed transfer alongside other material operations; retain both approvals, participant roles, exact command/result identity and source conservation across archive cuts. Historical command and retired-lot identities remain occupied. Retry after recovery returns the original authorized receipt without another movement. |
| Complete world and durable authority | Authenticate both participants through actual Host control/session boundaries; join body/reach, readiness, access, custody, production, receipts and timing in one world transaction. Pin the complete candidate and publish once. Old profiles and genesis remain exact; any additional world interpretation must be explicit, never a silent rewrite of the existing save. |
| Asynchronous proposal lifecycle | If offer and acceptance occur at different times, persist the owner's actual proposal and withdrawal/expiry evidence. Acceptance rechecks current stock, control, reach and exact terms. A proposal is not a reservation or a control credential. Reserve goods only through the existing material reservation owner when the product deliberately promises availability. Restart, disconnect, reordered commands, competing use and changed terms must have defined outcomes. |
| Player and NPC behavior | Both use the same operations. A helper can offer owned medicine based on a real policy/commitment; the player chooses whether to receive it. Conversely, an NPC can accept or decline a player's gift using its own decision. No forced acceptance, guessed private preferences or privileged inventory mutation. |
| Native presentation | Show the named parties, exact item/icon and quantity, relevant limitations and a separate acceptance action with rich inspection. Cancel, close, focus loss or stale selection must not act or retry. Keep requests, pending offers, completed receipts and treatment permission visibly distinct. Use capability/context filtering so this does not overload the ordinary action wheel. |
| Full joined journey | Acquire a real dose through the admitted bilateral route, spend it through ordinary self-use, and prove exact material/resource/care/time effects through native input, reopened state, recorded history and archive authentication. Preserve the prior 520-dose ledger and its failed acquisition attempts. A separate fresh-world demonstration is useful evidence but does not close this retained-world objective. |
| Exchange and economy | Build reciprocal goods/payment exchange on complete atomic material/currency transactions with real stock, quote knowledge and acceptance. An accepted gift is not proof of a market, price evolution or the P02 two-market cargo/escort circuit. |

## Acceptance discipline

Each binding must have actual executable evidence before its status changes. A serializable approval
record alone proves neither authorization nor publication. A green component test proves neither
network delivery nor native interaction. Actor and command-role identity collisions, old-history
replays, split-child reuse, stale terms, loss of control, changed capacity and reserved/protected
stock require rejection without partial effects.

The existing source world may require an explicit supported rules/content transition before new
operations can run. That is a real integration question, not permission to alter its saved genesis,
inventory policies, medicine definitions or historical journal. Keep the full original objective
visible when choosing that transition. No production or human acceptance threshold is relaxed.
