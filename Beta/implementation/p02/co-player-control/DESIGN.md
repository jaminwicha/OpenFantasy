# Consented co-player control

## Full dependency and delivery boundaries

The preserved technical design requires a permitted co-player or conservative autonomous behavior
after the recorded disconnect allowance. Presence alone is not permission. Original character identity,
possessions, accepted work and the returning owner's deliberate Resume must remain intact.

The complete dependency is delivered through these connected increments:

1. Durable named permission: owner offer, separate named co-player acceptance, exact terms,
   replacement/revocation, canonical history and recovery under the existing world authority.
2. Effective command authority: distinct active assignment, original disconnect episode and control
   revision, acceptance-time witness, truthful submitting principal and character attribution, current
   authorization for new decisions and retained authorization for accepted work/history.
3. Player control selection: an immutable authenticated home connection plus explicitly selected
   character, fresh observation-bound Resume, bounded input/query state and immediate refusal of new
   delegated decisions when the owner returns or revokes permission.
4. Actual local/TLS/Godot interaction, archive/reopen/crash proofs and retained full production gates.

The first increment records consent. It does not claim co-player takeover, change permanent
`Inventory.Controls`, authorize gameplay as another character, expose that character's private data,
or admit a production world into a new interpretation automatically. The following increments remain
required; this division does not narrow the design.

## Permission law

Fresh explicit world profile 39 admits an empty permission owner over an unplayed profile-38 world.
Earlier canonical encodings and storage interpretations remain unchanged. Each ordered owner/grantee
home-actor pair has one bounded slot (at most twelve for a four-player roster), with a monotonic slot
revision. A current offer records both immutable home bindings, the grant command identity and exact
terms. Acceptance identifies that offer, revision and terms; replacement invalidates old acceptance.
Revocation clears the effective offer while retaining the pair's revision. Historical journal identity
occupancy prevents recycled grant/acceptance identities without unbounded live grant history.

Offer and acceptance require their original home entitlement and human assignment. Revocation belongs
to the original owner even during a held or absent-control boundary. A disconnected identity claim on
the wire is not authentication: the trusted host still owns admission. No operation can delegate
onward or manufacture presence, readiness, movement, time, supplies or a command on behalf of the owner.

Recorded capabilities are Combat, Traversal, Recovery, Inventory, Trade, Production,
ConversationAndQuests, CareDisposition and PrivateHistory. The provisional simple default is
Combat + Traversal + Recovery. The owner was asked about this default; no answer is recorded at this
design checkpoint. Every offer carries explicit terms. Recovery does not imply cancellation/decline
of another accepted care obligation. Inventory, commerce and private history require separate scope.
Capability storage alone supplies no execution authorization. Later dispatch must define each action's
required scope and retain ordinary costs, skills, custody, reach and target rules.

Permission actions use a dedicated, isolated, nonadvancing one-command boundary with exact ordinary
receipts. They can be recorded while the world is held without releasing the hold or interrupting
accepted work. Only bookkeeping revisions and the permission facet change. New command and world
codecs are explicit; old commands keep their exact bytes. Storage admits the new boundary in an
explicit schema under the same transaction, writer lease and recovery fence.

## Required authority and interaction integration

Permission is eligibility, not possession. Takeover additionally requires the matching expired owner
disconnect episode, a safe action boundary and explicit acquisition by the actually connected grantee.
Keep B's principal/session while B operates A; do not synthesize an owner delivery or replace inventory
bindings. A typed authorization context must bind the exact actor, command, grant and control revision.
Movement needs equivalent attribution. A retained witness is not proof by itself: complete replay
authenticates issuance, acceptance and assignment at their original predecessor states.

Original-owner return or revocation prevents new delegated choices immediately; accepted retreat,
excavation, care and other commitments retain their acceptance-time authority and finish exactly once.
Closed paid quotes/material outcomes remain historically valid after revocation; outstanding offers
must revalidate permission before execution. These records currently retain delivery information and
need versioned authority evidence as part of the second increment.

An original submitting principal must be able to retrieve its exact old command receipt after
revocation without gaining new action or private-view access. Current permission must not be used to
invalidate prior history, and a plausible witness must not authenticate a fabricated history.

Selecting A cannot silently make B's own character autonomous, ready, asleep or absent. B's required
human decisions continue to hold. Selection changes clear queued input and readiness, reconcile
captured menus and publish a coherent authorized observation before deliberate Resume. Bind queries,
offers and responses to actor and selection generation; old A→B→A state cannot revive stale authority.
The existing reconnect proof (generation zero/one) is not an arbitrary actor-selection counter.

Grantee transport loss cannot fabricate a second owner disconnect or restart A's allowance. Restart
cannot infer live possession from permission or charge offline time. Cyclic permission pairs cannot
manufacture an active player or bypass the shared scheduler.
