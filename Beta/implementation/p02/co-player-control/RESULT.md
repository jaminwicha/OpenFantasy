# Durable co-player consent

This increment implements named permission offers, exact grantee acceptance, replacement and owner
revocation through the existing deterministic world authority. It is the first delivery boundary in
[the complete co-player control design](DESIGN.md), not active takeover or a player-facing consent UI.

## Implementation

- Explicit fresh profile39 over an unplayed profile38; bounded twelve ordered player-pair slots with
  persistent revisions, exact principal/session/actor terms and configurable capability scopes.
- Separate offer and acceptance, original-owner revocation, no onward authority, strict identity and
  chronology admission. The provisional CombatAndSurvival preset includes combat, traversal and
  recovery; it excludes goods transfer, commerce, care disposition and private history.
- Isolated nonadvancing commands and ordinary exact receipts. Reading holds, bodies, resources,
  custody, original control entitlements, controller assignments and accepted obligations remain intact.
- Explicit action22, snapshot39 and step39 encodings; previous commands and profiles retain their own
  bytes and interpreters. Ordinary copies and real coordinated archive publication preserve consent.
- Fresh-only schema11 uses the existing transaction, lease, occupied identities and journal; measured
  allowance recovery is retained. Fabricated later migration activation is refused. Opening a lived
  older save performs no profile upgrade.
- The local player adapter refuses fresh consent commands before queueing until its dedicated route
  is implemented. Authenticated exact receipt retry remains available while paused. The existing
  scheduler fallback remains intact; no host-crash prevention claim is inferred from the added guard.

## Remaining integration

original disconnect-episode binding, immutable acceptance-time authority witnesses, historical material
and paid-offer authorization, selected-character observations, readiness, protocol/UI and actual
rendered TLS handoff remain required. Connected idle humans retain their ordinary holds. Earlier
accepted work must finish exactly once when a future delegated assignment is revoked or returned.

No automatic production admission or lived profile38-to39 migration is supplied. The new managed
adapter contrast does not qualify network consent, active encounter switching or native controls.
No new process-kill, rendered, Windows permission, physical-device, human or art acceptance is claimed.
Existing full 1–4-player, campaign, content, capacity, history-latency and platform gates remain open.

the critic use Astra capacity. This unit provides no measured Claude-credit or net Astra saving claim.
