# Terrain material continuation

Explicit qualified terrain cuts preserve the complete allocation catalog, active-cell bindings,
source rights, direct raw retirement and produced ancestry across bounded material histories.
Refinement and demotion now participate as typed material operations with exact before/after evidence.
The [architecture reference](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/terrain-material-continuation.md)
describes the contract; this packet records implementation and qualification.

## Scope proved

The actual two-allocation journey extracts raw stock, crafts, consumes raw and produced items, recrafts
outputs, moves custody and refines/demotes terrain across an initial cut and two further cuts. Its 16
custody revisions and 14 cumulative Production/material operations retain exact source intervals,
all storage revisions and the surviving original output interval `[3,4)`. Imported direct raw retirement
counts source units once; recrafted ancestry does not retire them again.

The unchanged 512-operation test executes all 512 real representation operations, refuses the next
without changing its owner, cuts the actual registry and accepts that same unspent cause. Historical
causes remain unavailable. Other controls cover empty active-cell demotion, coalescing outside selected
cells, combined raw/produced lot and capacity limits, cross-allocation retirement, fixed catalog and
active-cell corruption, missing permanent identity lookup, stale plans and generic-cut laundering.

Terrain registry/state V4 archives use explicit Production V6 and require actual closing dependencies
plus opaque operation proofs. Missing/wrong proofs and rewritten/reordered/dropped operations refuse.
Actual two-project construction completion coexists with terrain representation and retains original
source batches across further cuts; each opaque evidence kind is separately required. These are
standalone component contracts, not durable world archive authentication.

## Qualification

[Machine-readable evidence](qualification/verification.json) records source/execution pins, reports,
legacy compatibility and preservation checks. The solution builds with zero warnings/errors.
Core qualification combines the 1,534 passing cases of the substantive full regression with the final
40-case registry regression after a diagnostic-string-only correction: **1,535 distinct Core cases have
passing final outcomes**, including all 21 new terrain cases. The two original full-run diagnostic failures
remain in [FAILURES.md](FAILURES.md); this is composite coverage, not an uninterrupted all-green full run.
All **331 affected Hosting cases pass** on the final build, for **1,866 distinct managed cases**
with passing final outcomes across this qualification.

The [independent review](REVIEW.md) found and fixed a mandatory-history callback bypass and examined
qualified cuts, conservation, exact representation replay, capacity, construction evidence and the
prefix-hash optimization. It ran no tests; execution remains separately recorded. The unchanged
512-operation case took 694.17 seconds before and 145.61 seconds after removal of redundant prefix
snapshot/history reconstruction. These shared-machine observations do not establish production latency.

All **24 pre-change binary fixtures** (6,492,034 bytes) reproduce exactly. The
[initial checkpoint](qualification/preservation-before.json) pins base `9d9f236`, 67 earlier milestones,
23 frozen specification files and the exact unrelated predecessor staging. The additional
[WIP checkpoint](qualification/replay-optimization-checkpoint.json) preserves owned source/tests/docs
and evidence before the replay optimization, including the known diagnostic failure. Neither checkpoint
misrepresents unfinished work as accepted.

Native runtime/test workers had disjoint ownership; Astra owns integration, execution, architecture,
Git and the completed checkpoint. The optional runtime-worker performance follow-up hit the account
usage limit; Astra handled that bounded change and the independent critic completed delta review.
External Claude failures were not automatically retried, and no credit saving is claimed.

## Remaining integration

Complete-world admission still rejects unsupported joined continuations. Terrain observation and a
material cut do not grant mining labor, excavation permission, source issuance, reserved liquid transport,
care/resource continuation, durable archive retrieval/authentication or migration of an older save.
Reserved transport and excavation need their actual closing-owner contracts before complete-world
material/care publication can retain these components together. Combined liquid/terrain ownership,
production-scale history, native gameplay, platforms, art and human acceptance remain open. All baseline
requirements and full P00/P01/P02 through P14 gates remain unchanged.
