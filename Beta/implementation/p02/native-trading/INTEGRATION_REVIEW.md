still open. It supplies production market entry, actual buy/show/sell commands and saved outcomes.

composition, participant projection, input/offer/session changes, panel, native driver, journal verifier,

boundary without claiming multiplayer timing. Frame updates do not query saved history. The existing
input release bookkeeping applies to native buttons. Paused wheel navigation can open an inspection
panel but cannot submit a world action. The market entry is a separate scene invoked by `play.py --markets`;
existing bootstrap/save selections remain intact, and mismatched saves reject without resetting.

## Remaining native and product work

These are required follow-through, not waived tests:

- Qualify controller and pointer dropdown selection and navigation rather than substituting keyboard
  rows or programmatic focus. Keep physical-device and OS-focus acceptance separate.
- Native expired/stale terms, insufficient funds and an issuer moving after review need real contrasts.
  Current managed expiry/funding checks and source guards do not prove those native cases.
- Refine player-facing copy: remove save-segment/revision/step and multiplayer-implementation explanations
  from the ordinary flow; retain meaningful price, cost, remaining time and pause consequences. Correct
  singular goods text. Review icon/focus clarity and confirm no clipped controls at supported sizes.
- Inspect the one-stall assumption when adding more merchants/commodities: explicit stall and commodity
  matching must prevent a merchant's other offer from being mislabeled or substituted.
- Full archive discovery, live-save migration, legacy merchant import, demand, travel/cargo/escort,
  multiplayer, scale, art/audio, human comprehension and all phase exits remain open.

A focused independent review/refinement assignment should challenge these contracts and close the
bounded native gaps without widening Core, changing admitted quote terms or manufacturing fast test
success. The [cargo dependency notes](../spot-exchange/CARGO_DEPENDENCIES.md) identify the fixed farm
renderer and real multi-region travel as subsequent integration dependencies; they are static findings,
not executed route/streaming proof.
