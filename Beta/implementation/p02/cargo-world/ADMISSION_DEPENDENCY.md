The obstacle is a Host composition boundary, not evidence that the portable world model inherently
requires a single region. LocalSpatialSnapshot supports bounded multiple regions, and
WorldMarketAdmission already derives a fresh market world from an admitted paid genesis.

The narrow candidate repair is to compose new residents and regions **before** the existing terrain,
liquid, encounter, care and coordinated-history owners bind the complete fresh world:

1. EncounterJourneyAdmission already has an internal `composeFreshFarm` callback after its two wolves
   and before BreachFarmBuilder. CareJourneyAdmission consumes that callback for CareFarmBuilder but
   does not expose a subsequent fresh composition hook. RestorativeJourneyAdmission hides its final
   ordinary-care admission behind a private method. A bounded internal composition path through these
   Host builders could retain their existing meanings and eliminate a new parallel admission chain.
2. BreachFarmBuilder.CreateSpace calls `Regions.Single()`, excavates its authored upper region and
   adds its lower receiver region. Its local terrain edit needs to identify its own exact region and
   carry unrelated admitted regions intact, while rejecting missing coverage, overlaps and too many
   resulting regions. The material palette must come from that region, not incidental list order.
3. TerrainLiquidTopology.Create already receives the complete region set. Encounter lifecycle also
   binds all residents and checks shared terrain seed interpretation. These owners must still perform
   their normal validation; the repair must not fabricate their outputs or bypass them.

This is a proposed seam, not a proved fix. A next assignment needs explicit ownership of the affected
existing Host builders. It must preserve old genesis bytes and old tests, prove added-region/body/
custody/progression binding, and execute the actual cargo admission before claiming the dependency
resolved. CareFarmBuilder identifies the helper through the quest giver and changes that helper's
placement in its own fresh content transform; compose source-positioned merchants afterwards.

The worker's proposed 14 surface regions plus the breach receiver appear to fit the 16-region limit,
but final admission, serialized size, occupancy and route throughput remain unmeasured. The native
movement sample is 32 subunits: a 461-cell journey needs many real cutoffs. Keep that workload and
the actual original merchant coordinates. A passing local leg test cannot establish complete-world
travel, persistence, performance or a cargo-circuit result. If the full workload fails, retain the
failure and improve the relevant implementation without relaxing the gate.
