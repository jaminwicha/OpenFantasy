# Fixed reservoir containment and the wet-world integration boundary

## Purpose and retained scope

The P01/P02 plan requires conserved water in actual terrain and construction, followed by flowing
materials, breaches, occupied flooded places, phase changes and ecology. The existing reservoir owners
conserve material and receiving capacity. Their nominal vessel geometry does not establish real walls.
The boundary evaluator can inspect walls, but an external diagnostic policy cannot constrain a saved
world. This increment makes complete geometric containment an authoritative, retained invariant.
It advances the existing reservoir owner rather than adding another material ledger or simulation process.
The core and application remain C#, with Godot receiving permitted projections.

The complete game obligations in the frozen plan remain unchanged. Fixed contained vessels establish
neither hydraulic strength nor complete wet/structural composition. The next dependencies below are
required continuation work, not optional features or a substitute definition of completion.

## Evidence determining the boundary

The existing adjacent-column transport gate has no finite shutter or channel material. A positive-
thickness shutter on the common face of two complete one-metre interiors intrudes into at least one
nominal volume. The existing boundary evaluator correctly rejects this intrusion. Removing its
`UnprovedGate` result cannot turn that authored permission into an operable physical gate.

The evaluator also uses original project origins and authored member boxes. Those are exact for fixed
construction, but cannot represent a displaced physical member. Current physical construction permits
intact downward settlement; calling an upward/sliding shutter `Resting` would bypass that interpretation.
Structural admission represents carried and installed loads but has no retained stationary fluid-load
owner. Its action preflight can project the next calendar while transport still owns the current boundary.
These are explicit integration requirements; merely removing the dry/wet constructor guard is invalid.

The nominal interior rule reserves the complete capacity envelope, including its dry upper portion.
It rejects body clearance inside that envelope. Body clearance is not an exact displaced-fluid volume,
and this reservation rule is not the future swimming, drowning, buoyancy or immersion interpretation.
