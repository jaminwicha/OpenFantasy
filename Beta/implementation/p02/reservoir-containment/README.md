# Durable fixed reservoir containment

This increment makes actual geometric containment a retained world invariant. Fresh profile nineteen
owns its complete sealing policy alongside conserved reservoir stock, terrain and construction. Every
admitted successor must retain closed nominal interiors, floors and walls under exact admitted material
pins. Independent diagnostics can use other policies but cannot authorize publication under them.

The [design and continuation contract](DESIGN.md) records why existing nominal transport gates cannot
simply be combined with dry construction. The [architecture reference](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/reservoir-containment.md)
describes the implemented ownership, action and persistence contracts. The core and application remain C#;
no native kernel, IPC worker or additional material authority is introduced.

## Implementation and meaningful behavior

- Fresh admission retains the complete profile-eight basis and pins the prior admission plus the full
  sealing policy. Profiles one through eighteen retain their interpretation; existing saves are not upgraded.
- A direct eighteen-field root decodes construction before spatial admission and retains the existing
  16 MiB limit and sole schema-three writer. Decode reruns complete material and geometric admission.
- Actions join reconciled inventory, Production and reservoirs before containment checks. Selected work
  uses the correct eventual calendar boundary. Failed actions restore their components and identities;
  independently accepted time and movement continue according to existing rules.
- Policy admission requires every liquid and solid content pin to be already admitted, even unused rules.
  Missing walls/floors/regions, intruding solids/bodies, wrong versions and incomplete finite work refuse.
- The ordinary Host creates, executes and inspects the new profile. This is an authority increment;
  neither the default farm nor the worksite silently gains rendered water.

## Limits and full-plan continuity

The fixture's initial basin holes are explicitly authored geography. Water stock is conserved; fabrication and labor
execute through real authority actions; this does not claim earned mining of those initial holes.
The whole nominal envelope, including dry upper space, is reserved against body occupation. It is not
an immersion or displacement model. Fixed construction geometry does not follow moved or damaged members.

This profile therefore excludes transport, excavation and physical/structural consequence owners.
Finite shutters/ports and actuation, actual fluid mass/support, conserved breaches/spills, occupied wet
space, flowing/reaction/phase behavior and playable water remain required integration work in the
[continuation sequence](DESIGN.md#required-continuation-toward-the-full-fluid-specification).
Whole-scene geometric work, full-journal recovery and production workloads remain scale obligations.
No P00/P01/P02, LANG, art, UX, networking, export, device or full-game gate is closed by this checkpoint.

Frozen specs, earlier evidence and unrelated staged predecessor work are preserved. Project-specific
contracts stay here and in the timeless reference; existing ecosystem diagnostic/authority and
risk-based verification doctrine applies without a new generic skill or process layer.
