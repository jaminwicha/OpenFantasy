Core and application remain C#. This record distinguishes source critique, execution evidence and

## Independent source critique

The bounded native critic reviewed complete root admission, lifecycle processing, fractional process
boundaries, permanent debt, ordinary actor eligibility and compatibility with profiles22/23.

- **Caller-selected randomness:** the first live-entry draft accepted an arbitrary new encounter ID,
  which also keyed damage randomness. The correction derives episode identity from immutable scope,
  seed, scenario ID/version and committed episode ordinal. Entry rejects a different supplied ID.
  Unrelated clock ticks and command/cause IDs cannot reroll the same next encounter. The content's
  stats, ability costs and damage rules are unchanged.
- **Unenterable exploration admission:** an extreme but otherwise valid speed could enter profile24
  exploration and overflow `Speed * 10` only at entry. Complete lifecycle admission now checks every
  resident, including inactive opponents and bystanders, for positive representable normalized speed.
  Focused controls exercise three actor roles and the maximum legal speed.

The source review found no additional consequential issue in retained debt/carry, inactive action
dispatch, incapacity or the old-profile branches. It does not substitute for execution or prove
general wet/structural hazard closure, crowd scale or native presentation.

A follow-up session/storage review confirmed both fixes and caught a deterministic-ID poisoning
case: a refused ordinary command could occupy the exposed next episode ID. The correction reserves
an entire per-scenario64-bit identity family, including future ordinals, against ordinary causes,
commands, results and spawned entities. Only an authenticated entry may claim its exact next episode
ID, and actual admitted/historical collisions remain forbidden. The complete current root protects
prospective names; genesis/entity membership and committed history remain separate checks. Targeted
poisoning regressions supplement the repeated lifecycle journey. A further admission check applies
this disjointness to cached terrain and geography identities as well as dynamic roles. Static layers
lazily cache their identity prefixes; unchanged roots do not rescan terrain cells. Two public-world
controls replace only an unused terrain material pin, prove the alternate fresh profile22 is valid,
and require lifecycle admission to reject both next and far-future family collisions.
