## Contracts

- Each selected call captures exact port/home/operated actor/generation/witness, operation, subject and
  world/branch. Admission, serialized execution and publication require current selection and relevant
  scope. The immutable result carries the original context; applying it requires an exact current match,
  including A-to-home-to-A. A published authorized read cannot be retroactively unlearned.
- Trade allows nonbinding inquiries and open quote inspection. Inventory allows open proposal inspection.
  PrivateHistory independently permits terminal participant quote/proposal history; it supplies no open
  offers or other inventory/trade authority. Home reads retain permanent entitlement. Existing unbound
  read APIs always remain home reads; generation-bound mutation does not silently retarget them.
- Durable authorization precedes subject lookup under the sole authority gate. Typed selected readers
  enforce participant and open/terminal separation; caller projection cannot accidentally expose closed
  history. Terminal retrieval retains complete replay, pinned head and quote publishing-root provenance.
  No schema/profile/canonical byte change, cache, automatic retry or extra authority is introduced.
- Legacy and selected inspection share existing bounded per-kind slots. Keys include selection, operation
  and subject. Identical running requests share work; different keys are busy. Transitions invalidate
  results without abandoning tracked workers or creating extra replay capacity. Shutdown joins admitted
  work before disposing storage. Expected stale/revoked/cancelled input is not a host failure.
- Human inquiries belong to exact port/selection ownership as well as shopper actor. Switching, losing or
  releasing control cancels only that owner's outgoing inquiry; one actor still has at most one waiting
  inquiry. Existing merchant authority, FIFO service, world-time expiry and immutable quote life remain.
