an unoccupied lower trench. Actual terrain determines which nominal cells have capacity. Intact cells
have none. Every open column has a known empty spill-plane cell and a direct vertical vent; roofs,
unknown terrain and unsealed walls/floors refuse. Actors and installed members remain outside the full
potential envelope and its declared vents. This establishes neither pressure nor swimming, displacement,
fluid loads, sealed chambers, structural settlement or physical gates.

three work actions. Partial work preserves the solid divider. The final action publishes the original
finite substrate interval, terrain removal, new topology, labor and receipt atomically. Before spending partial
labor, the planner proves that the eventual complete opening remains admissible. Existing conservative
flow then moves actual retained water one neighbor at a time; no material is created by the new void.
An exterior opening that loses a floor or wall is refused, not converted to disappearing spill.
