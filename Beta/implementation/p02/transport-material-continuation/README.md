# Reserved transport across material boundaries

Explicit transport-owner cuts preserve funded source reservations, incoming capacity, the complete
stage journal, geometry, gates and processed time while continuing material history. A typed reserved
delivery records exact custody changes with a cause distinct from the previously scheduled stage.
The [architecture reference](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/transport-material-continuation.md)
describes the timeless contract; this packet records implementation and qualification.

## Scope

The mixed journey crafts, consumes, recrafts, waits through a reading hold and delivers a real split
water commitment across three material cuts. It preserves exact source/output intervals, eight custody
and material revisions, all three storage clocks, original producer ancestry and the terminal stage.
Other controls retain completed, canceled and blocked journal facts without inventing retrospective
material operations. Actual produced water contributes to physical head; consuming one unit can change
a valid delivery into a blocked one.

A multi-route candidate can fail head after preparing fragments without publishing any of them,
spending its cause or consuming shared gate throughput. A later independent stage on that same gate
can succeed. After actual receiver consumption changes the head, the originally blocked split stage
can deliver with its original funding. Identity collisions retain the entire obligation.

The actual boundary test performs 511 real transfers, accepts one delivery as operation 512, blocks
the next, cuts the complete owner and proves that repeating the same time grants no new allowance.
The next actual step can deliver. This leaves the 512-operation and finite stage-journal bounds intact.
Initial and subsequent cuts change representation, never quantities, stage outcomes or time.

Transport V3 nests explicit liquid/runoff/capacity V4 with Production V7. Actual complete-owner
dependencies and opaque delivery evidence are required. Tests distinguish absent proof lookup from
substituting a genuine proof with a different produced-history prefix. Structurally legal legacy
rewrites of outcome, gate allowance, revision and time refuse under the qualified new interpretation.
Actual construction completion and dry transport preserve both distinct evidence kinds across cuts;
wet transport and raw/produced head have separate executed controls.
