# Durable excavation consequences

This increment implements a retained consequence owner for the existing harmless intact-settlement
interpretation. It does not close broader collapse, performance, presentation or full-phase gates.
The [architecture reference](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/consequence-continuation.md) describes the
runtime contract; qualification below records this increment's evidence.

## Behavior and design refinement

Fresh profile sixteen retains an immutable query budget independently of its optional pending operation.
An eligible, isolated excavation reserves its advancing interval and exact command at trigger. Published
terrain, material, physical members, actors, effort and clocks remain unchanged while actual geometric
queries progress. The original terminal receipt and all accepted consequences publish together, spending
one selected interval. Refusal also spends that interval, with normal recovery but no excavation debit.

The prior [integration map](../structural-settlement/CONTINUATION_DESIGN.md) proposed spending time at trigger.
The reviewed refinement defers that charge until terminal publication. This preserves capped effort and
calendar/gathering/exposure bindings without inverse calculations or a duplicate basis snapshot. Earlier
profiles keep their existing interpretation and byte formats.

Real base-contact, member/rider sweep and actor-landing work is resumable. At most 192 geometric units are
admitted. Prefix archives bind complete geometry inputs and reconstruct exactly the retained progress;
forward continuation consumes retained calculations. Setup, hashing, restoration and complete root/load
admission are explicitly not production CPU bounds. Final publication consumes the completed calculation,
not a delayed rerun of the synchronous resolver.

The pending owner pins the entire stable job-free world at its captured revision. It temporarily reserves
all possible finite terminal result IDs and the potential source ID. Trigger/continuation causes cannot
steal those IDs. Original Command is recorded once; the actual Result/Spawn are recorded only at completion.
The host preflights enough unchanged journal capacity to complete the bounded job. Schema four reserves
commit kind four for empty continuation; older schemas and migration kind three remain preserved.

The session selects singleton excavation before NPC actions or movement, resumes a saved job before client
readiness, rejects unrelated controls while pending and attaches authenticated exact retries. Stopping
cancels waiting only. Read-only opening and history never advance saved work. Resolving is a distinct public
view/run-state cue; the pack and action wheel have a corresponding status label.

## Remaining obligations

The physical boundary remains one isolated excavation, one uniformly descending intact assembly, harmless
member/body thresholds and known rated terrain. Multiple moving assemblies and a complete affected-neighbor
closure, surviving/deforming joints, harmful impacts/injuries, fracture/debris conservation, long flight,
cross-assembly support and wet displacement remain open. General asynchronous world work, long-lived
checkpoint-tail recovery, production throughput, 1–4-player timing, exports/devices, art/audio/human acceptance
and the full P00–P14 campaign are not closed by this increment. The playable farm is not replaced or
visually upgraded by this backend qualification.
