# Shared physical carriage boundary

`WorldActorCarriage` extracts the real physical material selection from `StructuralMassCatalog` into
an immutable Core projection. The structural mass reader uses it directly. This is a retained
preparation boundary for partition ownership; no storage format, persistent owner, movement permission
or second authority is introduced. Full PLAN-STATE remains open.

## Behavior and proof

Primary inventory follows its actor. In structurally admitted worlds, explicitly builder-carried
project stock follows the builder too. Installed output backing belongs to the assembly, while
uninstalled byproducts in the same custodian remain carried. Equipped and reserved goods retain their
mass. Legal access ownership alone never adds a storage to the physical view. Older profiles retain
their primary-inventory-only physical interpretation. The new view captures exact immutable lot
objects with their original identity/provenance, stores no complete root and cannot mutate custody.

Two new actual-world tests prove:

1. Ordinary committed movement crosses a cell boundary with the same complete inventory bytes. A
   subsequent real gift splits the six-unit source into two delivered and four retained units, with
   contiguous exact source intervals and unchanged earlier captured views. Original-genesis reopening
   reproduces the final world and both holdings. A cell crossing is **not** a partition transfer.
2. Ordinary quest funding puts six reserved wood units in builder-carried project custody. Actual NPC
   construction in the existing structural stair fixture installs the assembly; three unreserved
   byproducts remain carried, and the installed backing is excluded. The builder loses exactly
   270,000 mg of load, corresponding to the installed assembly. Original-genesis reopening retains
   exact final state and carried output identities/intervals. The fixture's single-unit construction
   and authored ratings are a diagnostic, not production balance or the 200-unit adventure workload.

The existing unlocated-custody regression now also checks that a legally owned spare storage does not
become carried through this view. Structural admission continues to refuse its unlocated live stock.
Existing tests cover legacy project-stock semantics, equipped/reserved material, missing/conflicting
mass calibrations, support, load propagation, failure and physical boundary behavior.

[Focused runs](check-01): 33 Core cases and four Hosting cases pass, no failures/skips. The broader
[structural run](check-02/process.json) passes all 96 cases in that family. The 33 are included in the96,
so the total is **100 distinct passing cases**, not133. Invocation times are10.649,22.567 and2.421
seconds, each under its180-second bound. Builds report no warnings/errors. The wider family is justified
by the shared mass reader's use in bearing and descent/failure calculations. No unrelated suite or
rendered journey was repeated, and no timing/scale improvement is claimed.

The view reports its world/branch/revision for context; these are not an authorization token or proof
that a later candidate still matches. A consuming action must prepare again from its current admitted
dependencies. Capture still scans the complete supplied world's live lots. It does not supply bounded
partial residency, an ownership directory, offscreen scheduling, atomic cross-owner publication or
partition-aware history. Those are the next integrated prototype obligations; this extraction cannot
stand in for them or satisfy LANG/city/frontier gates.
