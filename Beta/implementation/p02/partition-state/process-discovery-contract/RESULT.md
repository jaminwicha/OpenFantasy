## Findings that change implementation preparation

1. Discovery from a pre-action directory alone is insufficient. `DecideImmediate` runs actions before
   expiry, transport, gathering/exposure and liquids; it passes their changed tentative owners to those
   operations. A discovery adapter must bind the effective owner versions and downstream dependencies.
2. Evaluated blocking is not skipped mandatory work. Transport records head/material/throughput blocking
   while advancing its boundary and preserving original reserved material/stage identity. A scheduler
   that waits for all stages to succeed would alter this law. Missing dependencies still abort; a lawful
   blocked result is never a substitute for missing evaluation.
3. There is no single interchangeable due counter. Proposal/quote expiry uses advancing count, gathering
   uses process intervals where pacing exists, transport uses its exact calendar boundary, liquids use
   their physical process boundary, and residents receive actual exposure. Encounter choices and
   lifecycle/care/control boundaries are not ordinary exploration ticks. Tactical accumulation can earn
   exposure without yet earning another full gathering/liquid interval.
4. A general index needs proof of owner membership, including owners with empty due sets, plus refresh
   after action/process changes. StoredConstructionInterval remains a known captured legacy cohort,
   not such a general persisted index. Reserved excavation and unpublished read handles retain distinct
   durability contracts.
