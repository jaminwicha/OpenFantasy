## Decision and next work

Keep the typed stable-owner/admission-cohort recommendation conditional. The results reject a blanket
speed/space argument for the current fine-grained representation; the coarse candidate remains a real
alternative/reference for small cohorts. The complete-root path also stays the historical interpreter.
Storage granularity and rule admission remain separate; returning to one global world root would not
meet the frontier/residency design. Default current40/41 gameplay is unchanged.

Potential costs already visible in source include per-boundary owner decoding, complete directory
rebuilding, per-actor exposure writes, archive accounting and full replay verification. This experiment
measures their combined effect, not their individual attribution. Any optimization needs a bounded
profile and the same equivalence/recovery checks; do not add a cache, second authority or generic
facet rewrite based solely on these totals. Final admission and replay costs must remain visible.

The combined due-boundary and real noninitial-checkpoint proofs retain their own scope. General
oldest-due age is unavailable without authoritative multi-domain obligation/membership binding; it is
not reported as zero. Continue the existing live scheduling/due-discovery and remaining measurement/
review exits before permanent NPC persistence. Cold/platform/scale, richer-profile migration,
independent/human acceptance and the full P02 table remain open. No new scope or phase is removed.
