# Native encounter controls and presentation

This increment connects the retained profile24/schema6 lifecycle to Godot. The source-backed world,
authenticated commands, deterministic damage, permanent recovery ledger and SQLite contracts remain
unchanged. It adds no authority, native library, custom IPC bridge or alternative time law.

## Playable contract

- An explicit `--encounters` launch selects its own retained save. Existing farm/worksite saves are
  preserved. Wren is selected through the admitted quest-giver role rather than a unique-host assumption.
- Select a visible wolf with target cycling, then inspect/submit its entry. Staging uses actual positions.
  In combat the context offers the player's granted strike and costed Wait, then a legal outcome release.
  Existing keyboard/controller intentions and the four-sector wheel remain the input owners.
- Public host projections evaluate the player's own ability/target combinations through actual legality.
  Costs, recovery, mana, side, reach, incapacity, queued choice and outcome restrictions remain truthful.
  They do not expose another actor's grants, RNG or root snapshots. The local profile is sight-complete;
  this is not a general visibility implementation.
- Offered intent, actor revision, target and encounter timing are captured. A changed wheel choice needs
  a fresh deliberate selection, without silent substitution or automatic retry. Host admission remains
  authoritative. An applied ability receipt says queued; visible HP changes drive hit feedback.
- Required-choice Wait holds time without revoking host readiness. Pack/wheel inspection does not pause
  by itself. Explicit pause/focus loss retains normal readiness behavior. Movement and ordinary farm
  shortcuts cannot leak through encounters; held directions require release before walking resumes.
- Wolf artwork is a byte-exact copy of the original retained 48×96 idle/walk sheet. Visible damage is
  restrained tint/shake plus the actual observed HP decrement. Incapacitated bodies remain present and
  dim after release. Reopening establishes a new visual baseline without replaying past damage.

## Required evidence

Actual injected native keyboard/controller/pointer journey: enter in place; hold required choice;
block movement/farming; inspect/cancel Wait through focus changes; inspect inventory; confirm a strike;
close and reopen midway through the same duel; reach its real source-backed outcome; release; reopen
with exact condition retained. Use isolated disposable saves, strict success deadlines and background
focus binding. Render representative states and inspect readability/viewport fit separately. Run
focused managed public-offer/input/recovery regressions and ordinary native controls after integration.
Retain failures and critic dispositions. No threshold changes, edited source stats or manufactured wins.

## Remaining scope

This is the first native binding of the existing finite physical confrontation. Full ability browsing,
healing, flee/rescue, rewards/death, richer choreography, authored battle animation/sound, independently
controlled co-op, broader encounter dependency closure, production art, physical devices, accessibility,
platform exports, scale and the full campaign remain their existing obligations. A defeated initiating
player currently cannot release; the UI must communicate this limit instead of inventing recovery.
