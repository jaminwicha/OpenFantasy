# General incapacitated assistance

Continue the full recovery dependency from the preceding checkpoint. The independent design review
confirmed that ordinary material/resource transactions can serve this law, provided definition,
permission, witness, historical resource admission and serialized interpretation all change explicitly.

## Decision and owners

Fresh profile 37 retains the controller/outcome/market world and adds an immutable
`WorldRecoveryPolicy` pin. `WorldRecoveryAdmission.CreateFresh` accepts only an unplayed profile 36
basis and explicitly authored incapacitated-assistance definitions and permissions. It preserves all
existing catalogs and grants; no existing agreement expands its scope. Older roots retain their laws.

`RestorativeDeliveryMode.IncapacitatedAssistance` requires current canonical HP 0, an exact supported
corporeal form and a distinct controlled helper. Current health remains in progression; injury and
anatomy remain in body condition. The helper must be capable, ready, in real reach and own the selected
finite dose. A separately pinned, active patient permission names that helper and definition. Receiving
aid does not require the patient's manipulation or readiness. This is HP restoration, not resurrection,
injury repair, teleportation, custody transfer or a safety cooldown.

Reuse `UseRestorativeItemIntent`, `WorldRestorativeActions`, `WorldRestorativeResolver` and
`RestorativeItemPlanner`. Publication joins the exact dose consumption, use witness, capped resource
effect, 1,800-ms ordinary exploration interval and durable receipt. Supplier work excludes movement;
ordinary clock/process/debt rules continue. No last-encounter or lethal-decision witness is required.

Care V4 supports inline and continued recovery catalogs. The old use constructor and Care V1–V3 keep
their earlier legality/bytes. Recovery uses have an explicit zero-before construction path and are
validated against the exact recovery definition. Ordinary resource evidence authenticates the actual
historical HP boundary. Coordinated archives preserve permissions, revocations, chronology and identity
occupation; they cannot reset eligibility or create supply. Profile 37 has explicit root/step dispatch
and retains schema-nine controller journals. Opening a lived save is not migration.

At the host, automatic HP-0 acknowledgment is allowed only under the new recovery interpretation and
the existing autonomous/ready/occupation guards. It disposes of the encounter without spending or
healing anything. Host helper aid can select the new policy through the same supplier entitlement and
preflight. Explicit player helpers use the same authority law; automatic selection does not conscript
them. Client interpretation must explain that completion does not guarantee rescue.

## Qualification

Prove real attack → incapacity → disconnect/expiry → encounter completion with unchanged body →
ordinary helper travel and finite supply → actual permitted treatment → save/reopen/exact receipt.
Prove another real encounter and an actual coordinated cut can precede treatment; use an authored
scenario with the incapacitated patient on side zero rather than weakening encounter entry.
Contrast revoked/mismatched permissions, missing/stale doses, out-of-reach/incapable/unready helpers,
legacy profile refusal, version/pin corruption and unsupported forms. Preserve all earlier tests and
specs; serialize heavy checks and review the resulting code independently.

## Required following dependency

An old unmatched aftermath acknowledgment still blocks completion. Do not drop or pretend to treat
it. Add an explicit authenticated patient defer/cancel or helper decline operation with a retained
terminal disposition, then version archive totals and a bounded closed-defeat frontier. The original
equality between archived acknowledgments and treatments cannot represent cancellation. A later
recovery command must remain separate from that cancelled request. Until this, restart/co-player,
client control-return and native 1–4-player policy qualification pass, production selection stays closed.

Lived-save migration, death/spirits/estates, broader medicine, active-combat delivery, rescue pathfinding,
production balance and every full-campaign gate remain in their required scope. This increment builds
the general recovery law; it does not redefine full survival as currently available bedside aid.
