# General recovery checkpoint

Qualified scope: an explicitly admitted incapacitated character can receive separately authorized
ordinary assistance after encounter completion, using actual helper work and finite medicine.
Completion changes no HP, body, inventory or readiness. This is a bounded domain/host checkpoint;
the managed Server aggregate is not all green and the full survival/disconnect policy remains open.

## Behavior and architecture

Fresh profile 37 pins `WorldRecoveryPolicy`, retains existing catalogs and grants, and adds explicit
incapacitated-assistance definitions and patient/helper permissions. A supported corporeal patient
must currently have zero HP; the distinct helper must be capable, ready, entitled, within reach and
own the selected dose. Living assistance and aftermath agreements do not silently expand permission.
The ordinary transaction publishes consumption, a zero-before use witness, capped resource restoration,
work/time and the original durable receipt. No latest encounter or lethal-decision archive is required.

Care V4 and explicit root/step dispatch preserve the new interpretation through real coordinated
cuts and schema-nine journals. Old constructors, formats and worlds retain their earlier rules.
Autonomous acknowledgment is separate from treatment and requires a gameplay-ready port. The helper
selector submits under its own authority and respects occupied commands and reserved participants.

Observation body version 3 discloses recovery policy without claiming an individual permission or
guaranteed rescue. Offered aftermath care still warns that accepting declines it. Current HP controls
movement/action prompts even when a later encounter replaces the old outcome projection. These
presentation corrections are source-reviewed, compiled and text-tested; they have no new render or
human acceptance. The maintained [architecture](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/general-recovery.md)
and dependent references are updated.

Regression testing also exposed a real remote-query cleanup race. `RemotePlayerSession` now tracks
and joins the exact typed caller task, including result projection. The unchanged paid-quote regression
and new other-query/invalid-call tests pass. This repair does not explain the independent timing failures.
