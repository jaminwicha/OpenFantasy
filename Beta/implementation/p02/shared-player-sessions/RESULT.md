# Shared player host

Qualified bounded increment, preserved by `codex/milestone-shared-player-sessions-20261002`.
Work remains on `codex/runtime-foundation`. This milestone does not close a full phase or campaign.

One `LocalWorldSession` now admits up to four authenticated in-process `LocalPlayerConnection` ports.
Each owns its input lease, pending action, readiness, participant view and inspection slots. One host
owns the world clock, authority, autonomous controllers and serialized publication. The solo client
uses the original port. Core, persisted profiles/schemas, content and original test files are unchanged.

Ordinary requests receive rotating service. Ready movement joins one advancing step; simultaneous
combat choices share their captured timing boundary. Required-human Wait and outcome reading remain
individual obligations. Zero ready ports pause ordinary work; a ready peer can continue while another
is paused. Closing a port cancels only its uncommitted input and transient inquiry. Its resident remains
subject to lawful world threats, and the authority stays alive. Reconnection requires a fresh trusted
binding and grants no time, automatic decision, private access or replayed action.

Ready current-episode patient care precedes observer traffic and outcome completion. After acceptance,
the original patient's matching stale offer can receive one separate durable rejection while the world
revision still equals the acknowledgment sequence. That receipt closes the window across reopening;
subsequent requests cannot starve treatment. Failed preflight cannot gain a deferral. Core owns action
legality; rejected unrelated lifecycle/care traffic cannot consume extra world holds. The existing
initiator's premature release retains its own tested Confrontation/Staging boundary.

The [architecture reference](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/hosted-player-connections.md) describes
ownership, scheduling, queries and recovery. Nine added cases use actual ports to prove the shared clock,
private views/inquiries, trusted controller checks, four-port admission, fairness, simultaneous decisions,
independent closure, care priority, durable reopening and lawful hostile entry after the target leaves.
The shared-care contrast covers both uninterrupted and reopened worlds with competing observer traffic.
