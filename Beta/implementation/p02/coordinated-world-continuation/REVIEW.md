implementation is not its own independent review. Neither critic executed checks; retained commands,

- Continued world crafting omitted permanent historical identity lookup. The adapter now supplies it.
- Ordinary and aftermath care passed the already-reserved current command to the continued planner as
  occupied. Exclude only that command; every prior identity and derived output remains checked.
- Imported live producer witnesses were missing from the complete world role index, breaking installed
  construction after a cut. Index witnesses, retained transformed lots and original construction
  ingredient roles while retaining actor/custodian/source/recipe checks.
- A joint closing world revision can be later than the last care operation. New acknowledgments,
  treatments, uses and revocations must follow the complete closing revision. Only exact carried pending
  care and already-recorded revocations survive at earlier times.
- Retain exact encounter ability/scenario catalogs through the joint anchor, not merely monotonic counts.

The follow-up source review confirms those causes are addressed. It also caught an invalid chronology
test positive control: world revision advanced without lifecycle revision. The test now advances the
lifecycle consistently and checks the intended rejection message.
