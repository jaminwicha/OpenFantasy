# Recovery dependency exposed by autonomous control

This is a required implementation dependency, not a reduction of the full design or an instruction to
automatically accept defeat. Profile 36 production remains closed until its selected disconnect policy
has a complete admitted continuation for incapacitated participants.

## Evidence and contradiction

The full design permits conservative survival control after a confirmed disconnect allowance. The
existing profile-35/36 outcome law holds ordinary time, movement, replenishment and trading while
waiting for every positive-side human character's acknowledgment. Exact-defeat aftermath care requires
an already capable, in-reach neutral helper and owned remedy. A pending unavailable treatment has no
expiry or reassignment. Completion permanently closes this aftermath opportunity, and ordinary
restorative use refuses HP 0. HP 0 is incapacity, not biological death.

Consequently, neither “always accept after disconnect” nor “wait for a helper to arrive” implements
the intended survival behavior. The first imposes an undeclared terminal disposition; the second waits
for movement/time the current outcome law prohibits. The implemented bounded controller requests
currently lawful care and acknowledges living characters only, honestly retaining this gap.

## Required resolution

Separate encounter disposition from general treatment/recovery, consistent with the world-composition
contract. A character's health and incapacity remain owned by the progression/body owners; healing
permission, a particular request, helper commitment, actual finite treatment, recovery costs and
encounter acknowledgment remain separate facts. A rescue/recovery operation must work through the
same authority inside its supported encounter boundary and after encounter completion. It must not
depend on the latest encounter archive staying resident forever or on the initiator surviving.

Before implementation, identify the exact reusable restorative operations and additional domain facts
needed by a real contrasting recovery case. Prefer the admitted current condition plus explicit
permission and supported treatment effect over copying HP into a second owner. Retain injury, position,
custody, readiness debt and history; neither release nor reconnection may revive or teleport anyone.
Living and incapacitated recipients can share resource/effect machinery where semantics actually match,
without erasing their distinct eligibility and consent requirements. NPCs and players need the same law.

The policy must also resolve already committed treatment obligations: continuation, explicit failure,
or authorized reassignment/cancellation with retained costs and receipts. It must not silently expire
them because a timer or provider changed. Define an admitted way for an encounter to finish without
foreclosing rescue, then qualify that disposition before enabling autonomous acknowledgment at HP 0.
Preserve earlier world profiles, canonical bytes and recorded terminal-completion meaning; any changed
law requires explicit versioning and a deliberate retained-save migration policy.

## Required vertical proof

- A real attack incapacitates a character, transport loss and allowance expiry occur, and a ready
  co-player's world can continue through the qualified encounter disposition without erasing the body.
- Available pre-agreed care consumes a real dose once; initially unavailable rescue becomes possible
  through real travel/supply/work under a valid simulation clock and explicit control entitlement.
- Permission denial, absent supplies and failed/occupied helpers have explicit outcomes; no free
  revival, repeated consumption, silent mandate or permanently invisible obligation.
- Completion, another encounter, disconnect/rejoin, archival rollover and actual save reopening retain
  the recovery opportunity, injury, ownership, original receipts and deterministic history.
- Human Wait remains unlimited; automatic choices require the admitted controller policy. Native
  presentation explains incapacity, recovery and control availability without claiming personal reading
  or consent that the person did not supply. Real 1–4-player journeys prove the whole continuation.

General death, resurrection, spirits, estates and long-term injury remain in their full campaign phases.
This dependency is about executable incapacity/recovery and cannot claim those larger systems complete.
