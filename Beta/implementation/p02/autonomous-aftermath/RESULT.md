# Autonomous aftermath checkpoint

Qualified scope: disconnected Autonomous human characters can request already permitted, currently
lawful aftermath care and acknowledge an outcome while alive. Treatment and encounter completion
remain separate committed operations. This does not complete the survival/disconnect policy.

## Implemented behavior

The host selects stable command identities through the ordinary preview/commit boundary, preserving
the character's controller binding, current episode and captured action terms. A gameplay-ready port
is required. Human and allowance-period choices remain human-owned; returning control is reconciled
before autonomous selection. Occupied care identities are checked before current helper eligibility,
preventing a failed or committed choice from becoming an automatic different-agreement retry.

Care requires the existing prior agreement, exact incapacitating decision, an eligible host-controlled
helper, reach and an owned finite dose. Patient acknowledgment spends no remedy; the later helper
transaction consumes the actual dose, pays readiness and resumes the same encounter. A player-bound
helper is not conscripted. Pending human commands keep their own identity and eventual receipt;
an obsolete outcome request after treatment receives its own durable Unavailable refusal.

Only living Autonomous participants acknowledge an outcome automatically. Incapacitated participants
without lawful available care remain unresolved. The independent investigation exposed why a broader
rescue law is necessary: held outcomes prevent travel and replenishment, while present completion
forecloses aftermath care. [Recovery requirements](RECOVERY_GAP.md) specify the next domain dependency.
No free healing, teleportation, deletion or automatic terminal disposition masks this gap.

Controller-world views use neutral character acknowledgment wording, including after human rejoin.
They do not claim that a person read or requested something. Observation body version 2 explicitly
carries that interpretation; legacy HumanReading retains its default-omitted marker and version-1
bytes. Strict decode rejects mismatched versions/markers. Actual delta/replica tests cross both version
transitions. This is not old-client compatibility negotiation or production profile-36 acceptance.
