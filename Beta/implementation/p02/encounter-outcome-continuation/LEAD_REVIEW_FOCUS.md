# Integration review focus

These are questions for the delivered implementation, not findings or acceptance claims.

- Distinguish durable outcome acknowledgment from connection/readiness. A recorded acknowledgment
  must not impersonate another required human, and a controller's incapacity must not prevent reading.
  Inspect the actual two-controller cutoff path; a standalone list test does not establish hosted
  readiness or network support. Ending combat at zero duration need not itself advance world time.
- Require a precise zero-survivor proof. Current sequential single-target combat may end before the
  last living side can also fall. Label an admitted fresh or standalone calibration honestly; do not
  manufacture played history to make it appear reachable. The completion rule must still accommodate
  the state without requiring a living releaser or falsely claiming a demonstrated live encounter.
- Follow care through the actual resource/history boundary. Older treatment resumes its original
  encounter. The new completed-outcome interpretation must explicitly reconcile care permission,
  pending acknowledgment, dose ownership, current HP and episode history; a new view token alone
  cannot make the existing processor legal. No automatic immunity or free movement may hide reentry.
- Inspect save-and-leave in the real native flow: orderly persistence, no fresh admission over the
  old save, exact reopen and an accurate unavailable-rescue explanation. Keep full biological death,
  succession and resurrection distinct from this profile's HP-zero incapacity.

Use the actual delivery diff and evidence to resolve these questions. Preserve the accepted old
profile behavior and complete the independent critic after a coherent delivered checkpoint.
