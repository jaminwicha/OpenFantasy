## Implemented behavior

- Exact portions exhaust each mapped custodian's live liquid holdings, with no missing, duplicate or
  invented units. Whole-cell volume, material calibration, fixed geometry and origin kinds are checked.
- Starting quantities and starting free capacity constrain all proposals. Downward and lateral faces
  share those budgets. Incoming units cannot relay, and outgoing units cannot make same-step receiving room.
- Canonical face and source-range ordering gives deterministic transfers. Odd lateral differences retain
  their indivisible remainder. Repeated boundaries are idempotent; skipped/partial boundaries and stale plans reject.
- Actions reconcile complete material, reservoir and cell owners. Unrelated material work rebinds;
  generic water crafting that would invalidate the allocation refuses with ordinary movement/time preserved.
- Actual flow runs after actions/time and before movement. Dialogue opening suppresses that selected
  flow; held close advances no time, and release grants no catch-up allowance.
- Fresh admission requires exact unplayed profile nineteen state and terrain-supplied enclosure. Direct
  canonical profile twenty bytes and schema three retain every component through the existing sole writer.
  Earlier profiles retain their original interpretation. Host diagnostics expose actual cell placement,
  rather than labeling the aggregate equilibrium projection as a local moving surface.

## Operational limits and continuation

The 4,096-cell / 16,384-portion profile is a bounded fixed terrain domain. Overflow aborts the whole step
and faults the local session; reopening restores the prior state but does not make an intrinsically
excessive next step succeed. The durable rollback test does not execute a session fault separately.
Recoverable fluid continuation and representative-scale costs remain required work.

This interpretation has no pressure/momentum, cross-vessel transfer, mechanical shutter, fluid load,
breach/spill publication, occupied wet space or reactive second liquid. Those are explicit composition
dependencies, together with native water projection, ecological effects, encounter clocks and coarse/detail
exchange. Construction can run outside the water envelope but cannot supply its sealing faces here.
The default rendered farm/worksite is unchanged. No full P00/P01/P02, LANG, art, UX, export, device,
multiplayer or production gate is closed by this authority increment.

Frozen specifications, earlier fixtures and unrelated predecessor work are preserved. Existing ecosystem
verification, source-backed authority and architecture-maintenance doctrine already covers this work;
no additional generic process or skill layer is needed for this project-specific state contract.
