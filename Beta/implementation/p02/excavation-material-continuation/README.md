## Scope

Player and NPC work begins from actual admitted partially worked worlds. Further work can cross a cut,
complete its cell exactly once, craft away all extracted raw stock and recraft surviving outputs across
additional cuts. Direct source retirement is counted once, and the hole remains excavated. Partial work
changes neither custody nor storage revisions. Dry-profile consumption remains explicitly unavailable;
consumption-enabled care/liquid integration remains required by the full plan.

Mining and gathering share effort and one action opportunity per actor. A second actor can use the same
global boundary, but cannot rewind the owner's observed time. Rejections and cuts grant no extra labor.
Real donor movement can invalidate a captured actor pin or put an actor over a hole absent from the donor.
Unaccounted donor harvesting/mining and recapture of an older world cannot reset current component facts.
A positive reach contrast gathers through a real mined opening that donor-only geometry still blocks.

All source-world roles remain occupied, including when material batch or retired ingredient identities
would alias a mining site. Static construction preserves project funding, installed backing and support;
ordinary unreserved transfers remain possible. A real funded/completed building crosses two cuts and
reopens exactly. Unsupported reading, load, liquid, care and pending consequence owners refuse capture.

Explicit owner-bound component decoding requires matching work evidence and the actual complete owner.
A genuine sibling witness with identical raw inventory but different preceding Production cannot
substitute. Older codecs/generic cuts cannot silently drop this interpretation. Component dependencies
are not authenticated durable Host history and do not admit a continued complete world.
