# Excavation continuation: implementation boundary

The prior transport increment leaves actual excavation source/work continuity as a dependency of
coordinated complete-world material/care continuation. This increment must preserve the existing
meaning of a mined cell: material becomes available only when its entire admitted work threshold is
earned and its complete physical opening is accepted. A counted-source capacity is not extraction.

## Shared effort and action opportunities

Excavation and gathering use the same Gathering effort owner. Every successful mining swing spends
effort, including partial work that changes no material inventory. Recording only final extraction
would lose partial spends during later material cuts or idle recovery. Every accepted mining or joined
gathering swing therefore needs an ordered typed operation with actual opaque work evidence.

Partial work changes site progress, effort and material-operation history only. Completion also changes
terrain and issues one exact source lot, advancing custody and its touched store once. Rejection must
retain all owners, effort, opportunity frontiers and identities. Do not create custody revisions for
partial work merely to satisfy an older operation-count assumption.

The joined state owns exact current regions, excavation, shared effort and per-actor opportunity
frontiers. Actual committed source-world frames provide fresh actors and time under pinned source
admission. Both mining and gathering share the same per-actor frontier: an actor cannot do both at one
observed boundary. Another actor can use that same global boundary, but cannot supply an older frame
that reverses already observed world/step/process time. A failed action spends no opportunity.

## Geometry and evidence

An older source world's original solid terrain must not replace the joined state's excavated regions.
Fresh donor actors are checked against current component geometry. A pose supported in the original
world may now stand over a hole and must refuse rather than restore its floor. Unaccounted external
donor effort/cursor changes also refuse; idle recovery cannot infer away actual spending.

Cuts retain exact partial/completed work, effort, terrain and frontiers. A freshly captured original
world cannot reconcile with newer continued Production simply because its inventory and admission
still match. Required evidence binds the exact current state and material prefix. Archive tests must
rewrite a proof reference to a genuine sibling proof to reach before-state validation, in addition to
the simpler missing-proof checks.

Source coverage combines live raw intervals, imported direct raw retirement and current direct raw
retirement exactly once. Recrafting produced outputs does not count their original raw ancestry again.
Completed holes remain complete after all their source material changes form or leaves custody.

## Physical publication boundaries

The ordinary excavation planner proves the eventual opening before partial work, but complete-world
publication adds structural carried-load validation, liquid reconciliation and pending-consequence
ownership. A standalone continuation adapter cannot bypass those owners by supplying an old world or
removing its policies. Capture must explicitly refuse unjoined pending, structural, settlement, liquid,
encounter and care owners. Static construction geometry and original fabrication backing remain exact.

Pending excavation is a separate persisted calculation pinned to its complete world basis and original
command. Material compaction cannot cancel, restart or reinterpret it. Supporting that calculation,
changed structural loads, liquid breaches and complete-world publication remains required work.
Donor-world movement also does not prove navigation inside a newly opened component-only cell; live
travel through that opening requires the combined world binding.

## Consumption admission discovered during integration

Actual dry excavation worlds carry consumption-disabled Production. `WorldSnapshot` admits enabled
consumption only with Care, encounter lifecycle and resource history; the current care admission uses
the terrain-liquid profile. Those owners are outside this standalone dry component's authority.

Preserve the captured consumption interpretation. Do not fabricate a care-free enabled world or add a
silent capability toggle to make a planned test pass. This component can prove exact extracted-source
retirement through real crafting and recrafting across cuts and retain explicit consumption refusal.
The full requirement for consumption of extracted material across material/care/liquid continuation
remains in the complete-world protocol and is not qualified by this narrower component evidence.

## Review and validation scope

One read-only explorer identified existing ownership and publication barriers. One independent critic
challenged action-frontier reuse, partial-work revision semantics, donor geometry resets, recapture
laundering and exact proof substitution. Runtime implementation, behavior tests and lead-owned
codec/integration tests have separate ownership; no worker runs overlapping builds or edits Git.

Before runtime changes, the clean build and 21 Hosting baseline cases passed. Twenty-four exact
binary frames (184,230 bytes) retain real player/NPC initial, partially worked and completed worlds,
both excavation archive versions and nested Production. These establish old-format comparison inputs;
new continuation acceptance requires execution, independent source review and preservation. The
completed qualification and exact supported scope are recorded in [README.md](README.md) and the
[verification record](qualification/verification.json); baseline fixtures alone establish no new behavior.
