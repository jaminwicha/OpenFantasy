## Retained boundaries

The current inventory and production owners already conserve source intervals, produced provenance,
capacity, protection and reservations. Progression owns the existing finite currency ledger. New
exchange state may own quotes, allowance and receipts; it must not duplicate balances or stock.
Actual Host delivery and current control remain separate from payload claims. Accepting medicine,
agreeing to treatment, joining a party and accepting a quoted payment remain different acts.

Current world resource admission authenticates currency changes by replaying supported causes,
including funded quest rewards. A component-valid paid exchange therefore cannot simply replace
the progression snapshot in an existing world. The next world binding must give the complete paid
transaction its own typed cause and exact replay, alongside custody and quote allowance. Do not
mislabel payment as a quest reward, health effect or gift, or cache an admission result to bypass it.

do not edit source prices or invent infinite inventories to create a profitable demonstration.

balances. Its stated scope is one funded reward, not a market treasury. Inspect the actual chosen
world's balances before constructing a trading journey. A new merchant's purchasing power needs
explicit finite admission or actual prior earnings; a receipt must never create its own funding.

## Presentation and learning

Introduce the first transaction through a local need and one useful commodity. Keep the ordinary
view to item, quantity, total price and the reason an action is unavailable. Hover/focus inspection
can explain fees, provenance, condition and price age; earned economic knowledge may expose richer
history without forcing every player to use it. A remote price stays a dated learned observation.
Do not add a global market dashboard before the character has the relevant knowledge or role.

Asset production remains separate from engine work. Merchant cultural dress, cargo handling and
trade gestures use accepted native art and semantic action cues; this component does not license
bulk art production or claim art/audio acceptance. Preserve quiet work ambience and meaningful
feedback for transfer, refusal and changing circumstances without an alert for every price tick.
