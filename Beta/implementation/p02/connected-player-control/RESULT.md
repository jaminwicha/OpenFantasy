# Connected participant control

Consent, acquisition and character selection now connect the existing local authority to actual
authenticated TLS peers and the Godot application adapter. The permanent home character remains the
human participant; selecting another actor never borrows its delivery credentials or transfers its
inventory. Explicit scopes govern observations, inputs and participant queries.

## Implemented contract

- Profile39 exposes home-authorized consent administration. Profile40 adds explicit selected
  observations, generation/witness-bound input and query envelopes. Legacy payloads retain their bytes.
- The host confirms a Resume basis only after its bounded observation send completes. A separate
  delivered-frame proof prevents a client from racing that installation. No automatic Resume follows
  selection, revocation or owner return; old A→home→A input remains stale.
- Private facets remain withheld without the relevant scope. Paid receipts preserve original identity
  and idempotency; obtaining an old receipt never grants fresh authority. History responses match the
  exact submitting selection and operation before presentation.
- The remote session and native Characters panel bind explicit offers, acceptance, revocation,
  acquisition, selection, release, scoped pack inspection and reviewed buy inquiries. Quick actions
  and movement retain the displayed actor/witness. Transient selection/proof changes discard stale
  input without falsely failing the world or silently retargeting an action.
- Admitted history reads cancel correctly when shutdown races semaphore acquisition. Already executing
  reads finish under their original authority rules; shutdown joins workers before storage disposal.

The [timeless protocol reference](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/selected-player-protocol.md),
related architecture pages and [independent review](REVIEW.md) describe ownership and invariants.
The [native adapter notes](CLIENT_NOTES.md) retain incomplete screen and rendering coverage.

## Genuine connected journey

The four-human fixture uses actual authenticated TLS/WebSocket clients, ordinary commands and recorded
movement. Owner loss waits the unchanged real thirty-second allowance. The grantee acquires lawful
custody, selects the actor, explicitly resumes from a delivered frame, sends a nonzero movement sample
under the unchanged five-second observation assertion, and chooses an ordinary Strike with the original
zero-MP cost, positive action delay and actual damage. The 150ms movement lease is unchanged.

All four human actors earn encounter-range positioning through recorded movement. After the operated
actor's attack acceptance, its effect remains pending while the grantee deliberately selects home and
chooses Wait. The other live participants choose their own observed Wait actions. No timeout makes
their decisions. Owner return restores paused home selection; revocation preserves the already accepted
action and exact old receipt while refusing fresh stale input. Replay from original genesis reproduces
the movement, action, costs and receipts without converting humans into NPCs.
