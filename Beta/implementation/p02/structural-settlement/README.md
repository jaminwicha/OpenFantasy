# Atomic intact structural settlement

This increment adds actual excavation consequences to fresh dry profile 15. The canonical root owns a
harmless-settlement calibration alongside its full load policy. Whole rigid assemblies and riders can

## Behavior and architectural choices

- Real excavation prepares unsupported candidate geometry before stable root admission. Every base,
  upper member and rider is swept against all outside obstacles. Full contact, complete local support
  and a stable post-action load graph are required. Missing terrain, surviving partial ledges, foreign
  structures, bodies, unsafe distances and an exhausted horizon refuse the entire action.
- At most one intact assembly moves per removal. All of its members retain one vertical translation;
  original joint geometry, mass, volume, condition, member identities and installed lot remain intact.
  Exact subunit contact planes avoid treating conservative collider gaps as physical contact.
- Riders move with the assembly; other unsupported actors seek landing only after settled material is
  reinserted. Moved actors cannot also consume their selected XY movement. Independent bodies remain
  obstacles, never landing floors. Cross-assembly resting, rotation and fragmentation are unqualified.
- A removed foundation retains its immutable catalog entry and identity. Active load contact retires
  only through its exact completed excavation owner. Lower exposed terrain needs a preexisting rating.
- Final work, source lot, terrain, physical placement, occupants and terminal receipt commit together.
  A one-metre fall under the retained 9,800 mm/s² calibration takes 452 integer milliseconds, inside the
  original 1,800 ms interval. The safe distances are authored gameplay calibration, not proven injury or
  material-damage thresholds. Larger/harmful responses are refused, not erased or silently healed.
- The direct twenty-field root appends the five-field settlement policy; schema 3, full journal replay,
  root byte limits and result identity rules remain unchanged. This is an atomic bounded solve. There
  is no saved pending cursor, second time step or automatic continuation claim.

The first native protocol investigation found that correct pending work crosses exact schema/commit
kinds, deferred receipt accounting, retries and host readiness. The bounded live slice can use the
