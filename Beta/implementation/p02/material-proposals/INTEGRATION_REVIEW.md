## Result and ownership

The fresh profile30 world owns saved offers and terminal outcomes as typed immutable state.
A sender's authenticated creation records its exact grant; a separately authenticated recipient can
later accept or decline, and the sender can withdraw. Advancing world time expires offers.
Acceptance rechecks control, exact stock/terms, bodies, readiness, reach, both opportunities and
coupled physical/liquid/complete-root consequences before publishing one transaction. Creation
reserves nothing. Every command has an immutable independent receipt and permanent identity.

The SQLite owner serializes current authorization, selection and publication. Open offers survive
an automatic coordinated cut; a bounded anchor preserves their exact grants and capability.
Participant-authorized `FindRecorded` retrieves a terminal outcome after archival through one
complete verified replay pinned to the live head. No second stock authority, proposal table, cache,
new schema, native calculation layer or IPC bridge is introduced.

Profile30/action16 is a named sibling of paired profile28, not a lived upgrade or combined route.
The nominal29 gap is intentional; old bytes, tests, counters, capacities and interpretation allow-lists
remain intact. The architecture reference describes both contracts and the exact boundary.
