## Required design and implementation

1. Record your concrete ownership/lifecycle design briefly in IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md, then implement.
   Use a typed immutable proposal owner in the complete deterministic world, separate from inventory,
   production, care permission and transfer evidence. Reuse existing shared material decisions where
   semantics match; keep readable encapsulated C#, not an untyped bag/universal ECS. New canonical state
   requires an explicit new interpretation and codec, with fresh admission and named Host selection.
   Do not silently expand old profile bytes, mutate old genesis, duplicate all history into proposals,
   or introduce a second SQL journal/stock authority. Preserve named profile-family validation.
2. Proposal identity, its creation command/result, later acceptance/withdrawal/decline commands/results,
   and actual transfer identities are distinct. Creation commits a permanent creation receipt; a later
   outcome cannot overwrite it or reuse its command identity. Do not manufacture a fresh sender command
   or a current sender delivery from the recipient's payload to trick the old simultaneous pair planner.
   Persist the sender's actual authenticated grant and exact terms at creation. A historical grant is
   permission for only that proposal, not transport authentication or a reusable control credential.
3. Each incoming command and each query receives its own independently supplied TrustedCommandDelivery.
   Check principal/current session/current actor control before disclosing existence, availability or
   terminal outcome. Fresh requests need the current session; exact terminal retries may retain their
   original origin only after current authorization. The recipient cannot supply the sender's approval.
   The Host must serialize authentication, selection and publication against the same authority state.
   Public response/projection gives only authorized proposal information and the caller's receipt, not
   a privileged world root, another command stream, NPC preferences or unrelated inventory.
4. Offers reserve no stock by default. Pin exact parties/storages/scope, lot/kind/definition, quantity,
   lot revision, split identity/terms and expiry. Acceptance rechecks actual current lot, reservation/
   protection, capacity/physical consequences, parties' current control, body/readiness/reach and agreed
   terms. Unrelated bag edits do not invalidate exact unchanged stock; competing use or changed exact
   terms must not silently retarget/requote. A successful acceptance consumes both participants' required
   action/movement opportunities and publishes one complete root. Reconciliation refusal moves no goods
   and reports no success. Retain existing normal time semantics for refusal.
5. Define deterministic expiry in the existing world time/step vocabulary, not wall clock. Specify the
   exact boundary and action ordering and prove it. Withdrawal and decline are separate authenticated
   terminal actions. First authorized terminal outcome wins in authoritative order; duplicate/reordered
   requests cannot move stock twice or reopen an offer. Late acceptance gets a defined non-disclosing
   refusal. Explain whether an accepted but failed transfer closes its proposal; keep that policy explicit.
6. Disconnect is not acceptance/cancellation or control transfer. The durable offer survives reconnect,
   but stale deliveries cannot act. An earlier sender grant can remain valid only while its named sender
   principal still controls the source actor, its exact terms remain current, and expiry/withdrawal permits
   it; a session rollover is not permission to impersonate another principal. Recheck current sender
   authority at acceptance without pretending the recorded old delivery is a newly authenticated request.
   Document and test the actual supported control/session transition; never patch a lived state to claim
   a real transition. If no admitted transition exists, qualify actual disconnect/reopen with unchanged
   control and explicitly retain the missing transition proof.
7. Keep live proposals and terminal identity occupancy coherent through coordinated material/care cuts,
   recovery, recorded history and archives. Pending offers cannot disappear at a cut; spent/withdrawn/
   expired identifiers cannot become fresh. Bound added collections/frames/events explicitly and justify
   their development envelope; test exact edges. Do not change ANY existing limit/checkpoint/archive/
   journal/state budget or old validation. Failure must refuse safely rather than silently evict pending
   offers. Full production scale remains a separate gate.
8. Bind fresh admission, canonical decoding, complete root validation/identity checks, actual advancing
   actions and sole-writer Host replay/publication together. Keep one transaction for proposal changes,
   actual goods, command receipts/identities, archives, head/checkpoint. Recovery checks actual replay,
   not a self-consistent stored hash alone. Keep older profile/schema behavior exact. If reuse of schema8
   is valid for opaque roots, explicitly name the new profile across every admission/recovery/accounting
   join; don't create a table/schema by habit or allow arbitrary higher profiles.
