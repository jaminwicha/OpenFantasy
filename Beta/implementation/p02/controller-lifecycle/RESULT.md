# Durable controller lifecycle foundation

Qualified scope: explicit fresh profile 36/schema 9; immutable per-character control state; bounded
canonical Disconnected, AllowanceExpired and Reconnected events; and publication/replay through the
existing SQLite world authority. This is a dependency of the complete live disconnect policy,
not completion of its network, timer, automatic-control or production gates.

The original resident and permanent control entitlement remain intact. Recorded host elapsed duration
must meet 30,000 ms before expiry; replay never reads wall time. Stale revisions, episode IDs and occupied
causes refuse publication. Controller events change control state and world/lifecycle bookkeeping
revisions only. They do not advance a clock, resolve a choice, move a body, alter ownership or spend
resources. Reconnection cannot erase a queued combat choice, committed retreat or pending care.
All old world/schema interpretations remain explicit, with no silent save migration.
