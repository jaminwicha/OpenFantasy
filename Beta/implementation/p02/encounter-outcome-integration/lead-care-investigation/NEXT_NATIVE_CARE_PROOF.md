# Native care reachability and acceptance

This is a proposed next proof, not a dispatched assignment or passed gate. It does not expand the
currently pinned input/presentation assignment. Preserve the existing HP1 prepared-Active evidence
at its actual scope.

The retained failure is a poor starting calibration for an ordinary native approach: starting at
HP1 permits Wren's full-dose ordinary aid on the first exploration tick, spending the sole dose
before the wolf encounter. A production full-health start avoids that trigger by an existing rule,
without suppressing a policy, changing permissions or introducing stock. `LocalRestorativeController`
requires a living patient with enough missing HP to receive the full dose and runs only in exploration.
An uncalibrated `CargoOutcomeWorldAdmission.CreateEmbedded` world therefore provides a preferable
first candidate for native care reachability.

Prove the whole sequence with ordinary controls and the existing source content:

1. Open an unplayed full-health world, with Wren's actual finite medicine and unchanged permissions.
   Authenticate exact declared genesis. Native input owns selection, approach and wolf encounter entry.
2. Inspect the actual public choices and wait through the wolf's real actions until the player is
   incapacitated. Capture Wren's availability and the real control/position/stock history. Do not use
   forced damage, precomputed Outcome, grants or hidden fixture actions to reach the state.
3. Request the admitted care through its real control. Authenticate the patient's acknowledgment and
   Wren's separate own-controlled treatment, exact consumed source interval and HP change. Treatment
   resumes the same encounter and invalidates prior outcome readings in one canonical transaction.
4. Demonstrate a subsequent genuine outcome and completion/continuation or terminal handling, with
   the spent dose remaining spent; save and reopen with exact recorded identities and roots.

Retain existing deadlines and use actual observations to decide feasibility. A full-health wolf fight
may require more actions than the HP1 calibration; do not increase deadlines or manufacture damage to
fit. If the normal action loop cannot fit the existing route budget, retain that failure and separate
the needed gameplay/performance diagnosis from any proposed qualification change.

Explicit withdrawal of ordinary assistance is an alternative interaction worth testing separately,
because it preserves the separate aftermath agreement. It has an additional native scheduling question:
the player must actually submit withdrawal before the helper spends the dose. Source confirms pending
player action actors are excluded from ordinary helper selection at a cutoff; that is not proof that the
native pause/menu/input sequence lets the player win the first cutoff. Do not use that hypothesis as
the primary shortcut when a normal full-health world can provide the desired situation.

Reuse the completed input/receipt observation mechanism and immutable fixtures only after dependency
comparison. This proof is about authentic native reachability and accepted treatment, not multiplayer,
resurrection, human UI/art acceptance, production balance, or completion of the full game.
