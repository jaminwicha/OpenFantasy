# Ordinary restorative use and permission

This coherent increment implements the exploration/domain/durable portion of the preserved
[ordinary-care proposal](../care-continuation/ORDINARY_CARE_NEXT.md). It does not complete that proposal,
the full care system or a phase gate. The broader world-composition decision and unchanged specification
remain authoritative. The maintained contract is [ordinary-care architecture](../../../Runtime/docs/architecture/ordinary-care.md).

## Implemented scope

- Fresh explicit world profile26, Care V2 and action V14 retain exact ordinary-use definitions,
  patient/helper permissions, one-way patient revocations and completed-use witnesses. Profile25
  care bytes and its aftermath semantics remain unchanged; no lived-save migration is introduced.
- Self-use and assistance compose existing material retirement and capped progression restoration.
  The initiator uses their own control and exact stock; assistance additionally requires the
  recipient's scoped permission. No universal effect framework, duplicated HP or second acknowledgment.
- Real exploration steps use current candidates in actor order and advance the normal 1,800 ms
  interval once. Successful initiators cannot also move. Reading holds, pending work, encounter
  boundaries, stale selections, zero/full HP, unavailable capability, form, reach and permission
  retain their distinct rules. Ordinary use cannot bypass combat Wait or aftermath choice.
- The same schema-seven authority publishes actual commands, consumption, resource evidence,
  care records, receipts and identities. Potential writers reserve capacity before an archive cut;
  historical grants/revocations/uses survive exact retrieval and replay.
- Fresh content partitions the existing two Potion source units: one player dose and one Wren
  dose, with six berries still held by Wren. The original three-berry recipe, 40 HP effect,
  player/monster stats and reward rules are unchanged. Prior fresh care content still gives both
  doses to Wren; original production genesis hash is pinned by a regression.
- Generic `LocalWorldSession` player commands obey readiness and available exploration boundaries;
  duplicate results remain reusable while paused. This API does not create a native player offer or
  authorize a player to impersonate Wren.

## Evidence and interpretation

[Verification](qualification/verification.json) records exact final build, test counts and source pins.
[Failures](FAILURES.md) preserves failed attempts and their corrections; [review](REVIEW.md) distinguishes
independent source conclusions from executed tests. Test and architecture checks do not prove production
performance, complete gameplay or human acceptance.

The source-stat journeys earn injury through actual defeat, acknowledgment, finite Wren treatment,
victory and exploration debt retirement. Raw self-use and genuinely crafted companion use then restore
once, retain receipt identity, record exact history and reopen through SQLite. Adversarial journeys test
both real actor orders for use/revoke, simultaneous self/ally requests, actual movement out of range,
patient/helper impersonation, stale doses and evidence tampering. Controlled-clock local sessions test
ready cutoff, pause, movement exclusion, reading-hold refusal and explicit patient revocation.

The two archive tests use explicitly calibrated large HP/speed fixtures to execute 254 actual bites
and one victory. At 255, a complete batch with one valid aid request and one unowned/stale request
reserves two potential writers before cutting. This is not two funded successful heals. Separately,
one real aid fills 256; patient revocation follows, then a stale request triggers archival of the
actual prior use/revocation boundary. Retrieved bytes, original receipts, permanent identities, exact
reopening and five corrupted-save refusals are checked. These are real earned archive histories,
not original combat-balance or external-crash qualification.

## Required continuation

1. Before public ordinary offers, distinguish material/care history capacity from normal gameplay
   unavailability. The shared 256-consumption limit is independent of resource-history archive cuts;
   `Prepare` does not yet report that limit. Refusal is atomic, but ongoing care/material ownership
   needs its own admitted continuation. Do not turn this implementation envelope into a lifetime limit.
2. Bind public self/companion offers, helper-owned selection policy, player confirmations, icons/wheel,
   cancellation, focus and exact status. Prove actual native raw and crafted ordinary journeys and
   reopened use/permission state. Existing native aftermath controls remain a separate route.
3. Bind active-combat item choice/resolution under the actual due-actor Wait machinery; specify stale
   resolution costs and complete consumption/restoration/debt publication. No zero-time bypass.
4. Qualify profile26 with actual external process interruption around ordinary use/archive publication.
   Managed close/reopen and corruption are not SIGKILL evidence. Prior profile25 crash proofs remain
   retained, not represented as rerun here.
5. Add isolated unsupported-form/no-manipulation evidence when admitted transitions can create those
   boundaries, and exercise a genuinely constructible late complete-admission rollback. Source review
   verifies rollback owner restoration; current rejection tests do not execute that late failure branch.
6. Retain runtime grant creation, activities/interruption, passive healing, conditions/injury, broader
   delivery/form policies, full journal lifetime and all scale/platform/multiplayer/human/art gates.
