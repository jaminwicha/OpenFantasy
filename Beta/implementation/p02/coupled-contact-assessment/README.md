## Implemented contract

- `ConstructionContactScene` pins the retained world and complete proposed terrain/member/actor geometry.
  It retains substrate lineage and old holes, exact fabrication/backing, body identities/conditions,
  uniform downward intact poses and all colliders. Known clear space is mandatory; support is assessed
  separately. Additional holes/poses are proposals without work, source or movement authority.
- `ConstructionLoadPolicy.AssessContacts` reuses the original mass/member/body/carried-stock binding.
  It partitions each complete base footprint among exact same-plane terrain or foreign member tops,
  preserves declared internal joints and excludes same-assembly external self-support. Actor footing
  is proved before attaching weight. Full candidate and selected policy pins prevent stale reuse.
- Explicitly selected alternate calibrations support read-only comparisons. They cannot replace the
  retained policy used by stable-world admission. The architecture explains this authority boundary.
- `ConstructionLoadNeighborhood` follows both directions of bearing edges and complete rigid membership.
  It conservatively includes alternate supports and shared loads. A future consequence owner must use
  old and candidate graphs and separately close swept collisions; neighborhood membership does not
  mean every included actor/member falls.
