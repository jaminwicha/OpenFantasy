# Independent source critique

A native independent critic reviewed the coherent Core/Hosting delta with a finite 15-minute,
read-only scope. It ran no builds or tests. No external model request or Claude-credit saving is
claimed. The lead owns integration and the separately retained qualification reports.

Two concrete findings were accepted and repaired:

1. **Impossible acquisition chronology.** Two consent commits and two original-controller commits
   cannot coexist with a first acquisition at world revision four. Complete replay already refused
   the missing event, but structural root admission lacked the combined lower bound. Admission now
   counts permission revisions, original-controller revisions and each necessarily isolated
   acquisition with overflow-safe `UInt128`. Automatic release can share a permission/controller
   commit and is not counted twice. The added real-journal counterexample rejects the impossible
   fourth boundary while retaining actual acquisition five and shared revocation/release six.
2. **Public standalone planner boundary.** Optional public authorization arguments let a caller reuse
   a scope/actor/command-ID context with another request, despite its private command hash. No
   player-facing persisted exploit was established; world resolvers supplied their own exact
   operation. The repair restores the original owner-only public signatures for crafting,
   consumption, restorative care and source delivery. Authorized overloads are internal to trusted
   Core composition. Public APIs cannot substitute a delegated context for standalone ownership.

The critic re-read both fixes and found no further concrete defect in its bounded pass. It checked
the distinction between current assignment at settlement and retained evidence in closed history,
and between exact submitter receipt lookup and private-history access. It did not certify unrun
tests, produced-lot/merchant-price additions still in progress, transport presence, rendered actor
selection, native gameplay or production feasibility. Those limits are preserved in the result.
