## Review the actual delivered implementation

Challenge the implementation against all eight requirements in
`../encounter-outcome-continuation/ASSIGNMENT.md` and the world-composition contract. Inspect real code,
new tests and terminal evidence rather than trusting summaries. Separate a demonstrated defect,
a source-supported concern, a missing acceptance proof and a future full-game obligation. Existing
profiles and old assertions are protected. No compatibility or native pass excuses a missing new
behavior. Test deadlines and original failed evidence remain authoritative.

Concentrate on these consequential boundaries:

- Canonical ownership: world-owned completion, authenticated human reading, bodily action, permission,
  helper commitment and funded care must remain distinct. They must compose in one actual transaction
  where effects are coupled. Check zero-duration preservation and the exact encounter/action identity.
- Winners, losers and no survivors: neither living initiator nor living reader can be required for
  completion. Demand actual committed proof for both winners and the zero-survivor case; a codec
  round-trip is not a gameplay proof. No free reward, movement, healing, time or deleted obligation.
- Two humans and Full Wait: inspect the real scheduler/control path, missing versus received readings,
  unavailable/not-ready controllers and unrelated observers. Distinguish single-session readiness
  from a claim about a shared two-session authority. Evaluate whether any cutoff advances time or
  another player's choice after a zero-duration completion.
- Care/reading races: a pending obligation cannot be lost; real care resumes only an unfinalized
  episode and atomically invalidates old outcome readings. Prior exact receipts remain retrievable
  without becoming acknowledgment of a later result. Inspect occupied IDs, stale/conflicting requests
  and actual resource/debt/carry preservation.
- Durable interpretation: validate supported rule pins, format/schema dispatch, malformed bytes,
  authority admission versus mere structural consistency, replay and crash publication. Test fresh
  calibration honestly; an edited lived root is not a legitimate migration. Inspect repeated episodes,
  latest-witness replacement, bounded owners and retained history/identity behavior.
- Public information and UI: historical result facts are not unlimited current knowledge about remote
  actors. Inspect bounds, observers, incapacitated winners, actual reason for unavailable care,
  deliberate decline of available care, continued movement/inventory, and explicit save-and-leave.
  Native rendering, exact commands and entire journal authentication must match the claimed journey.
- Modularity and docs: preserve portable C#, in-process Host and Godot adapter responsibilities. Verify
  changed architecture explanations against source, using timeless reference prose. Identify concrete
  omissions or misleading claims; avoid stylistic rewrites and speculative generic frameworks.

The lead's `LEAD_SOURCE_FINDINGS.json` and `LEAD_DESIGN_REVIEW.json` are questions/findings to assess,
