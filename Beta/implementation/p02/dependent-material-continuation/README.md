## Implemented construction contract

`ContinueConstruction` selects V5 from an opaque construction state captured at an admitted closing
world and its exact inventory/production boundary. The context preserves the construction recipe
catalog and at most 32 original direct-source construction batches, matched to canonical imported
producer witnesses. Subsequent cuts preserve capability and classification. V1–V4 and complete-world
guards retain their original meanings.

A distinct material operation and genuine completion evidence retire project-reserved raw stock and
create its reserved installed output. Ordinary crafting cannot acquire that permission. The planner
reuses existing ownership, quest, work, tool, access and placement rules; it reconciles physical members
and returns every affected owner together. Refusal preserves the actual supplied owners. Partial work
updates fabrication and physical dependency pins without retiring material or creating members.

Per-builder work boundaries prevent repeated/backward source frames from granting work. Source
admission remains fixed, and current actor/body/terrain facts must match the actual frame; explicitly
continued structure geometry is qualified separately. Exact preceding inventory **and production**
hashes prevent completion-proof reuse against a different transformed-material state.

## Dependent terrain/liquid obligations

Read-only source audits identify additional transitions required by the same complete boundary:
terrain representation partitioning mutates exact custody and revisions outside the material operation
history; reserved reservoir transport also needs a distinct typed operation. Neither is an ordinary
transfer. A liquid-cell flow changes placement without changing material custody and can be qualified
separately. Nested request/prepared/capacity/reservoir codecs and hashes need explicit continued
interpretation with exact context; wrapping only their outer frame would discard the dependency.

A pure capacity cut must re-prepare pending runoff against the exact continued material basis while
preserving reservation, intervals, domain, timing, contributions, projections and capacity revision.
Ordinary reconciliation must still refuse a context replacement. Liquid-cell admission prohibits
pending runoff, so pending preservation and actual cell flow require separate compatible journeys.
These are implementation obligations, not completed integration or a reason to remove guards.
