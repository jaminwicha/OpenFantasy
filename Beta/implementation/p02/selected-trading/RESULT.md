# Shared selected-character trading

The ordinary Market wheel and stall screen now serve home and permitted operated characters. Trade
supplies that actor's own wallet, usable commodity lots and open quotes; Inventory and PrivateHistory
remain independent. Characters links to the shared stall instead of maintaining duplicate merchant
controls. Exact lot selection, showing, retracting a showing, asking, review and confirmation have
distinct meanings. Withdrawing a showing transfers nothing and does not close an issued quote.

Reviews retain the exact shopper, selection generation/witness, character revision, quantity and
quote terms. Changing characters or returning ownership cannot redirect an old click. Panel/stall
lifetimes exclude obsolete replies while preserving the new visit's first read; failed refresh stops
polling, and shutdown observes all admitted asynchronous wrappers. Existing home behavior remains
available through the same presentation model.

## Native proof

A fresh original four-human Cargo world uses four actual TLS/Godot clients. Preparation earns the
owner's authored 200-coin quest reward through ordinary verified commands and walks to Marla through
journaled movement. It does not mint funds, move entities directly or replace genesis, and is not
claimed as native player travel. Explicit Trade-only consent and the real 30-second disconnect allowance
precede the takeover; no Inventory or PrivateHistory permission is borrowed.

The delegate buys one ration for 20, selects that exact received lot, shows it, stops showing, shows it
again, and separately reviews/confirms its 14-coin sale. A third unconsumed review becomes unusable when
the owner reconnects and revokes permission. Five gameplay commands and three permission commands are
matched to exact receipts, principals, actor and control witness. Original-genesis replay authenticates
the whole saved world after stop/reopen; this is not a second native process epoch or a crash test.

The funded quest also activates Wren's real NPC construction. Verification accounts for exact movement,
work receipts, tool/project revisions and any completed 200-work transformation, rather than suppressing
the NPC or pretending unrelated world state freezes. Human positions, currency accounts, finite lot
identities and provenance are reconciled with those real causes. Only the owner/merchant trade balances
change by minus/plus 6; no authority, rules, deadlines or protected assertions are weakened.
