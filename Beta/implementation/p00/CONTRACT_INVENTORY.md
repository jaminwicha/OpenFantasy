## Rules selected by the technical design

- IDs compare canonical big-endian bytes. Generated identity derives from seed, generator version, stable address, role and ordinal; live identity derives from accepted command/event and spawn ordinal. Detect collisions rather than silently merging.
- X/Y/Z use signed 64-bit coordinates at 1/256 cell units, initially one metre per cell; realms are separate. Mass and volume use typed integer units. Use checked arithmetic and declared rounding. Rates/durations are not float-frame counts.
- RNG uses versioned SHA-256 counter streams keyed by seed/rules/domain/responsible identity/counter; unsigned 64-bit big-endian words and rejection sampling for bounded choices. Cosmetic streams cannot perturb authority.
- The initial six attributes are Strength, Agility, Endurance, Intellect, Will and Presence; Health, Mana and Stamina are distinct pools. Source stats remain preserved with explicit transformation records. The d100 check, tie behavior, modifier ordering and caps follow the TDD.
- Authority targets 20 steps/second; due transitions/expiry precede commands, then observations/planning/follow-ups. Due scheduling and accepted ingress order are canonical; worker completion cannot choose a winner. The initial planning limits are 32 candidates/256 expansions per decision.
- The day profile uses 2,400 exploration seconds; calendar uses signed 64-bit days plus bounded milliseconds. Wait does not accrue retrospective biological/condition exposure. Empty hosted worlds pause; acceleration needs affected-controller consent.
- SQLite is the initial durable adapter, one writer per world, WAL, `synchronous=FULL`, foreign keys and explicit transactions. Success follows durable commit. All transit-participating branches belong in that world database.
- Canonical binary uses schema tags, explicit lengths and sorted maps. Diagnostic JSON is not canonical merely because a serializer emits stable property order. Hash all consequential state.
- Package definitions and schemas are versioned; unknown references and illegal/unbounded operations reject. Ordinary content packages do not execute unrestricted C#.

## P00 concrete decisions still required

Record the exact tagged field layout, byte lengths, domain framing, version handling and golden vectors as retained implementation work. Avoid ambiguous concatenation of identity/RNG keys.
