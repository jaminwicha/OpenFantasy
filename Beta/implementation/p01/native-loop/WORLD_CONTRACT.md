# Native local-world increment

This increment connects retained presence, finite custody, explicit language, dialogue and world time. It is a bounded exploration-locality profile, not P01 or P02 completion, the complete world schema, quest completion, or production qualification. The campaign of requirements and broader implementation gates remains authoritative.

## Accepted behavior

The portable C# core has no Godot or IO dependency. `WorldSnapshot` admits the complete local set of corporeal actors and its terrain, inventory, control bindings, resident language, individual experienced time and addressed dialogue sessions. Cross-domain role aliases and inconsistent content pins are rejected on construction and restoration. Definition identities remain distinct from per-world instances. Unworked or excavated terrain source identities grant no inventory extraction rights in this profile.

The host selects a typed batch at a 50 ms cutoff. One advancing step contributes 1,800 calendar milliseconds and the same exposure increment for every local resident: a full day takes 40 minutes of advancing host time. Inputs cannot request extra steps. Advancing steps, world commit revisions and custody revisions are separate counters.

Discrete actions are ordered by actor identity and evaluated at the step's starting positions. A new conversation suppresses all movement in that step; the already selected advancing step still contributes its single time increment. An existing reading hold admits only discrete action batches, with no movement, calendar time or exposure. Closing the listener's own dialogue releases the hold; the next advancing step resumes time. This local reading hold does not implement the planned multiplayer encounter/Wait ownership contract.

Player and host-controlled NPC movement use the same explicit control, actual body dimensions, condition, swept occupancy, ground support and terrain completeness rules. Each selected movement is one cardinal 32-subunit displacement. A terrain cell is 256 subunits. This proves fixed-elevation grounded movement; vertical traversal, falling, ladders, flight and terrain-changing actions remain later coupled contracts.

Opening dialogue requires an admitted speaker, current actor and target presence revisions, reachable geometry, functional vocalization/hearing and each participant's explicitly admitted Conversational spoken channel. A speaker's authored text is its utterance, not an assertion that its contents are world truth. Restoration repeats these checks. Injuries may impair the action; ordinary scars retain its functions.

A handover checks control, current presence, reach, source ownership and manipulation before reading recipient custody. It uses the existing counted transfer planner and exact source intervals. A partial handover creates one child lot; a full handover retains the existing lot identity. It emits one outer actor-action receipt. No nested counted receipt is published and no new wood, quest reward, repaired windbreak or completion flag is invented.

## Identity and persistence boundary

Each committed batch has a fresh cause. Each discrete action has its own command identity and canonical terminal receipt. Current root, genesis, historical, sibling command, spawned entity and result identities must not collide. A prepared decision is private until the storage owner atomically commits the batch, receipts, identity reservations and new head. Exact retries belong to the durable host and do not rerun the core action.

The bounded canonical codecs restore existing body and language contracts and compose them with persistent terrain, residents, dialogue and custody. Unsupported versions, malformed lengths, duplicate or noncanonical ordering, inconsistent bindings, aliases and invalid clock remainders are rejected. Only immutable terrain object-reference matches permit reuse of the prior terrain identity index; dynamic admission always runs again.

Host input preflight must recover from an occupied client command/result identity without allowing that request to stop unrelated actors or the normal clock. Storage failures and uncertain commits require recovery; they cannot become a convenient gameplay rejection. The host-session and storage evidence cover these separate responsibilities.

## Verification status

The core test pass includes independent action and receipt encoding vectors, collision and body restoration checks, action-first hold timing, player/NPC parity, finite transfers, rejected actions, historical identity collisions and a composed move/speak/give/restore trace. `core-validation/VALIDATION.json` binds exact source and test-assembly hashes to the retained TRX results.

These tests do not establish art acceptance, fun, seamless frontier streaming, gathering, crafting, Wait combat, Wren quest completion, multiplayer or desktop export readiness. The original source packet and its omitted gameplay requirements remain retained for the connected first loop and subsequent phases.
