## One honest object and its bill

Build a **small freestanding seedling wind guard**, not a farm-sized fence. The baseline already defines **one metre per cell and 256 subunits per cell** ([technical design §3](../../../spec-v1_1/TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md)); the new blueprint must pin that existing calibration. Six exact original wood units are **300,000 mg and 600,000 µL** under the retained [production admission](../../../Runtime/content/production-farm/admission-v1.json). They cannot fund a large windbreak.

The other critic supplied this concrete all-wood cross-slot candidate:

| Member or output | Exact proposed physical dimensions/allocation |
|---|---|
| Upright panel before cutting | 400 × 246 × 5 mm = 492 cm³ |
| Two perpendicular feet before cutting | Each 125 × 20 × 10 mm = 25 cm³; total 50 cm³ |
| Panel slots | Two bottom slots, each 10 × 10 × 5 mm; remove 1 cm³ total |
| Feet slots | One top slot per foot, each 5 × 10 × 10 mm; remove 1 cm³ total |
| Installed guard | 540 cm³ solid wood, **270,000 mg**, one derived output |
| Retained offcuts | **30,000 mg**, three units of the existing exact 10,000 mg offcut definition |

The raw allocation is 300 g → 270 g guard + 30 g offcuts. Cutting the gross 542 cm³ pieces leaves 58 cm³ from the raw stock; cutting their slots adds 2 cm³. Existing offcuts occupy 25 mL per 10 g unit, so the output's occupied packing volume is 75 mL; it is not a claim that 75 mL of solid wood was created. Mass conservation and collision-envelope volume are different checks.

Proposed local member coordinates in millimetres: panel X0–400, Y0–5, Z0–246; feet X75–85 and X315–325, Y−60–65, Z0–20. Complementary bottom/top slots interlock over Z0–20. This yields ten disjoint physical boxes. Panel and feet rest directly on the existing surface; there are no nails, rope, embedded stakes, or unrecorded terrain removal. Outward rounding each physical bound to integer subunits gives conservative collision boxes; rounding may overlap boxes within this one assembly and must never inflate accounted material mass.

The blueprint still needs a pinned usable-stock/joinery interpretation and **an actual Wren-owned suitable tool with explicit admitted capability**. The imported wood item has no board shape, and the current copper axe/sickle or Wren's Unassigned stats do not prove fine slot cutting. A finite authored tool supply is an explicit new admission, not an imported NPC possession. Do not claim weather resistance, horticultural benefit, tool wear, or a structural engineering simulation merely from this geometry.

A reasonable candidate site is a clear part of the farm near cell (106,324), with work stance near (106,325), floor feet Z256, away from the oak at (101,327), berry plant at (105,332), and Wren's home at (103,322). The coordinator rejected the earlier (104,323) suggestion because it lies on the house floor. These replacement coordinates remain **candidates requiring final source-map, visual and clearance validation**, not an executed placement proof. Pin the assembly origin, orientation, stock pickup point, work stance, and protected route anchors in the new content record. Keep the player and Wren outside the future assembly at completion.

| `WorldSnapshot`, `WorldStepProcessor`, shared SQLite world authority | Keep job, stock, produced output, geometry, identity registrations and public outcome in the same immutable candidate and durable commit. |

**Do not silently turn an instant recipe into a timed job.** `CraftRecipeDefinition` currently requires physical `RequiredWork == 1`; `InstantCraftPlanner` can execute any admitted recipe from ordinary eligible inputs. Adding a guard recipe without a new eligibility rule would let `CraftIntent` bypass the construction work and tool. Choose either a small explicit versioned production execution-kind contract or a construction-only material-finalization binding whose one operation is legal only after the typed job completes. The latter must be enforced in the authoritative ordinary craft resolver and recipe projection, not only hidden from the C key. Preserve the old instant ration interpretation and frozen bytes. An internal validated job context is preferable to `ignoreReservation=true` or temporarily unreserving stock.

Minimum meaningful additions are one pinned blueprint/process definition, one bounded construction snapshot containing jobs and installed structures, one concrete start/resume action, checked progress/completion planners, and real structure-aware geometry. Jobs must retain exact bill/lot revisions, reservation, builder, tool, work stance, progress/remainder, and state/reason for interruption. Completed structures reference their actual installed derived lot and exact blueprint; they are not duplicated inventory. Reject aliases across job/structure/output/cause/tool/definition roles with the existing world and historical identity checks.

## Wren must actually do the work

Suggested initial calibration from the other critic: **200 WorkUnits at one unit per 1,800 ms of eligible experienced work**, equal to six local calendar minutes and ten nominal active host seconds under this farm profile. Pin this as a new game rule, not a measured woodworking duration. Retain any fractional remainder so splitting intervals cannot change completion. Manual pause, loss of client readiness, held dialogue, unsupported work, movement away, and offline wall time add no eligible work.

The current `LocalNpcController` only performs home amble. Add an explicit project priority and bounded deterministic route to the pickup/work stance. It submits ordinary actor movement and an authorized start/resume through the same host cutoff as player intents; no teleport, hidden host grant, or rendering callback advances construction. One active job owns the builder's work interval; reject double booking and prevent work plus displacement from claiming the same interval. Use a stable route order and bounded search budget, retain a retry/blocked state, and avoid re-searching an unchanged obstruction every rendered frame.

For every eligible interval, revalidate builder control/body/tool, exact reservation/materials, reach and support. Pause without losing legitimate progress when they become invalid. At finalization recheck the complete future volume and important reachable connections. A player standing in the footprint postpones the build; the game must not move the player or activate an intersecting collider. An unfinished reservation is not an invisible wall. No construction work progresses in a held interaction; closing the dialogue releases work on later advancing steps. Existing quest completion and rewards remain final while the subsequent job is blocked.

Keep the structural profile explicit: this guard bears only its own declared 270 g through pinned direct-ground contacts; it is not a floor, bridge, climbing surface or general load-bearing building. Missing support prevents start/progress/completion. Tests can remove support in candidate terrain to prove rejection, but live mining, collapse and conserved debris remain future work. If removal of completed support becomes a supported action, it must couple to a declared fall/collapse outcome before publication.

## Author implementation handoff: spatial component only

The coordinator subsequently approved the 270 g guard/three-offcut calibration and assigned the reviewer to implement the Spatial component. Consequently this section is **author documentation**, not independent validation of that implementation. The Construction and Hosting review remains independent of their authors.

The current executable API adds immutable `SubunitBox` and `SpatialStructure` values, `LocalSpatialSnapshot.Structures`, `WithActors` and `WithStructures`. Existing constructors default to no structures and existing enum values retain their numbers; `BlockedStructure` is appended. Actual corporeal occupancy and swept movement test the union of member boxes. Positive overlap blocks, exact face contact permits movement; sightline grazing remains conservatively occluded like the prior terrain contract. Actor interaction, resource interaction and the new `QueryPointInteraction` share structure obstruction. The point query uses actor-center height and a bounded same-floor range, not a separate actor or a complete reach/animation model.

A locality admits at most 32 structures, each at most 32 unique boxes with a combined span at most 2,048 subunits per axis. Internal collision-envelope overlap is legal after quantization; different installed structures may not overlap. All boxes require known clear terrain. Direct ground support covers each complete bottom face at the assembly's lowest Z; raised members do not need separate ground directly underneath. The canonical Construction owner must establish their actual joint/bearing relation. Structure identity may not alias known spatial actors, forms, physical bodies, places, body definitions, world, branch or realm; complete cross-domain identity admission remains the world owner's responsibility.

`QueryGroundRoute(actorId,destination,maximumExpansions=256)` uses the actual actor body and position. Its explicit `from,destination` overload qualifies authored connections without relocating that actor. Search uses a deterministic cell-center lattice and individually swept cardinal connectors for arbitrary subunit endpoints. Every emitted segment is at most 256 subunits on one axis; results exclude the start and include the destination. The search can conservatively miss a narrower off-lattice route. Expansion limit, unknown space, missing locomotion, different floor, obstruction and numeric failure are explicit outcomes; none proves placement or authorizes movement. At most 256 BFS nodes expand per query, plus bounded start/end connectors; this is not a measured frame-time guarantee.

`PrepareStructurePlacement(proposed,requiredAccess,maximumExpansions=256)` requires one through 32 explicit actor/from/to requirements, copies them, checks insertion against all supplied actors/structures/terrain/support, and returns a candidate only if every required route succeeds in the candidate geometry. Access anchors must be complete for the admitted content; the geometry API cannot infer which world connections matter. Maximum search cost is per access requirement, not a claim of 256 total expansions for 32 connections. No input collection or original snapshot is mutated. Profile-five serialization must deliberately derive these structures from canonical Construction state; old spatial encoding must not silently discard them. The other Core owner handles those world codec guards and profile integration.

Nineteen new Spatial Facts were authored, including repeated 32-subunit controller-sized replanning, thin-sweep blocking, blocked sole corridor, off-grid negative endpoints, raised-member support, missing support at a face edge, source/resource obstruction, stable ordering, malformed shapes and explicit search/access bounds. At this handoff they are **unexecuted** under the coordinator's build hold. No compilation, test pass, native movement, rendering or performance qualification is claimed here.
