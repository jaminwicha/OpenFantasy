## Closed finding: live nodes could outlive their finite reserve

The earlier constructor admitted an oak with four unissued units, five remaining swings and a power-one axe. Four successful swings would leave a visible live node with one required swing and no stock; extraction could never finish. Regrowth already required a complete next cycle, but new/restored live state did not impose the matching condition.

`GatheringSnapshot` now requires unissued stock to cover `RemainingSwings × strongest admitted matching tool power` before admitting each live node. The current-cycle and regrowth guards agree. `ExactPowerNeverCapsYieldAtTheFiniteSourceBoundary` now rejects both the short initial budget and an insufficient partially harvested power-three state. Exact-power output is retained; the fix does not silently shrink yield or reset the source cursor.

The independent arithmetic check covers every supported remaining swing count, strongest power 1–3, selected matching power at or below that maximum, and 33 reserve margins. If reserve is at least `r × pmax`, spending `p ≤ pmax` leaves at least `(r − 1) × pmax`. The finite authored power-one profiles contain 32 complete cycles and consume 160 oak or 128 berry units through adjacent, nonoverlapping source intervals.

## Static scope and conclusions

- **Finite source and candidates.** `GatherPlanner` spends one swing and 400 banked effort, creates exactly the selected owned tool's power at the next unissued interval, and advances node, destination storage and custody revisions together. Rejected capacity, reservation, revision, rights, identity and checked-arithmetic cases retain both original candidates. No regrowth branch resets the cursor. The standalone planner is a candidate API; it does not authenticate delivery or prove historical ownership of a source.
- **Actor applicability and privacy.** Current actor control precedes private tool lookup/revision details. Source anchors are nonblocking, require clear admitted terrain and direct support, and use same-floor exact bounded center reach with solid/missing terrain occlusion. Manipulation is an actual body-condition prerequisite. The same action applies to an NPC with its own admitted control and owned tool; nearby NPC inventory is not granted to the player. The worker's other reviewed fixes for shared item pins and anchor support are present; they were not discoveries of this reviewer.
- **Time and action ordering.** Effort gain ten, cap 1,000 and cost 400 retain banked readiness rather than introducing a cooldown. A swing's depletion time is the action-start advancing-step ordinal. The already selected ordinary step then contributes once. Reading holds prevent gathering and further clock/effort growth; opening a hold earlier in a batch prevents a later swing. Rejection of one command does not cancel an independently selected advancing step. Commands use starting spatial state, with movement afterward. Respawn at 900/700 committed advancing steps and stump visibility below elapsed 20 have explicit boundaries; no wall-clock or offline growth is used.
- **Atomic outer root.** The processor uses evolving inventory and gathering candidates in canonical actor order. Same-node revision competition rejects the later stale request. Batch causes, commands, results and spawned identities are reserved, and historical spawned collisions discard the extraction candidate. The final root validates complete actor work, scope/time equality, exact source/yield/capacity joins, tool pins, issued-right cursors and cross-domain identities before publication. A database authority must continue to persist the complete root, receipts and identity history together; this code review does not replace the separate persistence review.
- **Strict restoration and compatibility.** Component field/count/byte bounds are checked before collection expansion. Exact tags, widths, enum/rule values, depletion constraints, canonical sorting and final re-encoding reject ambiguous component input. Profile two wraps a retained profile-one exploration basis plus mandatory gathering state. Dispatch uses an exact bounded tag/version and rejects unknown versions instead of guessing. Existing profile-one requests, batches and worlds retain their own codec. Golden tests compare independently encoded action, batch and negative-coordinate component bytes and reject every truncation and trailing byte; profile tests cover old dispatch and explicit mismatch/unknown-version rejection. Constructor tests cover semantic constraints beyond those byte cases.

The reviewer also read the source-linked contract and planned next transformations. The imported gathering node counts, random relocating source respawn, unlimited source minting and original partial-pack behavior are explicitly distinguished from the new finite anchored admission and atomic rejection. Selecting an explicit owned tool is the current API; the supplied kit has only one power per kind, so it matches the source's strongest-tool choice for this admission. A future profile with multiple simultaneously owned powers must deliberately decide selection behavior before claiming full source-equivalent tool preference.
