# Lead review before independent challenge

The three-pass analysis is useful candidate work, not an accepted change to the architecture or a
full-scope audit. It returned successfully with verified context, but omitted the required requirements
ledger and implementation plan. No source tests ran. The independent review must correct coverage and
source claims before these recommendations become implementation requirements.

Specific challenge points:

1. WC-01 test 3 says a no-level XP reward creates no record, contradicting actor-resources.md and the
   selected decision: that reward advances actor revision. Preserve continuity for unchanged-resource
   rewards. Comparing only the latest value is insufficient; validate the entire admitted chain and
   cause-specific/journal evidence. Do not turn a self-consistent claim list into authentication.
2. Reconciled copies of exposure/readiness can be legitimate projections or compatibility witnesses.
   Check actual constructor/admission invariants before declaring duplicated authority or a missing
   test. Per-subject divergence remains a design obligation, not proof the current coordinated profile
   is invalid. Don't erase useful source evidence to meet a simplistic one-value rule.
3. WC-04's proposed 'treatment pins body revision only' is unsafe if movement changes reach or delivery.
   Separate body ownership if warranted while retaining exact geometry and interaction dependencies.
4. Verify source claims about constructor guards, existing injury/condition types, custody lot identity
   under transfer, and acyclic recipe/provenance rules. Batch ancestry and recipe-definition cycles are
   different. Several conclusions currently rely on documentation alone or are explicitly unverified.
5. Shared capability evaluation must preserve action-specific semantics. Life, consciousness,
   incapacity, form and control are distinct; don't replace them with one universal Alive enum or
   accidentally prohibit legitimate targeting/treatment of unconscious bodies or spirits.
6. WC-06 is a useful content-binding direction, but the new planner deliberately composes one
   instantaneous health remedy. A bandage treating bleeding isn't that operation. Move varying policy
   into definitions when real alternatives require it; don't inflate the narrow planner into a
   universal medical/magic dispatcher.
7. A process-order contract is needed, but 'damage before recovery' is a proposed gameplay decision,
   not derived necessity. Explain ordering, exact thresholds, non-integrable processes, interrupted
   activities and deterministic scheduling without imposing one global phase table on every domain.
8. Compaction must preserve accessible causal archives, interval ownership and authentication across
   timeline branches. A union plus hash is not by itself sufficient. Individual item identity also
   needs explicit continuity through assembly, repair, replacement, crafting and cross-branch travel.
9. Check the structural/wet lineage join against actual exclusions and the production plan. Preserve
   gate strength and compatibility; a written join obligation isn't a claim that joining is easy.
10. Full coverage must include divine/cosmic/species/cultural systems, skills and research, combat and
    action-puzzle interactions, settlement/nation construction, resource economies, ecology/geology,
    death/afterlife/dream/plane/time-travel identities, progressive disclosure and art/audio/animation.
    Tie findings to real requirements and phases rather than inventing phase numbers or new policies.

Accept useful extensions only with clear owners, contrasting concrete uses, proposed invariants,
meaningful adversarial tests and incremental integration boundaries. Avoid a compulsory ECS/DSL,
generic property mutation, universal event store, global social transaction or a rewrite of every
existing owner. The goal is stronger systemic coherence and rich emergent behavior in pure C#.
