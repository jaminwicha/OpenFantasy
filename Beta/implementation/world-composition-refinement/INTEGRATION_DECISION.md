## Corrections to the independent review itself

1. Its care sketch lists `LevelGainRefill` beside `ExperienceReward` and describes a resource-kind
   entry while requiring exactly one entry per actor revision. Use one actor transition envelope
   containing HP, MP, level, experience and capacities for the complete reward. A separate refill
   revision would violate the selected resource primitive. Multiple actors in one command remain valid.
2. `WithResourceTransition` and `WithActorState` are not literally all snapshot writers; combat also
   constructs a complete progression snapshot for its affected actors. Inventory every real writer
   when wiring history rather than trusting that short list.
3. Reject **illegal or unbounded** recipe cycles according to the compiler policy. Do not reject every
   self-referential/reversible recipe by name: finite inputs, losses, stages and explicit bounded
   iteration can make such recipes valid. Immutable batch ancestry still cannot be cyclic.
4. The statement that fitting is never transformation applies to identity-preserving assembly only.
   A recipe that melts, fuses or consumes a component must declare the actual transformation. Do not
   preserve a phantom independent part after its material has changed disposition.
5. Fresh profiles are required when canonical bytes or admission interpretation change. A
   behavior-preserving capability helper or documentation clarification does not inherently require
   another profile. The existing 16 MiB envelope is not a proof of production-scale adequacy.
6. The critic's 1,849-case baseline is historical. The committed resource-primitives increment passes
   1,887 cases. Separate current WIP checks belong to the care packet and do not promote that code.
7. Its numbered steps are a dependency sketch, not a replacement campaign schedule. In particular,
   evidence/testimony, audio, challenge composition and the two-market loop remain P02 obligations,
   even though later numbered steps revisit them. The [phase cross-check](phase-crosscheck.json)
   retains 52 explicit requirement references against actual COVERAGE rows. Introduction and full
   completion differ: for example RQ-126 begins P02 and completes P12, while the stronger rest proof
   appears in P03. No phase is silently deferred by this supplement.
8. The review did not inspect test bodies, Hosting/client behavior or complete per-row coverage.
   Its 'coverage missing' labels mean unverified, not proof no test exists. It supplies no bug
   reproduction or completed implementation. Do not replace required evidence with critic agreement.

vehicles, skills/puzzles, evidence/language/presentation, planes/death/dreams/time travel and residency.
Each future unit must name its owners, active/passive laws, exact dependencies, interruption and
observable consequences. Full-scope coverage and production feasibility remain pending actual work.
