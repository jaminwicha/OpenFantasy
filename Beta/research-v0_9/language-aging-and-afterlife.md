# Languages, material culture, slow lives and the consequences of death

Research date: 8 September 2026. Project: **Beta**; predecessor: **Alpha OpenFantasy**. This study separates documented reference behavior from proposed Beta rules. Its eight CULT cases are retained specifications and remain **unrun**.

Language should enrich belonging, travel and discovery while ordinary play stays readable. Aging should support lasting relationships rather than impose a frequent replacement cycle. Death can leave memories, obligations, remains and occasionally a supernatural presence. Those possibilities must use the existing people, history, custody and knowledge systems; a spirit is not an excuse to duplicate a person or turn every funeral into compulsory quest work.

## 1. Primary evidence and its limits

In a February 2024 direct interview, Rundisc's Julien Moya explains that *Chants of Sennaar* teaches languages through culturally coherent situations. Words recur in different contexts, allowing players to revise guesses; extra occurrences and clues helped address playtest difficulty. However, the team deliberately allowed some periods of confusion. Its authored language puzzle is therefore a useful model for optional deciphering, not evidence that Beta should obscure essential everyday interactions. [Moya interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/immersing-players-in-the-culture-of-a-people-with-language-puzzler-chants-of-sennaar).

Grinblat and Bucklew's 2017 *Caves of Qud* paper describes stateful mythic biographies and curated replacement grammars that connect historical descriptions, cultural artifacts, places and objects. It explicitly distinguishes history constructed as an artifact from a continuously simulated causal process. Beta can use restrained cultural vocabulary and inscriptions as discoverable evidence, but must not borrow retrospective cause invention for already committed history. The paper does not establish a comprehensive actor language-proficiency system. [Author paper, sections 1 and 4–5](https://www.pcgworkshop.com/archive/grinblat2017subverting.pdf).

Freehold's later developer responses also expose a production cost: localization work involves evolving text infrastructure and language-specific support, rather than replacing English words alone. That discussion is evidence about the localization effort at the time, not a confirmed release-language promise. For Beta, fictional-language simulation and real-world localization need separate representations from the start. [Developer-labelled discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/333640/discussions/0/837249796380223120/).

Bay 12's 2011 development log discusses delaying death confirmation to avoid revealing a hidden crime, allowing memorialization for missing bodies, and recording former offices for memorial text. Its December 2020 art update explicitly connects coffins with controlling ghosts and shows differentiated remains. These are dated developer descriptions, not an inspection of current proprietary ghost code or proof that every death must cause a haunting. [2011 log, 31 October and 16 December](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2011.html), [2020 update, 13 December](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/).

DFHack's versioned `autoslab` documentation provides narrower public tooling evidence: it queues memorial engraving for existing ghosts, avoids duplicate slabs/orders and requires available slabs. It is a third-party work-order interface, not the underlying Dwarf Fortress implementation. The useful lesson is that remembrance can be ordinary delegated production with finite obligations. [DFHack 53.08-r1 documentation, autoslab](https://docs.dfhack.org/en/53.08-r1/docs/tools/autoslab.html).

The official **D&D SRD 5.2.1** distinguishes literal language comprehension from decoding secret messages. Its corpse-questioning rule limits testimony to knowledge and languages held in life, permits unhelpful answers, and distinguishes this operation from returning the soul. Its revival spells have explicit eligibility, body-repair and old-age limitations. These examples demonstrate useful semantic distinctions; Beta should author its own names, quantities, costs and cosmology. No spell text or complete reference mechanic is imported here. [Official SRD, printed pages 117, 159, 164 and 170–171](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf).

## 2. Language as optional depth

**Proposed Beta design:** maintain language families, varieties, scripts and specialized registers as distinct definitions with history and geographic/cultural associations. An occupational cant can conceal a particular meaning within otherwise familiar speech. A national boundary need not be a linguistic boundary; migrations, trade, adoption and individual learning can cross both. Species anatomy may constrain vocal or gestural production without deciding a person's culture, intelligence or worth.

Each actor records applicable listening/sign comprehension, reading, expression and specialized knowledge. Related dialects can have bounded mutual intelligibility, independently of fluency in a script or familiarity with a guild's references. Use a few meaningful proficiency bands and supported vocabulary domains before contemplating a universal grammar simulator. Casual conversations should not require a skill roll for every sentence.

Ordinary services in supported beginner regions need legitimate accessible communication: a commonly learned trade language, multilingual staff, a willing interpreter, clearly demonstrated quantities/actions or an available translation capability. This is a generation and service-design obligation, not secret universal fluency. A displayed price can be understood through numerals and demonstration while a merchant's private conversation remains unknown. Essential danger cues should have comprehensible visual/action alternatives.

Render understood content in the selected interface language, retaining a little cultural phrasing, names or an optional original-script view. Avoid dense phonetic spelling and paragraphs of decorative gibberish. Mark uncertainty or missing meaning concisely. Greater expertise can reveal an inscription's nuance, a social opportunity, an older place name or a coded implication; it should not force expert players into more clicking for the same purchase.

Optional deciphering adventures need several independent contexts, persistent hypotheses and meaningful alternate routes. A translator, comparative record or historical visit may offer a different approach with real access and costs. Do not require the human to solve a language puzzle simply to keep a character fed. Conversely, choosing readable assistance does not automatically teach the actor a secret cant or grant knowledge of an unseen message.

## 3. Communication remains scoped evidence

Represent an utterance or inscription with stable content identity, expressed propositions, source knowledge/beliefs, intended medium, language/register and physical availability. Listening, reading and interpretation produce recipient-specific evidence. The localized sentence is presentation of that permitted evidence, not the authoritative command or an unrestricted truth record sent to every client.

An interpreter is an actual participant with comprehension, loyalty, time and a reason to cooperate. Translation can be incomplete or deceptive where supported, but repeated UI inspection cannot reroll meaning. Preserve the source, translation and the recipient's confidence separately. Knowing what a sentence means does not establish that it is true, nor does ordinary translation automatically defeat encryption or insider references.

Use authored semantic templates and tested grammar/localization rules, including stable references to people and objects. Optional model-generated phrasing remains scoped, validated and recorded; it cannot invent an agreement or reveal a hidden ancestor. Font coverage, text expansion, signing animations, captions and name handling belong in the native production matrix. Four co-op players can receive different legitimate interpretations without silently sharing character knowledge through the UI.

## 4. Slow aging within one chronology

The selected 40-minute full-day target means 100 uninterrupted real hours correspond to approximately 150 world days before sleep acceleration, tactical rates or holds. This is arithmetic on Beta's initial clock contract, not a measured campaign result or a calendar-year definition. There is no need to compress decades into that interval to make lifecycle systems visible.

Keep birth date, elapsed calendar history, biological age/exposure, development stage and remaining health distinct. Mature player and NPC lives should generally unfold slowly through long species-appropriate spans. Aged residents, established families and already progressing pregnancies can demonstrate life continuity without making every new relationship end during one campaign. Birth and maturation retain their own plausible profiles; do not accelerate children merely to showcase succession.

Biological aging uses owned applicable exposure, including the already selected Wait suspension rules. A historical journey changes the date and surroundings; it does not instantly age a traveler to match the destination. Declared long-term advancement can age those who actually live through it, with affected-controller consent, care arrangements and meaningful interrupts. A curse, rejuvenation or unusually long-lived form needs explicit effects and provenance, not a second hidden clock.

Separate slow natural aging from the consequences of combat, illness or disaster. This steering does not imply that a fatal wound must take years to resolve. Calibrate natural mortality over campaign-length exposure and disclose practical care risks without exposing a predetermined secret death roll. Long-horizon world history still contains generations and deaths; ordinary sessions should not become a repetitive funeral-management workload. Exact lifespans and acceptable event frequency remain human-tested tuning gates.

## 5. One person, several possible post-death states

**Proposed Beta design:** death commits one transition with a stable cause and death occurrence ID. It closes the living body's authority, interrupts its work, places remains and possessions into explicit custody, and starts applicable care, estate and office continuations. Actual death, legal presumption and each observer's belief about death remain separate. A missing person or rumor must not reveal truth through a quest icon or translated epitaph.

Track bodily remains, the continuing person's identity, any active spirit embodiment, a place-bound memory imprint and an independently created undead construct as different kinds of state. A memory echo need not be a conscious person. If a real spirit manifests, it has a supported form, senses, actions, location/tether and control policy attached to the same branch incarnation. It cannot retain unrestricted living-body actions after death revoked them, operate two inventories or continue a dead official's powers by default.

An afterlife realm is an actual declared realm relation with entry, return, perception, cost and clock rules. Divine intervention is a capability of a deity or authorized agent with constraints; a priest's explanation remains a belief unless evidence supports it. Spirits retain what they knew and can learn only through their supported post-death senses. Death must not grant omniscient testimony, free reconnaissance through every wall or a universal escape route from danger.

Hauntings should be conditional and uncommon, tied to selected causes, attachments, magical fields or unresolved relationships. Some are quiet, benevolent or ambiguous; others dangerous. Burial, memorial, ritual, negotiation, fulfilled promises or specialist aid can provide declared resolutions. No rule should demand a personally maintained marker for every dead creature forever. Families and institutions can perform ordinary care, and rituals must permit appropriate traditions and irretrievable remains. Damage to a grave can matter without automatically restarting an endless punishment cycle.

Resurrection must atomically reconcile spirit/body state, eligibility, permissions, costs and location. A second caster cannot revive another copy; old remains cannot become a second controllable incarnation. Material body restoration uses an explicit transformation/source rule. Existing inheritance, remarriage, succession and sincere grief do not roll back automatically. A returned person and society can respond through ordinary relationships and law. Preserve concluded rites and memories rather than treating resurrection as deletion of the death event.

## 5a. Culture expressed through settlement and craft

Mark R Johnson's **Ultima Ratio Regum** development accounts provide concrete spatial evidence. His June 2023 update describes courtyard pattern families based on civilization aesthetics, houses occupying previously empty castle surroundings, and climate-linked storage buildings. It also explicitly says almshouse occupants would need later AI work, and rejects tying every charitable institution mechanically to one political policy. This demonstrates authored cultural variation and its limits: a generated institution is not proof of an operating society. [Johnson, 0.11 development update](https://www.markrjohnsongames.com/2023/06/03/ultima-ratio-regum-0-11-update-5-relics-crowns-and-buildings/).

His 2015 GDC Europe slides further distinguish mansion layouts, whose numerous room/function constraints favored authored arrangements with variation, from more algorithmic cathedral generation. This supports mixing validated spatial patterns and procedural composition instead of requiring one universal building generator. It is a dated design account, not evidence of Beta's full cultural evolution or current URR release capabilities. [Johnson, Handmade Detail in a Procedural World, slides 29–32](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdceurope2015/Johnson_Mark_HandmadeDetailIn.pdf).

The Qud author paper cited above adds an artifact-level principle: cultural descriptions can lead to persistent sites and objects with related histories. Beta should extend that relationship through actual building and production records. A maker's mark, a repaired communal oven and an inherited street plan can refer to the same tradition without requiring every belonging to share one decoration.

**Proposed Beta adaptation:** cultural traditions should supply distributions, learned patterns and contested preferences. Separate national institutions, local communities, household needs, guild practices, available techniques, climate/material access and individual commissions. No species or citizenship value selects a compulsory universal style. A ruler may commission a monumental avenue while adjacent households continue using older courtyards; a religious minority may adapt an existing hall rather than receive an automatically isolated district.

Generate settlements through functional relations before ornamental choices. Determine real access to water, food, work, trade, gathering, care, worship, burial and defense. Select compatible street/block/courtyard and building patterns; allocate plots and rooms; then validate entrances, routes, service capacity, support, drainage and everyday use. Cultural values can favor shared kitchens, processional routes, private entrances, covered markets or distributed meeting places. Terrain, ownership, labor and existing buildings constrain which preferences can be realized. A preferred shape never overrides a blocked exit or conjures a working aqueduct.

The visible distinctions should change experience: where neighbors meet, how carts reach workshops, whether a ceremony shares a market square, where smoke and noise travel, and which routes remain public. Buildings need actual occupants, schedules and permission rules. Restrained silhouettes, thresholds, rooflines, material joints and a few meaningful motifs communicate identity; multiplying decorative clutter is not the objective. Common essential services still need recognizable cues and readable navigation under the onboarding contract.

Craft traditions are versioned families of real process definitions and teaching relationships. Differences may concern joinery and repairability, furnace/fuel requirements, alloy treatment, weaving patterns, glazes, fermentation or preservation methods. Where supported, they change tools, inputs, stages, resulting properties and maintenance—not just item names or bonuses. A learned foreign technique must fit the available material, workshop and worker capabilities. Use the same conservation and bounded research rules as other crafting; “traditional” does not exempt a recipe from physical costs.

Carry traditions through apprenticeship, books, trade, migration, patronage and individual experimentation. A migrant craftsperson retains existing knowledge, acquires others through actual learning and may develop a supported hybrid technique. A household's changed allegiance does not immediately repaint its roof, replace its language or destroy its tools. New construction, repair, demolition and deliberate renovation record the change over time. Older layers survive conquest or cultural change unless an event removes them, enabling historical comparison and disputed interpretations.

Candidate selection can score compatible patterns using cultural, functional and environmental preferences, but hard legality and feasibility constraints win. Record the selected tradition/version, builders, materials and causes. New settlement generation may assemble coherent prior history; live updates must perform actual work. Qualify recognizable variety across multiple places in one culture as well as differences between cultures. Native artists and playtesters must establish that those differences are both legible and useful; permutation counts alone cannot do so.

## 6. Retained validation — all unrun

| Case | Required result |
|---|---|
| **CULT-01 — Readable multilingual life** | A supported start includes two cultural varieties, a different script and a specialized cant. Player and NPC comprehension differ. Demonstrate ordinary trade, shelter and known-hazard communication through real accessible means; removing an interpreter triggers an honest alternative or stated limitation, not universal hidden fluency. |
| **CULT-02 — Interpretation and optional discovery** | Four players inspect the same inscription and overhear a conversation with different actor knowledge. Retain hypotheses, legitimate translation, deception and repeat inspection; no secret truth or language learning leaks through packets, localization, captions or tooltips. Optional deciphering can be bypassed through a valid alternative without invalidating the world. |
| **CULT-03 — Slow lives and mixed clocks** | Compare long ordinary play, interrupted sleep acceleration, distant batching, held encounters and historical transit. Retain equivalent owned biological exposure, stable birth identities and no destination-date aging or catch-up. Human sessions assess natural-loss frequency; pre-existing families and near-due events demonstrate lifecycle depth without forced generational speedup. |
| **CULT-04 — Death, spirit and knowledge** | Kill a real actor once across retry/restart, with an unseen witness and an applicable spirit outcome. Verify remains, estate, office and care transitions; one person/form authority; only supported spirit senses/actions; distinct legal presumption and observer belief; no secret death confirmation or free inventory/scouting. |
| **CULT-05 — Finite remembrance and haunting** | Resolve an eligible haunting through different supported traditions and delegated jobs, including inaccessible remains and a flooded grave. Preserve costs, memories and completed obligations; repeated entry/reload cannot duplicate spirits or demands. Extended settlement sessions must show optional atmospheric depth without universal recurring memorial chores. |
| **CULT-06 — Return without duplication** | Two casters, a divine intervention and a realm return contend for the same deceased person after inheritance and office succession. Under Wait, branch travel and crash recovery, commit one valid return with paid costs and reconciled body/spirit/custody; retain social consequences and prevent duplicate property, bodies, powers or export rights. |
| **CULT-07 — Cultural city and crafting differences** | Generate and inhabit settlements from two distinct cultural traditions under comparable terrain/resource conditions, plus variants within each tradition. Demonstrate differences in street/courtyard access, gathering, workshop organization and a real craft process, with working supplies, labor, drainage, support and routes. Native play must recognize useful distinctions without excess clutter or confusing essential services; no compulsory species/nation style or facade-only pass. |
| **CULT-08 — Migration and changing affiliations** | Move a craftsperson and household between cultures, change civic or religious affiliation, teach a supported technique and construct/repair a hybrid workshop. Retain individual knowledge/preferences, actual costs, old building layers and material provenance across restart and historical branches. Allegiance changes cannot instantly replace architecture, recipes, language or personality; hybridization must come from recorded learning and work. |

P01–P03 should retain identity, comprehension and life/death transition contracts with small real cases. P08/P09/P10 expand culture, social aftermath, narrative and realm breadth; P11–P14 close content, localization, native performance, long-session acceptance and platform evidence. None of these sources proves Beta's combined design, exact tuning or implementation capacity.
