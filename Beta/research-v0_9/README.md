# Integrated research: historical worlds, living ecology and player experience

This pass supports all **181 current Beta requirements** through nine new dossiers totaling **19,148 words**. It extends the [25-topic preceding map](../research-v0_8/RESEARCH_COVERAGE.json) to **35 topics**, preserving its original coverage while adding geology, underwater/underground/ice, fluids, ecology, adaptive audio, procedural teaching, cultural material/language/aging/afterlife, two local-code investigations and a focused Reddit/constraint synthesis. Read it with the [current specification](../spec-v1_1/README.md), [requirement register](../spec-v1_1/REQUIREMENTS.json) and [research coverage ledger](RESEARCH_COVERAGE.json).

Beta is the successor. Alpha OpenFantasy remains its preserved content and architectural lineage; historical filenames, payloads and requirement text are not renamed. Research supports selective algorithm and content reuse inside a stronger shared authority, rather than merging incompatible runtime ownership models.

## What the expanded evidence changes

Historical geography must be an ordinary persistent domain. Stable substrate, formation, reservoir and site identities survive erosion, deformation and changed coordinates. Geological process intervals and ordered events reconstruct exact requested instants; checkpoints accelerate that reconstruction. Mining, construction and intervention modify the lived state and dependent futures. A newer generated heightmap with an old excavation overlay cannot safely reconstruct volcanic infill or a changed river.

A practically unbounded plane needs finite canonical dependency contracts. Shared geological boundaries and finite contributing catchments/outlet witnesses constrain local terrain before it becomes an established fact. Fluids and hazards propagate through bounded causal fronts, revalidating changed dams, enclosures and sources. Wait requires real finite storage/backpressure and admission before harm; neither an infinite pending-water queue nor freezing every connected ocean is an acceptable shortcut. These are chosen design obligations with retained counterexamples, not solved planetary physics claims.

Ecology is more than populated scenery. Water, soil, seed stocks, growth stages, food, care, predation and dispersal participate in shared quantities and identities. Small inherited variations remain within supported trait/body families. Temporary acclimation, learned behavior and magical transformation are distinct. Forest recovery takes appropriate sources and time; stopping a taint source cannot instantly restore dead trees or erase displaced households. Coarse scheduling must preserve the model's consequences when players return or visit history.

Player presentation must keep this complexity usable. Default choices remain clear; character skill unlocks truthful depth, while accessible essential feedback is always available. Procedural teaching binds actual actors, resources, timing and viable routes, then introduces combinations and delegation gradually. Music and sound use allowed listener context, original material, appropriate silence and restrained detail. More simulated systems do not justify more simultaneous prompts, repetitive animations or permanent musical intensity.

Language adds social texture and optional discoveries through actual character comprehension, while localization and essential accessibility stay usable. Biological age follows owned exposure rather than a historical destination date. Ghosts, graves and divine outcomes remain optional cosmological systems with explicit identity, senses, costs and custody; they cannot mint another person or reverse already distributed inheritance. Cultural/species/national backgrounds influence directed relationships through experience and learned norms, without prescribing every personality or allegiance.

The latest cultural scope extends these mechanisms through weapon, armor and tool lineages, magical traditions, practical skills, architecture and fortifications. Civilizational capability is a profile of actual knowledge, materials, institutions and infrastructure across martial, mechanical, alchemical and magical methods. Native sprites, tiles, equipment and actions must express the supported differences. The required species catalog includes humans, elves, dwarves, gnomes, halflings, dragonkind, quicklings, giants, snake people, cat people and lizard people, plus supported subspecies. These are Beta design and production obligations; the cited games do not prove that this complete catalog or its combinations already work.

Generation quality also needs a precommit gate across these layers. Existing progression and local graph research informs bounded deterministic repair, pruning optional instances, rejection and new candidate selection. Mandatory coverage and accepted facts remain protected. A valid local feature cannot be assumed compatible with every ecosystem, historical event or quest; committed worlds cannot be silently rerolled to hide a contradiction.

Local reuse strengthens implementation only when contracts survive extraction. The strongest candidates supply small mechanisms and failure fixtures: plant target invalidation, bounded inheritance, stable identity, contextual guidance, constructive generation and replay checks. They do not establish Beta's full conservation/history/co-op model. The [C#–Rust assessment](../research-v0_8/csharp-rust-core-reassessment.md) remains conditional: added scope expands measured workloads, rather than automatically selecting another language or requiring an IPC architecture.

The current [geological design](../spec-v1_1/GEOLOGICAL_WORLD_DESIGN.md), [ecology/fluid contracts](../spec-v1_1/ECOLOGY_AND_FLUIDS.md), [audio contract](../spec-v1_1/AUDIO_DIRECTION_AND_RUNTIME.md), [player-experience/onboarding contract](../spec-v1_1/PLAYER_EXPERIENCE_AND_ONBOARDING.md), [language/afterlife contract](../spec-v1_1/LANGUAGE_AND_AFTERLIFE.md) and [generation validation](../spec-v1_1/GENERATION_VALIDATION.md) carry these inferences into explicit design. The [implementation plan](../spec-v1_1/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) retains real P01–P03 proofs before broad expansion, later domain/history breadth, full P12 qualification, P13 desktop production and P14 mobile support. The [implementation handoff](../spec-v1_1/IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md) supplies the current checklist and continuation instructions; research completion does not mark its implementation checks passed.

No new model A/B benchmark, installed-tool promise or universal reference-game superiority claim follows from this index.
