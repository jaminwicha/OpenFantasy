# Cross-system integration and combination atlas

This contract accompanies the [GDD](GDD.md), [technical design](TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) and [presentation contracts](PRESENTATION_CONTRACTS.md). It turns RQ-149 into observable chains of causes and choices. These are required design fixtures, not reports of implemented scenes. The [research coverage](../research-v0_8/RESEARCH_COVERAGE.json) identifies the underlying lessons and their limits.

## Shared foundations

Every consequential combination uses the same identity, custody, action validation, scoped knowledge, clocks, materials, locations, versions and durable transactions as its constituent systems. A quest, cinematic or strategic view cannot fabricate an alternative economy, relationship or movement rule. Designers may author a situation's structure and voice; the real world supplies participants, opportunities, costs and outcomes.

An integration record names its prerequisites, actors, resources, institutions, spatial/temporal/planar scope, committed causes, observer projections, available responses, presentation assets, narrative continuation and failure behavior. Each record links its requirement IDs and production phases. This is additional evidence for those requirements, not a replacement list that shrinks their scope.

## Combinations without an impossible Cartesian test promise

Define capability compatibility at the typed boundaries: actor anatomy versus action/attachment, material versus process/effect, action versus clock/realm, control versus custody, evidence versus observer, and definition versus version. Illegal compositions receive a clear reason before a transaction mutates state. Legal compositions still require semantic checks; type safety does not establish balance, usefulness or good animation.

The shipping catalog declares all supported body, action, material, item, effect and location families. A shared family implementation can cover many combinations when a validator establishes its assumptions and representative native examples are accepted. New family behavior invalidates only the related coverage claims. A generic placeholder cannot permanently stand in for a named imported creature or required profession.

Prioritize pair coverage across every declared boundary and three-way cases at the most dangerous intersections: time × custody × co-op; terrain × vehicles × Wait; ancestry × death × office authority; magic × process loops × economy; dreams × belief × investigation; anatomy × equipment × choreography; and progression × schedules × destructive events. This is a risk-based sampling plan, not exhaustive coverage of every permutation. Retain adversarial seeds and minimized failures so later changes exercise the exact discovered interaction.

## Vertical causal observability

A political event is not complete when a faction value changes. Verify its propagation through lawful orders, reachable communication, local perceptions, feasible decisions, inventory/work changes, dialogue and visible performance. Each transition should be inspectable in developer traces with cause and principal, while the normal player interface shows only justified local information. Measure latency and queue age so distant people do not accumulate unbounded unprocessed obligations.

Established relationships are sparse directed edges with explicit unknown/default interpretation, plus institutional membership and kinship. Twenty thousand residents do not require an allocated matrix of nearly 400 million directed pairs. Sparsity preserves actual relations and individual depth; it does not permit deleting memories when a character moves away. Qualification needs meaningful work, visits, interactions and news propagation, not merely a crowded idle rendering benchmark.

## Completion evidence

Each SYN fixture retains a seed or authored initial state, inputs, expected causal ledger, observed result, native captures, knowledge projections, interruption/restart variants and critic disposition. First build the narrow actual chain, then expand the participating catalog. Full-depth phases in the table complement the authoritative requirement completion phases in [COVERAGE.json](COVERAGE.json); any conflict must be reconciled before that phase closes.

An integration passes only when it creates a legible choice and correct persistent consequences through the real modules. Art, audio, writing and UI are part of this evidence. Human review judges whether discovery feels natural and whether the simulation enriches the experience without turning every action into administration.
