## Expanded contract review

**Spatial and material authority.** TDD §7 specifies real sparse 3D cells, signed coordinates, integer shape/clearance geometry and a realm dimension separate from elevation. Mining and tunneling conserve actual deposits and spoil; construction modifies occupancy, support and fluid dependencies. Deterministic inflow allocation reserves both source volume and destination capacity, preventing competing proposals from overfilling a receiver. Cross-chunk invalidation holds dependent authoritative queries until their required versions are ready. Rendering cannot fabricate a floor or erase an unloaded hazard. Rule-based bearing/span behavior is deliberately bounded; no universal physics solver is promised.

**Lives and institutions.** TDD §8 preserves full applicable actor state, bounded belief-driven planning, directed relationships, preferences and commitments. Growth, pregnancy/incubation and aging use applicable exposure; dates remain historical facts even when biological age diverges. Death commits control, work, body/estate custody and office consequences once. Continuing institutional mandates have explicit survival rules; resurrection does not reverse inheritance or duplicate possessions. Building ownership, political claims, recognition and effective authority remain distinct. Injury changes senses, grip, equipment and action eligibility, while valid declared restoration reliably restores the supported function. These contracts permit the requested breadth without equating every NPC with a fully deliberating actor on every frame.

**Exact history and dreams.** Preparation pins the requested instant, accepted head revision and optional same-instant commit cursor. An A→B→A change between checkpoints cannot be reconstructed merely from the final A; ordered lived history and compatible interpretation must survive compaction. Lazy ancient refinement is constrained by established identities/facts and must be request-order independent. It is generated history, not a claim that eons previously ran through detailed simulation. Dream tethers share real identity/resources and relevant body dependencies, preserve subjective evidence and exit safely under interruption. The material correction closes a specific loophole; arbitrary-history feasibility is still the largest foundational proof obligation.

**Knowledge, progression and production.** The GDD/TDD distinguish learning a rule from learning its unknown inputs. Novice and expert views share authority, preserve essential known hazards and can remain concise. Composite challenge assessments include capability, knowledge, materials, party, time and realm access; a scalar tier cannot establish a feasible chain. The final pain-point decision openly chooses viable supported starts and accountable later consequences. Ingredient selection protects visible equipment/favorite/heirloom/reservation preferences; TDD §8 explicitly prevents an override from bypassing custody, equipped-use eligibility or another job's reservation. No hidden quest flag is exposed as an inventory-protection oracle.

**Presentation and cross-system play.** The final GDD and validation register require separate pixel-finish and animation/interactivity acceptance. The presentation matrix spans actual states, observations, native actions and narrative consequences, including stacked interiors, work, life stages, injury adaptation and temporal provenance. Its family manifest does not claim that one generic motion fits every anatomy. The sixteen SYN chains bind politics, supply, domestic work, environmental change, mysteries, dreams, travel and progression to shared facts. Pair/triple coverage is explicitly risk-based and can be invalidated by a new family behavior. Neither matrix claims exhaustive permutation testing or a completed native scene.

**Language and ecosystem decisions.** D-02/Q-07 and TDD §1 keep C# selected with LANG-01–04 before broad P04. A whole Rust authority behind coarse in-process C ABI operations is a serious alternative without IPC; it still requires export, recovery, ownership and parity evidence. The language dossier correctly separates core GC from C# client allocation, data-layout/scheduling issues from language issues, and archival growth from execution language. It avoids speculative duplicate cores. The ecosystem dossier and inspected operating/readiness sources support interchangeable Codex/Claude leads, separate artists, bounded maintenance and selective skill loading. Neither documentation nor an installation proves improved model performance. Typed packages, historical version retention and recorded optional-AI outcomes remain real product obligations, with explicit unrun ECO cases.

The final validation register now names all expanded families, including arbitrary historical instants, stacked terrain, lifecycle/authority, composite progression, lasting injury, native presentation and SYN integration. It retains TERR/SOC/PX/SK/PLACE/LANG/ECO, earlier suites and critic cases without claiming they ran. The 25-topic research map records applications and evidence limits; mapped research is not proof that any reference game implemented Beta's entire union.

No production date, hardware floor, city capacity, campaign enjoyment or asset throughput has been established by this review.

## Adversarial reasoning retained for implementation

**Shared meeting:** a player and herbalist promise to meet after a duration; the player enters a long held encounter while the herbalist works elsewhere. Under the corrected default, a shared appointment has one world instant and can be missed. An explicitly negotiated duration alternative has exactly one clock anchor and one visible deadline. Repeat with both actors held separately, repeated encounter entry/exit and a recurring meeting. Check notices and feasibility on execution; do not merely compare stored timestamps. Public elections/festivals remain fixed-world events.

**Attached transit:** a carriage carries two actors, cargo, a familiar and a damaged axle under repair; a mount tows it. One actor refuses departure. The corrected group contract rejects the transition before departure until explicit legal detach/disembark actions create a permitted group. Repeat with destination collision, borrowed cargo, enchantment, resource reservations, disconnection and retry. A transaction is useful only after membership and permission are known. The revision supplies that rule; retained fault tests must prove it.

**No-consensus rest:** a separated player needs recovery, has no authorized consenting companion and cannot obtain shared acceleration. Normal-time sleep now truthfully exposes a possible idle interval. Short respite or deferred sleep must remain meaningful choices under the fatigue/resource tuning; merely listing them does not prove they are viable. Compare a lone expedition, a coordinated camp and split-party travel. Track actual inactive duration, forced stops, recovery pressure and player understanding. The solution must preserve the shared time-consent contract and partial recovery rather than hiding a reset exploit behind a menu.

**Large coupled encounter:** an occupied fleet or crowded workshop exceeds the initial actor envelope. The final technical design §6 distinguishes deterministic sequential evaluation of the complete dependency set from unsupported dependencies. Shared pause cannot by itself solve computational capacity or missing rule support. The text now specifies complete processing with persisted continuation and no partial logical commit; unsupported dependencies receive a recoverable diagnostic pause. This related correction was independently checked for integration, without claiming that its capacity target has been measured.

## Sound decisions and remaining qualification

The documents consistently choose one deterministic authority with Godot, network, storage and optional AI adapters. Bounded algorithms, explicit arithmetic/order and recorded external choices prevent presentation or thread completion from becoming hidden authority. Player/NPC parity is a state and action contract, and known individuals remain durable across reduced simulation frequency. Typed processes/effects connect crafting, research, maintenance, transport and lingering magic through shared conservation rules.

Wait timing is described honestly as local stasis in independently exploring co-op. Whole-vessel coupling, revalidated outside admission, retained costs and explicit release conditions address exploitable cross-clock influence. Outside opportunities can still be missed and independent dependants still need care. These are declared gameplay consequences, not a promise of physically uniform chronology. Long holds, disconnected controllers and concurrent encounters require executable evidence.

The GDD retains complete original content, all specified transport/mount families, deep relationships and politics, multiple grand narrative possibilities, planar/temporal play and viable ordinary careers. It allows player choice and autonomous world outcomes while requiring source-faithful legacy migration fixtures. The native art family matrix recognizes body/action/equipment incompatibilities, expressive choreography, changing environmental conditions and restrained variation. Accepted still-image directions and archives remain distinct from unproven native animation production.

The validation register correctly distinguishes research, specification analysis, pure-core execution, engine/export testing, actual virtual playtests and human acceptance. It requires a finite production catalog, full non-mobile closure before desktop release, a separate supported mobile phase, executed virtual observations and human art/fun judgment. Central coordination and durable task/evidence records allow interchangeable Codex/Claude ownership without making a vendor pipeline or maximal ecosystem usage compulsory.

The remaining uncertainty belongs to named qualification work:

- **Toolchain and capacity:** execute the exact Godot/.NET pair on Windows/macOS/Linux; establish device floors, actor/branch/frontier budgets, storage growth and four-separated-player workloads. Candidate numbers are not measured limits.
- **Clock and recovery behavior:** test retries/crashes, mixed-clock obligations, transit groups, long Wait, normal-time sleep and consented acceleration through retained real modules before dependent expansion.
- **Native art and production cost:** obtain user acceptance of imported motion/body/attachment kits and measure a second kit/region before extrapolating complete content cost. Attractive examples cannot establish combinatorial coverage.
- **Pace and coherence:** test the roughly 40-minute full cycle, camp frequency, solitary rest, meaningful frontier variation, ordinary careers and intersecting grand narratives through actual sessions. Revise failures; deterministic correctness alone cannot establish enjoyment.
- **Complete scope and migration:** reconcile every source denominator and shipping definition with reachable, integrated, reviewed behavior. A stored archive, dormant type or empty system menu cannot pass completion.

These gates may reveal necessary revisions. Their existence is not a missing rule disguised as certainty, and they do not justify planning a disposable prototype or another speculative engine before learning from retained implementation.

This is not certification of runtime correctness, complete production readiness or zero future redesign.
