# Evolving geography and geological history

## Scope and correction to specification 1.0

The prior GDD named geological prehistory, coherent drainage, persistent terrain edits and arbitrary historical access. It did not specify how continents, mountain chains, drainage systems, glaciers, coastlines and underground regions evolve together. This is a material design gap: a generated height map plus a history table cannot satisfy historical visits to a city before and after it sinks.

Revision 1.1 makes **geography a historical simulation domain**. Every supported epoch has coherent terrain, water, climate, ecological opportunity and human occupation. Slow evolution and sudden events use the same persistent world, material provenance, actors, structures, clocks and branch rules as construction and mining. The simulation is a controlled, physically inspired game model with defined fidelity; it does not promise a scientific Earth-system solver.

The supporting studies distinguish actual [reference-game and terrain-generation evidence](../research-v0_9/geological-world-generation.md) from [cave, underwater and glacial lessons](../research-v0_9/submerged-subterranean-and-glacial-worlds.md). Neither Dwarf Fortress nor Terraria is cited as proving this entire historical union.

## A world generated through time

The dependency order is cosmological/world profile → crust and relief history → climate/ice/sea-level conditions → drainage and sediment → soils/habitats → viable settlement and route histories → local terrain and inhabited places. It is an interacting schedule, not a one-time pipeline: uplift changes drainage, ice alters water storage and land exposure, erosion transports sediment, and settlements modify channels and slopes. Later passes cannot silently invalidate an earlier published fact.

Use three time scales with one chronology:

| Scale | Processes and evidence | Player experience |
|---|---|---|
| Geological eras | Continental drift, rifting/collision, uplift/subsidence, volcanic provinces, mountain denudation, ice-age envelopes and major marine changes | Visit recognizably related landscapes separated by ages; find mineral belts, extinct habitats, buried ruins or an ancient coastline inland |
| Historical decades/centuries | Channel migration, delta growth, shoreline change, local subsidence, glacial advance/retreat, erosion, silted harbors and changed access | Follow altered trade, abandoned towns, migration, flood defenses, sacred sites and historical claims |
| Lived events and work | Quakes, eruptions, landslides, floods, breaches, cave exposure/collapse, drainage works and excavation | Observe warnings where knowable, react to an event, rescue or relocate, explore a new entrance and live with persistent aftermath |

Rates are versioned world-profile parameters. Natural continental motion should not turn an ordinary 40-minute day into a catastrophic geological age. Rare catastrophes have geography, causes and frequency profiles; they are not periodic rolls to create mandatory excitement. Long-horizon change becomes visible through history travel and continued worlds. Explicit magic or a world-specific cosmology may accelerate a process through declared rules, with its provenance retained.

## Macro geography without false planetary guarantees

The current world is a practically unbounded addressable planar frontier. It is not simultaneously a finite spherical planet with globally solved tectonics. Generate hierarchical geological provinces and crust parcels with shared boundary histories, motion/deformation fields and valid neighboring transitions. This supplies continent-like landmasses, old belts, rifts, basins and volcanic regions while retaining seamless frontier continuation. A finite wrapped/spherical profile would be a separate future topology requiring qualification; it is not silently selected here.

Before local realization, establish the minimum complete macro dependency set: crust/boundary history, relief constraints, contributing catchments, outlets, coast connection and regional climate/water budgets. Each contributing drainage basin is finite and resolved through its declared sink or downstream macro contract. Preparation requires a finite outlet/boundary witness and a bounded acyclic dependency ordering. An explicit finite strongly connected reservoir component can be solved together; an endless chain of downstream promises or an accidental A→B→A generation recursion is rejected before dependent facts publish. Local tidal or groundwater cycles remain legal flow behavior, distinct from recursive generation. A named external boundary exchange closes preparation with a declared budget rather than inventing or discarding water. Basins and provinces can extend beyond loaded chunks; residency does not end a river. Very large basins prepare progressively. Bound search/retry work and retain a deterministic failure/fallback decision before publishing anything that depends on it.

Adjacent macro regions derive shared boundaries from canonical addresses and common ancestors, never whichever side happens to generate first. Upstream discovery cannot retroactively alter a committed downstream town, channel or flood event. Once a port, ancestor, shipment or witnessed horizon depends on geography, its relevant macro constraints become established facts. Unseen detail may refine them but may not reverse them. A later real event can change the river and its route, retaining the earlier river state in history.

This architecture favors tectonic and drainage consistency over unconstrained noise. Local noise can vary rock faces and microrelief only within committed relief, strata and flow constraints. Every supported generation profile has an exact version and deterministic bounded selection rules; seed equality alone cannot survive a generator revision.

## Durable geographical identities

Coordinates identify a place in a realm at an instant. They do not identify a mountain, a crust parcel, a river, a city or a mineral source. Distinguish:

- Stable crust/substrate parcels, formations and material reservoirs, with pose/deformation history.
- Features such as landmasses, mountain chains, volcanoes, glaciers, drainage basins and connected cave regions, with birth, change, split/merge and retirement lineage.
- Site/building identities and their attachments to substrate or mobile structures.
- Local cells, region material balances, water stores and their revisioned realization.

A drifting continent moves and deforms substrate; a city attached to that substrate follows the applicable support/deformation rules until a recorded rupture, collapse or other event changes them. Foundation-bound structures, freely moving actors, floating vehicles and loose contents have distinct attachment/motion rules. A local deformation resolves clearance, load and displacement: it cannot rigidly translate everything nearby or silently bury an occupant. It is not left at an old coordinate while a replacement city is generated on the moving land. A fixed-coordinate query and a historical feature/site query therefore have different meanings. The timeline/map interface supports both and explains a changed or unavailable arrival location.

River identity can continue through a reroute while individual reaches split, disappear or become abandoned channels. A cave connection created by a quake joins existing topology; it does not replace the entire underdark with a new dungeon seed. A mountain chain's erosion can leave lower remnants and transported deposits with geological lineage. A drowned city's surviving buildings, submerged roads, goods, dead, refugees and descendants remain consequences of that city.

The first delivery uses canonical integer/rational transforms and deterministic deformation jobs, not unconstrained floating-point rigid bodies for entire continents. When local shape detail is needed, realize it from the same substrate/deformation revision, then apply ordered subsequent excavation, construction and event changes. Changes must rebase or invalidate dependent geometry explicitly; an immutable old height field plus an unrelated new one is insufficient.

## Process contracts

| Process | Required state and causal changes | Integration obligations |
|---|---|---|
| Drift, rifting and collision | Crust parcels, boundary relations, motion/rate intervals, strain/uplift/subsidence fields and changed formations | Land/sea and drainage recompute from committed changes; retain site/substrate anchors and mineral lineage |
| Mountains and long erosion | Relief, rock properties, exposed area, declared weathering/transport rules and sediment stores | Old chains can become foothills/plains; transported material deposits downstream instead of disappearing or minting fresh ore |
| Volcanism | Magma/heat source, conduit/chamber, pressure/trigger profile, extrusion/ash and cooling transformations | New relief/islands, buried settlements, altered soils and heat/air/water hazards; source-budgeted new material and minerals |
| Earthquakes and slope failure | Fault/event identity, bounded displacement/damage field, affected support and fluid dependencies | Reveal or close entrances, split/connect cave passages, damage infrastructure and change springs; not every quake changes every cave |
| Rivers, avulsion and flood | Catchment water/sediment balance, channel network, bank/bed material, discharge/storage, breach and overflow conditions | River can migrate, abandon a reach, build a delta or cut a new route; floods erode/deposit and affect farms, goods, bridges, caves and routes |
| Coast, subsidence and marine change | Relative land elevation and regional sea-surface level, connected water bodies, sills/barriers and shoreline state | A low cell floods only through a supported connection/inflow. Subsidence, changing sea level, surge and tide are distinct causes with different durations |
| Glacial cycles | Climate forcing intervals, accumulated ice mass, movement/erosion/deposition, meltwater and ice-dammed lakes | Advance/retreat, glacial valleys, deposits, altered drainage and outburst floods; retreat preserves the changed landscape rather than undoing history |
| Cave and underdark development | Stratigraphy, fractures, void connectivity, dissolution/erosion rules, groundwater and geothermal contexts | Persistent networks and entrances, flooded/dry reaches, resource/habitat changes and inhabited subterranean regions |

Processes are modular approximations with measurable inputs and outputs. They must interact through ordinary topology, material, heat, exposure and actor contracts. No promise of full seismic waves, continuum stress, atmospheric circulation or planet-wide computational fluid dynamics is implied.

## Water, sediment, ice and source accounting

Distinguish surface water volumes, porous/fractured aquifer storage, traversable cave voids, snow/ice and atmospheric/regional water stores. Wet rock does not imply a passage full of navigable liquid. A lake can be perched above a connected lower cave only if the intervening material/flow rules support that state. Groundwater pressure and permeability are simplified declared properties; mining and quakes can change their connections.

Detailed cells and coarse reservoirs share one ledger and one owned interval. Cross-scale exchange records water, sediment, heat and source/provenance transfers at named boundaries. Refining an unloaded reservoir into visible cells removes those quantities from its coarse allocation; demotion merges the actual resulting state. Incoming flow cannot spend volume or capacity twice. Snow accumulation, freezing, melting and evaporation are named transformations, with sources/sinks declared by the profile. A renderer's ocean plane cannot supply unlimited usable fresh water or delete displaced water.

Erosion, sediment deposition, glacial transport and lava cooling preserve source lineage. Transforming or moving a mineral-bearing parcel cannot reroll its ore or create new cross-branch export rights. Initial geological reservoirs derive stable world/version/source identities. New volcanic material draws from an explicit geological source budget with deterministic origin intervals; weathering an existing lot preserves its claims. Use checked aggregate arithmetic and partitioned reservoirs where planetary-scale totals exceed a single quantity field's supported range. Claims use stable source quantity intervals with deterministic partition/apportionment and shared immutable provenance nodes; mixing does not require tracking every grain. Lossless range coalescing may compress representation but cannot erase source shares or round them into new export rights. Qualify fragmentation and query cost under repeated mixing, splitting and processing.

Regional climate/sea-level forcing can be analytically specified for uninstantiated background, but an affected realized watershed exchanges through explicit budgets and boundary conditions. Realized changes must reconcile with those conditions; an ocean level, rainfall curve and glacier melt cannot independently debit or credit the same water. Where exact global physical closure is outside the chosen profile, label and budget external boundary exchanges rather than claiming a closed scientific planet.

## Historical reconstruction and branching

Extend the existing `HistoryService` contract with geographical feature queries and the geology package/version. Generated ancient evolution uses deterministic keyframes, event intervals and stable refinement. Lived geography retains actual mutations and external decisions. A visit may request any canonical historical instant; checkpoints and geological eras remain accelerators, not the only permitted destinations.

Continuous evolution uses canonical integration steps or explicitly defined piecewise functions with retained remainders and threshold events. Querying half an interval must not change the numerical integration schedule or event draws. Large jumps replay the same defined evolution, not one giant step that bypasses a flood or cave collapse. Partial-instant queries reconstruct the specified intermediate geometry/quantities. Nonlinear processes use their declared solver schedule independent of request size and worker count.

A historical query reconstructs terrain, channels, groundwater, ice, site support, occupancy, infrastructure and the applicable local-clock suspension state together. Loading a new mountain texture while keeping the old river, routes and city foundations is invalid. Immutable prepared references pin branch head and same-instant commit boundary as already required by the technical design.

After intervention, replay only unaffected exogenous forcing. A dam, excavation, diverted river or magical eruption changes downstream dependencies: invalidate/recompute future endogenous flood, sediment, settlement and route events in the child. Do not blindly replay a parent flood through a dam built on that branch, or erase the parent event from other histories. Retain causal ancestry and explanation of divergence. Precise rewriting of an unbounded autonomous future remains outside the guarantee; future simulation follows the branch's new state.

## Sudden events, actors and Wait

A regional event has an immutable cause ID, accepted time, affected/dependency regions, deterministic stages, material/heat budgets and status. First atomically record the immutable cause/exogenous forcing, source budgets and initial deterministic stage plan. Uncommitted propagation is revisable: before each live stage commits, revalidate current topology, source availability, reservations, occupancy and relevant revisions. A player closing a sluice or an NPC breaching a dam recomputes or cancels stale downstream fronts, releasing or transferring reservations exactly once. Do not preserve predicted inundation or casualties merely to honor an old schedule. Preparation resolves each imminent stage's complete required dependency set against a pinned revision before that stage publishes. Advance finite causal fronts with declared arrival times and boundary fluxes; connected water alone does not place an entire ocean in one immediate dependency closure. Stages commit ordered consistent patches with durable continuation, not a partially corrupted world if the host crashes. Worker completion speed cannot decide which town floods first; if due authoritative work is late, hold its dependent progress. Independent unaffected work may continue; affected queries wait for required revisions. An unsupported dependency produces a recoverable hold, never silently skipped terrain.

Quakes and floods do not end at camera borders. The event can connect caves, undermine a road, displace cargo, contaminate a supply, force migration and change politics. Those outcomes use existing actor/custody/structure rules. Nearby people react through sensed hazards or received evidence; distant NPCs do not instantly know a city's fate. Warnings reflect knowable observations and skill, not guaranteed prediction of every natural event.

Consequential terrain motion, water, ice and collapse must join encounter timing admission **before** affecting held actors, their supports, vehicles or atmosphere. No separate geology clock can move their floor or drown them during Wait. Boundary volumes/materials remain reserved while admission is pending; they cannot be spent elsewhere. Pending environmental flow stays in an identified physical source cell/reservoir/buffer with finite capacity; a reservation is not storage. Reserve real source quantity and available receiving capacity before debit. If a held inlet cannot receive more, propagate legal backpressure/overflow or widen the dependency hold/shared pause; never create an invisible infinite queue of water or remove the hazard. This physical environmental accounting does not let an outsider action intent reserve arbitrary unrelated resources during Wait. If the full dependency closure exceeds the active envelope, use the established shared-pause and deterministic sequential processing policy. A continent-scale change is not forced into one unbounded frame or truncated to visible cells. Persist holds as actual lived state, so later historical queries reproduce the accepted stasis policy rather than a fictitious uniform clock.

## Underwater and underdark places

Underwater geography includes coasts, continental-shelf analogues, deep basins/trenches, reefs, flooded caves and inhabited or ruined sites. Bathymetry and water connectivity matter. Travel and interaction use declared swimming/diving, breath, pressure-tolerance, visibility, current, temperature and equipment/magic capabilities. Depth is real elevation; an underwater plane is not a substitute for submerged physical space. Existing boats, sealed assemblies and mounts need explicit supported capabilities before entering incompatible conditions.

Support underwater communities and visitors through actual air or water-breathing needs, resources, work, shelter, transport and institutions. An air-filled chamber requires a valid seal/supply and flood behavior. Standard profile depth bands and readable hazard rules are preferable to adding unrequested decompression micromanagement. Underwater combat uses the same on-field Wait authority and legal positions, with appropriate motion, targeting and cues.

Underdark regions are large connected or separated subterranean environments across many elevations, including shafts, caves, aquifers, underground rivers/lakes, geothermal regions, resources and settlements. Surface entrances are sparse and mutable. Food/energy, ventilation or species-appropriate alternatives, waste, light/senses and travel logistics must support inhabitants. Fungi, imported organic matter, geothermal ecologies or explicit magical sources cannot be decorative excuses for an otherwise impossible city. Connection to another plane remains a separate supported portal relation.

Use Jaquays-style loops, multiple entries and histories where they create meaningful choices, without forcing every underground component to be connected or walkable. A quake may expose a new leg, create a dangerous shortcut, close a known path or change only the water. New access triggers appropriate discovery and challenge assessment; it does not automatically spawn a balanced treasure room. Known routes and quest witnesses revalidate after change, with truthful recovery, alternate leads or failure where the world has removed the possibility. The supported original-content start constrains geological history so required legacy places and routes exist in their declared realization; later lawful world changes can alter them without erasing the import obligation.

## Society, borders and historical claims

A flooded or uplifted district changes routes, habitation and effective control through actual events; it does not erase property or automatically create sovereignty. Claims distinguish fixed surveyed boundaries from feature-following borders and include the rule for a river that moves, splits or disappears. Titles, estates, resource rights, recognition and present possession remain separate historical records. Displaced households, interrupted work, care, death and succession use the existing actor/institution contracts, and knowledge of a disaster spreads through real observations and communication.

## Native presentation and a retained comparison scene

Art expresses cause and age selectively: tilted or offset masonry, exposed strata, old river terraces, water marks, marine growth on a drowned street, glacial scoring/deposits, ash over a familiar roof and fresh versus established cave breaks. Appropriate knowledge can reveal further geological interpretation. Essential water, air, unstable-ground and heat hazards remain readable in simple views. Actual underwater movement, work, gestures, current response and confined-space actions need accepted native clips, sound and interaction feedback.

Retain one coastal river city and its inland headwaters across five linked states: older upland relief and initial channel; inhabited port; subsidence/flood event with a changed cave connection; drowned lower district and displaced households; and a glacial interval/retreat whose deposits and drainage differ. These are the same named places/materials across chronology, not five unrelated seeds. An intervention branch builds floodworks and changes a downstream outcome. Reference art may depict these states, but acceptance requires actual native geometry, actions and history.

## Phasing and acceptance

P00 defines the geology/feature/strata/basin/forcing/event schemas and finite dependency profiles. P01 implements a retained small macro-to-local generator and historical query contracts. P02 shows actual relief, river, strata and cave geography around an original-content location. P03 must prove exact-date reconstruction, branch interventions, source conservation and one staged quake/flood spanning a held encounter and a chunk boundary before broad systems expand.

P04 adds coupled erosion, sediment, groundwater and local ecological work. P06 qualifies changing coasts/frontier travel and underwater access. P07 expands subterranean/underwater settlements and infrastructure. P08 integrates migration, disaster recovery and political consequences. P09 expands historical adventurelets and discovery. P10 completes continental/volcanic/glacial and geographical-history breadth across the supported chronology and realms. P11 preserves the entire legacy catalog; P12/P13 qualify variety, disaster pacing, production assets, endurance and desktop operation; P14 retains the mobile obligation.

A small early geological history is real retained machinery, not a hardcoded montage. Later breadth requalifies its workload. Canonical histories, river/cave boundary continuity, mineral/water provenance, branch causality and native usability are required evidence; a visually attractive height map does not close them. No geodynamic, climate, underwater or historical runtime test has been executed for this revision.
