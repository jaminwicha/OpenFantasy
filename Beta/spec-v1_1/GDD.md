# Beta game design specification

## 1. Baseline and product promise

This version 1.1 specification consolidates all 181 requirements and their refinements into the full production design baseline. It is a specification version, not a claim that the game is implemented. [Requirements](REQUIREMENTS.json) retain every original requirement and its revisions; [coverage](COVERAGE.json) maps them through the full implementation plan. The technical design owns execution contracts, this document owns intended player behavior, and [decisions](DECISIONS.md) records defaults and qualification gates. Corrections create a new edition or tracked erratum; previous editions remain archived.

The project and successor game code name is **Beta**. It replaces **Alpha OpenFantasy**, whose content, source identities and historical documents remain preserved. This edition supersedes the authority of [specification 1.0](../spec-v1_0/README.md), which remains unchanged and archived.

Build an original, richly simulated fantasy world that can be lived in as well as adventured through. Classic JRPG readability, expressive characters and memorable journeys coexist with crafting, discovery, domestic life, institutions and long history. The world should offer Dwarf Fortress-like meaningful variation, Terraria-like opportunities to make and explore, and the warmth and legibility associated with the accepted Chrono Trigger/Sea of Stars/Stardew-inspired art direction.

The player is a person in that world, not the only actor capable of changing it. Every individual NPC has the same applicable depth of character model, progression, actions, possessions, relationships and history. Personal circumstances, knowledge, anatomy, learning and control differ. Major events can arise and be resolved without the player. A farmer, merchant, craftsperson or scholar has a legitimate game, while a heroic adventurer can pursue a complete satisfying path without mastering every profession.

The first production release supports Windows, macOS and Linux, solo play and one to four simultaneous players per hosted world. Mobile support follows in a named later production phase using the retained Godot/C# implementation. The authoritative simulation is seeded, deterministic and portable beyond Godot. Wider online/community/MMORPG layers remain architectural extension directions; a global MMO is not an implicit first-release commitment.

The intended substantial adventure remains a 60–80-hour quality target with optional chapters and further continuation. This is not a staffing estimate, a mandatory main quest or permission to pad travel. Roughly twice that horizon remains a stretch opportunity. Persistent society, new lives and frontier journeys continue beyond an individual's campaign.

## 2. Play loops and progression

Progression is a logical network across skills, knowledge, people, resources, events, places, dates and planes. A road can be physically open while a useful opportunity awaits a festival, tide, contact or learned technique. Majora's Mask is a reference for learning schedules and making informed use of time; Beta has no universal repeating reset or terminal date. Recurring opportunities retain occurrence identity and change when the people or world change. Historical and parallel-plane routes can reveal new options without erasing the consequences of earlier decisions.

Generated challenges carry a difficulty/DC assessment plus explicit prerequisites and context. Rate combat, action puzzles, deduction, social action, travel and production in their relevant dimensions; one number cannot represent all of them. Composite difficulty includes available tools, knowledge, party capabilities, supplies, time windows, repeated costs, failure consequences and alternate solutions. A very hard optional gate may be visible early, even hiding in plain sight, while a suitable local adventure remains available.

World assembly must retain a constructive example of a viable introductory advancement route from each supported start through attainable resources, knowledge and challenges. This route spans events and capabilities, not merely connected tiles. Validate that enabling skills, clues, teachers, items and opportunities can be obtained before the gates requiring them, and that affordable recovery is accessible. Track assumptions and re-evaluate when world events change availability. The player remains free to attempt harder routes, exploit a legitimate shortcut or encounter a dead end above current capability.

The selected guarantee is viable supported starts and truthful ongoing recovery/continuation options, not automatic victory after every destructive decision. A world in which every teacher, supply route and witness can be removed cannot honestly promise every original advancement chain remains completable. The [completed tradeoff review](PAIN_POINTS_AND_RESOLUTIONS.md) compares this boundary with stronger protected-path alternatives. A change to invulnerable progression paths requires an explicit product-rule revision before dependent implementation. Show known danger and suggest plausible next leads without exposing unearned secrets or secretly scaling every place to the party.

A short session should connect a local concern to an outing, discovery, choice and observable consequence. For example, help a farmer prepare a windbreak, find useful material or another solution, recruit assistance, encounter a threat, return and see the result change work and relationships. The first retained slice uses real Alpha OpenFantasy people and content rather than an unrelated demo.

An expedition combines party preparation, route choice, tools/supplies, exploration, combat or another resolution, discoveries and a return or onward destination. A domestic session combines work, learning, care, social interactions, changing demand and meaningful improvements. Institutional play adds delegation, contracts, infrastructure, political choices and consequences witnessed at ground level. All use the same people, inventories, places and rules.

Progression creates new possibilities: access, techniques, relationships, knowledge, responsibilities, construction, transport and influence. Combat numbers alone do not define growth. Practice, teachers, apprenticeship, research, discovery, institutions and historical travel offer overlapping learning routes. Specialization matters, but trade, allies and delegation let players obtain outcomes without personally mastering every graph.

The interface starts with practical intentions and expands into analytical views when useful. Introspection is an in-world trainable capability that can reveal real traits, formulas, provenance and causal traces. Essential accessibility, action costs, fundamental risks and control instructions never depend on having that skill. Knowledge privileges do not reveal another actor's secrets or a future random outcome.

The [introduction design](PLAYER_EXPERIENCE_AND_ONBOARDING.md) makes gradual learning part of procedural world assembly. Bind an authored observe→practice→independent-use→combination sequence to actual people, supplies, season and routes. Verify both feasible advancement and teaching opportunities, then test understanding with players. A lesson is optional guidance until an ordinary task is accepted; simply showing it reserves no mentor or resources. Alternative solutions and changed circumstances are handled truthfully.

Early play foregrounds one practical intention and a few relevant actions in a spacious, readable scene. Deeper causes can operate without generating a notification for every variable. Later play deliberately combines known mechanics: food, transport, construction, affinities, politics, magic, ecology and historical knowledge reinforce one another. **By the end game the player can orchestrate the connected full scope**, personally and through companions, specialists, contracts and institutions. The simple view remains useful; orchestration should produce consequential choices rather than constant acknowledgment of routine work.

## 3. Geography, frontier and historical continuity

Use one canonical world with local, regional and strategic views. Buildings, roads, waterways, dungeons, resources, weather, vehicles and named lives remain the same facts across views. The frontier has practically inexhaustible addressable continuation, with generation and presentation materialized as needed. The design does not promise an infinite quantity of precomputed history or concurrently active actors.

Geography is coherent across boundaries: broad terrain, drainage, climate, ecology, routes, history and occupation constrain local detail. Dense destinations alternate with wilderness that affords discovery, gathering, building and travel decisions. Empty space is not automatically a defect, but long stretches of repeated filler or obligatory loading fights fail the experience goal. Walk, ride, sail, fly and tunnel according to actual capabilities and environment.

Required Aldermere places and content relationships are guaranteed in the supported original-content realization. Their integration with a wider generated world preserves identity and progression rather than hiding legacy content in an inaccessible museum. The faithful original-map realization is retained for migration/fidelity checks. Original quest routes are tested from valid starting conditions; subsequent world actions may truthfully change their availability.

Discovered terrain, construction, excavation, ownership, actor identity and consequential history persist. Loading order cannot redraw a settlement, reroll a known person, replenish cargo or restore a ruined wall. Unseen regional populations may remain abstract, but any instantiated person's recoverable state is durable. Remote activity can instantiate a destination before a player visits it; concurrent requests produce one shared result.

No Man's Sky informs continuous preparation and seamless movement; its pipeline is not copied wholesale. [Frontier research and experiments](../research-v0_7/frontier-streaming.md) define cold traversal, far coordinates, upgrades, tunneling and four separated players. Spatial chunks never become battle boundaries, historical identities or separate fictional clocks.

### Geography evolves through history

The world has continental drift, rifting/collision, mountain uplift and weathering, volcanic activity, erosion/sediment, changing rivers, floods, relative sea-level/subsidence, glaciers and ice-age histories under explicit game-scale rules. Geological eras, historical decades/centuries and lived events share one chronology. Slow processes remain slow during ordinary days; earthquakes, breaches and eruptions have causes and bounded stages rather than mandatory periodic catastrophe rolls.

A mountain, river, crust parcel, deposit and city retain identities as coordinates and surroundings change. Historical travel can reach a port before silting, during flooding and after submergence, preserving its people, structures, claims and artifacts at each requested instant. Mining, dams, defenses and magic change actual dependent outcomes; a previously prepared flood cannot ignore a newly closed sluice. Long processes and lived interventions use the [geological contract](GEOLOGICAL_WORLD_DESIGN.md), not a new heightmap pasted over old saved edits.

The selected practically unbounded frontier is planar with continent-like macro provinces, not a finite spherical planet that also expands forever. Finite shared catchments, outlets and geological constraints precede local detail. This bounds preparation while keeping rivers and history coherent across loaded regions. Preparation can take visible bounded work; unexplored history cannot contradict already established facts. A failed generator must report the problem rather than substitute another requested date or silently reroll a known place.

Underwater and stacked underground/underdark locations are actual environments: flooded neighborhoods, seabed ruins, aquifers, traversable caves, mines, crypts and deep settlements have distinct topology, light, resources, hazards and inhabitants. Aquifer pores are not automatically navigable tunnels. Quakes can connect or close passages; ice or sea changes can expose and bury routes. Boats, diving means, magical protection and different bodies interact through the same access, care, construction and historical rules.

### Validate combinations before publishing them

The [generation validation design](GENERATION_VALIDATION.md) checks incompatible geometry, history, habitats, actor roles, stock, quest prerequisites and badly overlapping attention demands. Hard correctness constraints coexist with soft variety preferences. Repair bounded local conflicts, prune optional uncommitted proposals or reject a candidate and try another permitted seed. Record the actual accepted seed; an explicitly requested failing seed is reported honestly. Required Alpha content, supported-start witnesses and full product scope cannot be pruned away.

Committed world facts—including places realized by remote NPCs—are preserved. Live changes invalidate opportunities and permit ordinary consequences, not generator erasure. Contradictory beliefs, hybrid cultures, unusual individuals and layered ruins can be deliberate valid content when their causes are represented. Validation must remove incoherent combinations without stripping away mystery or every repeated everyday pattern.

## 4. Cosmology, history, species and gods

World generation includes eon-scale prehistory and long civilization histories at bounded resolution. Geological and celestial conditions, magical/crystal/elemental relationships, ecology, species lineages, cultures, institutions, conflicts and discoveries leave linked evidence. Ruins, artifacts, descendants, disputed claims and surviving infrastructure follow from events rather than unrelated random labels.

Species and subspecies may diverge, converge, adapt or transform under explicit historical rules. Ancestry, similar traits, cultural adoption and magical change are distinct causes. Species-associated affinity distributions derive from the world's cosmic history, but do not dictate every individual's powers, ethics or fate. Exceptional births, supported mixed ancestry, training, vows, exposure and deliberate transformation can change an individual's capabilities with recorded reasons.

Culture is not species. A species can include many civilizations and faiths; a culture can include multiple species. Language, craft traditions, clothing, architecture, law, institutions and values evolve. Body and supernatural properties affect practical needs—such as scale, senses and compatible infrastructure—without making a humanoid species automatically hostile or morally uniform.

Gods and deities have identities, domains, powers and limits, relationships, motives, knowledge and intervention histories where the cosmology includes them. Pantheons, worshippers, priesthoods, temples, rites, pacts, boons, curses and avatars connect to actual institutions and effects. A religion's belief about a deity can differ from truth. Worship can change powers, obligations and dispositions through rules; it does not automatically overwrite personality.

Divine interventions and lineage changes use authoritative effects. A new epoch's inheritance rules do not silently rewrite all living people. A power labelled divine does not bypass cost, scope, versioning or determinism. Apotheosis, immortality or divine death are supported only through explicit capabilities. Player and NPC worshippers can learn, negotiate, reject or oppose under the same applicable mechanics.

The additive species catalog covers humans, elves, dwarves, gnomes, halflings, dragonkind, quicklings, giants, snake people, cat people and lizard people, with broad supported subspecies. Each has actual body, scale, senses, locomotion, needs, lifespan and affinity definitions with individual variation. This list supplements all earlier required beings: dragons, medusas, minotaurs, giants, orcs and every Alpha creature remain eligible historical/social actors with their applicable depth. It is not an exhaustive replacement roster. Culture, nationality and species remain distinct. Every shipping body family needs equipment, infrastructure, care, action and native asset coverage; a giant or serpentine person is not simply a recolored or resized human.

Cultural background, perceived ancestry and nationality can shape familiarity, learned expectations and psychological tendencies between individuals. Personal history, observed conduct, loyalties and temperament can reinforce or overcome them. Relations remain directed and multifactorial; an institution's war does not instantly erase every friendship, and secret affiliation is not free planner knowledge.

Languages, dialects and cants add light social and historical depth. Ordinary starting-region play remains understandable through shared speech, context or available help. Learned registers can reveal nuanced dialogue, guild signals, old inscriptions and optional mysteries. Companions, references and supported translation provide practical alternatives. Comprehension neither reveals lies nor grants social permission, and accessible localized UI does not teach the actor a language. Avoid universal garbling, repetitive vocabulary chores and mandatory obscure-language gates in beginner progression.

Cultures shape functional architecture, town/castle layouts, clothing, kept objects, craft processes, equipment and magical/skill traditions. Values, institutions, materials, climate, anatomy and historical events influence how people gather, work, defend, maintain and decorate places. Regional variation, migration, borrowing and individual innovation produce related but distinct settlements and design lineages. Existing buildings and possessions change through actual work, loss and acquisition; affiliation changes alone do not repaint the world.

Civilizational technology is a profile of known and practically available methods across construction, metallurgy, mechanics, logistics, alchemy, martial practice and magic. Knowledge, facilities, teachers, materials and energy matter. A warded castle and a mechanically defended one need actual builders, upkeep, supply and counterplay; magic does not make every mundane method obsolete. Weapon/armor/tool lineages and taught practices carry real production, fit, repair, effect and performance differences. The [cultural material/language/lifecycle contract](LANGUAGE_AND_AFTERLIFE.md) supplies detailed rules and native catalog obligations.

## 5. Actors, classes, skills and powers

The skill catalog spans embodied, practical, intellectual, creative and social disciplines. Fishing, hunting, cooking, knitting, writing, acting and acrobatics are required alongside the previously named combat, magical, crafting, farming, scholarly and political skills. Each supported discipline has real actions, learning routes, appropriate costs/tools, consequences and readable sprite performances. Content packages can extend the catalog; “all imaginable skills” expresses expansive creative intent rather than a finite claim that every conceivable human activity has already been implemented. The production capability inventory must enumerate every named required discipline and its actual uses.

Skills participate in quest and puzzle solutions as well as progression. Knitting can produce or repair suitable textile equipment; writing can compose messages, records or learned works; acting can support performance, impersonation and social approaches under recognition rules; acrobatics can create spatial options. Fishing and hunting connect habitats, equipment, timing, technique, animal behavior and preparation. Activities can combine professions, companions and magic. A skill must change what an actor can do, understand or accomplish, not only add a menu label or generic success bonus.

Every actor has appropriate body/capability data, attributes, derived statistics, health and conditions, needs, skills, learned abilities, equipment, possessions, relationships, knowledge, commitments and history. Sparse/shared representation is permitted; loss of intrinsic depth is not. Major characters may receive additional authored scenes, while ordinary people retain equal systemic opportunity.

Classes and jobs are first-class progression definitions with identity, entry/training requirements, proficiencies, learning routes, resources, specializations and capabilities. Preserve Squire, Mage, Scout and every original ability. Support new coherent paths and explicitly governed class/job combinations. Profession, class, political office, species and current party role are distinct. A mage can farm or serve a guild without creating a second version of the person.

The versioned rules profile defines primary and derived stats, skill checks/opposed actions, defenses/resistance, costs, effect stacking, rounding, learning and recovery. The [technical design](TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) specifies canonical evaluation, and the production content catalog specifies actual class/ability coverage. Tabletop/D&D and Final Fantasy breadth guides capability families; importing every rule from every edition would not create a coherent system.

Skills and powers have combat, exploration, social, production and knowledge applications. Equipment, environments, traits and relationships can affect an action through traceable rules. A player input controller and NPC planner submit the same legal actions. Changing permitted control cannot upgrade the actor, restore resources or grant secret knowledge. Unusual bodies and cognition can restrict capabilities without introducing a shallow NPC-only model.

Injury, fatigue, recovery, death and supported magical restoration have consequences. Worlds may outlive their player characters. Retirement, apprentices, companions, descendants, reincarnation or new adventurers can continue where the chosen rules permit. Succession transfers actual assets, rights and relationships explicitly rather than cloning all power and history.

Injuries can persist beyond ordinary rest, including damaged or lost eyes and limbs. Serious lasting loss is rare; an occasional scar is the more common enduring mark. Anatomy changes affect applicable senses, grip, equipment, movement and actions, with readable adaptation and support rather than an unexplained unusable character. The appropriate restorative spell can heal its declared injury when its explicit requirements are met; no hidden exception makes a supported loss permanently unhealable. Restoring an eye or arm is distinct from resurrection and does not duplicate equipment dropped or transferred after the injury. Scars, treatment and recovery contribute to personal history and restrained sprite variation. Human testing must establish frequency, emotional effect and access to recovery, including whether an injury unfairly blocks an early advancement route.

## 6. Researched magic and crafted effects

Separate school of study, elemental/crystal/divine affinity and effect operation. Illusion and invocation are example specializations with original defined practices; affinity affects aptitude or interaction; an operation describes a consequence. Fire enchantment can combine a school practice, fire facet and object binding. Multi-facet fusion follows declared compatibility and transformation rules.

Spells compose typed prerequisites, sources, operations, targets, delivery/area, duration, triggers, costs and limits. Support direct spells, rituals, summons, weapon/object enhancement, spellbinding, persistent areas and actor/object attachments. A sword retains its actual enchantment when transferred; a recalled familiar does not duplicate its inventory. Effects have identity, provenance, stacking, expiry, dispel and upkeep contracts.

Lingering fire, wards, illusions and other fields participate in environment and encounter timing. An illusion changes supported perceived evidence, not the historical fact underneath it. A flame can consume fuel, spread or be suppressed through declared world rules. Effects crossing an encounter boundary must join its timing/authority rules before causing consequences. Damage cannot continue in real seconds against an actor held in Wait.

Research is gameplay: acquire knowledge, observe or hypothesize, compose a supported experiment, understand known requirements, reserve resources/time/facilities, perform the work and record a result. Findings become validated versioned spell definitions with discoverers, teaching permissions and history. NPCs can research and teach by the same rules. Spell libraries connect schools, libraries, guilds, ruins, lost epochs and personal discoveries.

The system supports deep combinations of implemented primitives. It does not interpret arbitrary prose as permission for an unlimited effect. Validation catches invalid types, unbounded recursion/spawning, missing costs, incompatible facets and accidental infinite-resource loops. Optional AI may propose a composition, but cannot make unsupported magic authoritative.

## 7. Items, materials and interconnected production

The mining-to-industry loop is a complete supported career: survey, excavate and shore a tunnel, extract actual mineral deposits, haul and sort ore, refine/smelt/alloy, then smith weapons, armor, tools, fittings and construction parts. Substance hardness, toughness, tool suitability, worker skill, energy, access and hazards affect the work. Hardness is not a substitute for every material property: a hard substance can still be brittle, heat-sensitive or vulnerable to a particular effect. Ore lots retain composition and provenance through processing; refining and recycling account for fuel, losses and byproducts. Workshops, expeditions, settlements and armies draw from this same supply chain.

Items and materials cover mundane tools, food, weapons, armor, ingredients, relics, parts, trade goods and historically significant possessions. Instances retain quantity/identity where needed, ownership/containment, material/process history, condition and capabilities. Templates and blueprints describe definitions; they are not free instances of goods.

Crafting, herbalism, cooking, alchemy, engineering, enchantment, construction and maintenance use connected typed process graphs. Processes express AND requirements, alternatives, knowledge, worker roles, stations, tools/catalysts, stages, time, energy/environment, output properties, byproducts, quality, interruption and failures. Jobs reserve real lots, labor and capacity and retain work in progress.

Automatic ingredient selection respects durable inventory preferences: equipped, favorited, protected heirloom and explicitly reserved lots are excluded by default. Preview the exact inputs and consequences before commitment, with an explicit permitted-owner override for intentional use. Protection derives from player-visible preferences and custody, never an undisclosed quest flag that leaks an object’s secret importance.

Herbalism includes identification, habitat/season, cultivation, harvest, drying, preservation and medicinal/magical properties. Cooking connects preparation, heat, ingredients, freshness, nutrition, preferences and cultural traditions. Alchemy includes extraction, separation, concentration, solvents/catalysts, reactions, residue and stability. Craft traditions and lost knowledge change possible recipes and substitutions.

Quality may distinguish potency, taste, purity, durability or stability rather than reducing every object to rarity. Outcomes derive from actual inputs, skill and processes. Show practical alternatives and bottlenecks; deeper expertise can inspect formulas. Meaningful risk follows the world's rules, and players can learn from failures.

Production conserves declared resources. Harvest, salvage, trade and explicitly priced supernatural creation are legitimate sources; duplicate accounting is not. Repair/recycling cycles are allowed with designed costs and losses. Validate reachability, dead ends, absent ingredients and unpriced loops. NPC work, machinery and magical automation use the same resources, upkeep and interruption rules as manual work.

## 8. Environment, ecology and domestic life

Day/night, sun, clouds, overcast, wind, storms and regional seasons influence the world and its appearance. Relevant celestial cycles affect tides, navigation, ecology or magic where defined. Weather and exposure alter crops, travel, visibility, materials, creatures and warfare. Cosmetic light and animation do not create a second environmental authority.

Farming, fishing, foraging, husbandry, breeding, cooking and home life participate in ecology and economy. Populations have habitats, resources, migration, reproduction, predation, disease and adaptation at suitable resolution. Removing a predator can affect prey, crops, hunting, demand and subsequent requests. Consequences require accountable rates and bounds, not a cascade of arbitrary event flags.

Plants have species-specific growth, dormancy, flowering, pollination, harvest and regrowth rather than a universal timer. Actual light, moisture, temperature, soil and season affect their supported stages. Seeds spread through permitted wind, water, creatures and caravans and establish only when conditions allow. Named or modified plants retain identity; distant cohorts preserve quantities and lineage. Forest loss, desertification and recovery arise from conditions, dispersal, competition and disturbance, not a biome label repaint.

Creatures inhabit food webs. Animals and monsters can graze, hunt one another, protect young, avoid risk or ignore the player according to hunger, fear, knowledge and circumstances; a monster label never mandates aggression. Feeding, reproduction, migration and death affect real stocks and individuals. Small inherited trait changes and acclimation remain bounded within supported species families, while eon-scale lineage history supplies the broader fantasy menagerie.

Magical taint has an actual source and supported carriers, intensity, resistance and recovery. A once-healthy forest can change through contaminated water/soil, altered growth, displaced creatures and frightened inhabitants. Removing a dark source stops its emissions but does not instantly replace dead trees, repair farms or erase memories. Ecology, historical investigation, craft, politics and magic offer combined responses with readable stages and manageable upkeep. See [ecology and fluids](ECOLOGY_AND_FLUIDS.md).

Food, water, shelter, fatigue, temperature, wetness, illness, equipment and preparation matter without demanding incessant trivial clicks. Delegated care, supply orders and household roles support extended adventures. Offscreen advancement preserves work and care; absence follows the selected host/calendar policy, with legible consequences.

Material history distinguishes harmless age, functional damage and evidence of past events. Wear depends on actual use/exposure; inspection, patching, repair, replacement and restoration have different effects. Maintenance can preserve a cherished old object. The art shows state through controlled detail, recognizable repair and weather response, following the accepted restraint rather than filling every surface with marks.

Condition changes come from both gradual exposure and discrete events: years of damp rot a beam, a storm tears a roof, a battle dents armor, a tunnel collapse fractures masonry, and fire or magic changes surfaces and function. Store what happened and its resulting condition; elapsed-time settlement must not apply an already committed event twice. Repairs restore defined capabilities while preserving appropriate age, replaced-part history and memorable scars. The same logic covers applicable terrain, structures, vehicles, objects and living injuries; it does not impose identical biology on stone or identical corrosion on flesh.

Foraging and farming connect habitat, seasons, soil, water, tending, yield and regeneration with household and regional demand. Herbal teas are actual preparations: identify and harvest plants, preserve them where needed, choose water, heat and steeping/process steps, then consume or serve a result with declared properties, preferences and effects. Hot springs have location, water, heat, access, capacity and environmental or magical qualities. Bathing can provide supported comfort/recovery/social opportunities, but time, safety, water and any supernatural costs remain accounted for. Cultivation, tea, baths and enchantments use shared jobs and effects instead of independent reset bonuses.

### Daily rest and camp pacing

Day/night initially targets approximately **40 minutes for a full cycle**, measured in authoritative exploration progression. This is a tuning hypothesis, not a proven final rhythm. Solo/shared pause does not accumulate wall-time fatigue; local Wait follows the explicit suspension contract. Tune day/night proportions, exertion, fatigue and recovery together so meaningful outings are not interrupted by frequent compulsory camping.

Sleep/rest is a normal daily concern with species-appropriate needs, warning and preparation. Full recovery requires eligible rest intervals; partial recovery can survive interruption. Short respite supports continued activity without repeatedly refreshing full-rest resources. Inns, homes, wilderness camps, caravan rings, ship berths and supported unusual shelters offer different security, comfort, access and opportunities.

Camps provide actual roles and actions: watches, cooking, companion feeding/care, treatment, repair, research, ritual, conversations and other learned skills. Duties consume resources/time and do not simultaneously count as sleep. Party/NPC relationships, injuries, fatigue, trust, visitors, rumors and weather influence what happens. Contextual camp events or combat can interrupt, but quiet uneventful rest must remain common enough to provide respite.

Solo or an agreeing affected group can advance through sleep, processing scheduled world events and stopping at a meaningful interruption. One player cannot silently skip everyone else's time. Without consensus, normal-time rest can continue while the player controls an authorized consenting companion or takes different camp duties; those duties do not count as sleep. This alternative must be tested for long inactive waits and excessive micromanagement, not assumed enjoyable. Restarting a camp does not reset pursuit, supplies, world threats or recovery entitlements.

The [camp/rest research and REST-01–06 scenarios](../research-v0_7/camp-and-rest.md) connect these rules to the production plan. Human playtests must confirm that camping creates worthwhile party, caravan and story choices without becoming a recurring chore.

## 9. Relationships, families, parties and living behavior

NPCs have persistent life courses: birth, age-appropriate development, changing care needs, education/apprenticeship, adult relationships, marriage or partnership, household change, aging and death. Species and cultures define applicable timings and customs; a marriage is not required for every family or every birth. Partnerships reflect the actors' choices under supported social rules. Biological ancestry, adoptive kinship, guardianship, household membership and legal marriage are distinct records. A child has an identity and history from entry into the realized population, rather than becoming a newly invented adult when first encountered.

The 40-minute day target does not imply a human generation passes during one adventure: 60–80 hours of uninterrupted normal exploration corresponds to about 90–120 days before rest acceleration or other clock policies. Seed existing families and varied ages, including plausible near-term life events. Long-lived worlds, species-appropriate life spans and explicitly supported historical travel/advancement provide broader generational play. Do not compress a person's life silently to satisfy a content quota.

Deaths persist as facts with causes, witnesses and knowledge propagation. Survivors can grieve, change plans, continue commitments, adopt dependants, dispute an estate or seek an eligible magical remedy. Property, pets, unfinished jobs, debts, offices, promises and story roles each follow their applicable custody/succession rules. A dead quest giver is not silently replaced by an identical living instance. Resurrection or other supernatural continuation preserves identity and explicitly resolves estate/office changes; it cannot duplicate inherited goods. Player-controlled actors use the same applicable lifecycle rules and supported continuation options.

Relationships exist NPC-to-NPC and player-to-NPC. Affection, trust, respect, fear, grievance, rivalry, loyalty and obligation are directed and contextual. Kinship, households, employment, cultural ties, factions and offices are explicit relations. An institution's reputation does not replace personal feeling; a gift cannot be a universal friendship engine.

People perceive evidence, form beliefs, consider needs and commitments, select feasible actions, and remember consequences. Immediate perceived threats can interrupt activity; routine and long-term planning are scheduled. They do not learn secret world facts from an omniscient broadcast. Rumors and messages have sources, reliability, travel and audiences.

Desires, motivations, likes and dislikes give those decisions individual direction. A character might seek safety, recognition, companionship, discovery, wealth, devotion or revenge, prefer particular food/music/work, and dislike situations for remembered reasons. Values, enduring temperament, short-term wants and urgent bodily needs remain distinct. Conflicting motives produce choices through feasible actions and recognized obligations; a preference is not a decorative biography tag or an unconditional command. Experiences can change priorities, tastes and ambitions with understandable causes, expressed through behavior, conversation and dreams.

NPCs can visit, reconcile, compete, migrate, train, seek help, form parties, take work, change allegiance and pursue ambitions through actual actions. Distant batching preserves individual identity, capabilities and commitments. Entering the camera view must not invent a deeper person, repeat work or reroll relationships. Named lives may be compressed but must be recoverable.

Recruitment is a consequential agreement. Family, duty, wage, risk, conviction and affection can affect acceptance or departure. A recruit cannot remain a duplicate full-time worker elsewhere. Leadership does not grant unlimited authority over every resident. Players coordinate control under host permissions; NPCs retain motives and lawful refusals.

Synergies span combat, travel, sensing, work and research. Joint actions declare participants, roles, prerequisites, agreement/control, resources, readiness, interruption and credit. A scout guiding a passage, a healer supporting a team, a pilot/engineer pairing or a cooperative technique all use those contracts. Bound stacking and coordination costs keep larger groups from gaining uncontrolled pairwise bonuses.

### Slow aging, death and continuation

Players and NPCs age under the same applicable species rules, very slowly in ordinary play. With a 40-minute day, a 60–80-hour campaign spans roughly 90–120 days before pauses or acceleration. Existing elders and near-due family events show life cycles without aging young characters by decades during a short adventure. Consented long advancement and continued worlds can span generations; historical travel changes a date/address, not a traveler's biological age. Wait and offline host policies remain authoritative, with no hidden catch-up aging.

Death changes actual remains, possessions, dependants, relationships, offices and claims. The cosmology can support ghosts, hauntings, graves/memorials, afterlife planes and divine or necromantic practices. Most deaths need not become hauntings or compulsory player chores. Families, temples and specialists can perform real delegated care and remembrance. A haunting has a supported cause, permitted agency and ways to understand, settle or transform it; it may be protective or mournful as well as dangerous.

Distinguish a continuing person from a memory echo, illusion or animated corpse. A spirit/return uses explicit form transitions on one accountable identity, with supported senses/actions and real custody. Two rituals cannot produce two people or restore gear already inherited or sold. Returning after death does not automatically reverse succession or relationships. Speaking with the dead respects their knowledge, beliefs and motives rather than solving every mystery omnisciently. Healing a supported lasting injury remains reliable under its spell's rules; this is distinct from a universal guarantee that every death is reversible.

## 10. Vehicles, mounts, pets and familiars

Transport includes canoes, boats, ships, carriages, chariots, dwarven tanks, sandships, tunneling ships, air balloons and airships, plus extensible fantastical configurations. Preserve all five original vehicle identities and their progression paths. Each is a persistent assembly or actor relationship, not merely an inventory flag that grants speed and immunity.

Assemblies contain compatible frames/hulls, joints, wheels/tracks, sails, propulsion, steering, armor, storage, stations, seats, weapon or magical fittings as appropriate. Customization covers parts, materials, function, enhancement and appearance within valid structural families. Performance derives from actual load, condition, crew and capabilities. A simple canoe remains useful without requiring the complexity of an airship.

Vehicles persist when unoccupied. Boarding, cargo, crew, docking, towing, theft, wrecks and repairs use actual identity/custody. Damage and wear affect meaningful parts; replacement preserves the vessel's identity and remaining history. Cargo capacity cannot come from decorative placement. Crew work consumes time and supplies.

Travel domains are distinct: boats need valid water/access; land vehicles need width, terrain and towing/propulsion; sandships need supported substrate and slopes; tunneling ships excavate or use actual passages under geology/clearance/spoil rules; balloons need lift and have wind/steering constraints; airships add powered control and landing requirements. Tunneling cannot reset terrain or clip through occupied structures.

Mounts are living actors. Include horse types and fantastical lizard and bird families with appropriate anatomy, temperament, movement and care. Not every riding bird flies or every lizard climbs. Rider/load, saddles, harnesses, barding, packs and training must be compatible. Draft animals connect their fatigue and cooperation to carriages/chariots.

Pets and familiars have identity, bonds, abilities and appropriate needs or supernatural upkeep. Feed, water, shelter, rest and care for them; treat injury/illness where supported. Neglect has readable consequences and recovery. Keepers and stables can perform real delegated care. A magical familiar may need a different resource, but the label does not silently waive upkeep.

Companions can sense, retrieve, communicate, support, explore or fight where capable. Recall, dismissal, death and re-summoning follow an explicit lifecycle preserving the correct individual and possessions. Shared senses reveal only acquired information. NPC-owned mounts, pets and familiars use the same systems.

## 11. Combat, time and seamless transitions

Combat uses on-field command/timeline rules with full required-decision holds. Threats inhabit or emerge into the actual environment; a contextual random selection does not require a separate arena. Characters react, make brief legal staging movements, and enter a readable formation as camera, audio and controls change. The terrain, people and relevant objects remain the same.

While a required choice is pending, tactical readiness, relevant costs, statuses and coupled hazards hold together. Harmless acting, music and interface feedback may continue. Players can plan compatible allied actions without gaining an advantage from fast packets. The host determines legality and stable resolution order. There is no default countdown for a connected player's decision.

Outside attacks, healing, supplies and coupled physical changes require admission before their consequences commit. Entry, surprise, flight, re-entry and merging preserve costs, statuses, resources and identity. Loading a chunk or boarding a vehicle cannot refresh a turn or grant immunity. Relevant vessel motion, crew, cargo and supports follow one declared encounter policy.

Solo/coordinated play supports shared authoritative pause. Independent co-op deliberately distinguishes outside world time from tactical progress. The [technical timing contract](TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) specifies personal versus world deadlines, suspension, notifications, coupling and safe exit. This is an explicit game-time abstraction, not physically uniform time. It must not hide catch-up damage or manufacture paused labor.

Victory, defeat, surrender, flight or another legal outcome commits once. The world retains injury, spent resources, destroyed objects, deaths, pursuit, memories and quest/political consequences. Combat controls withdraw and exploration resumes from the resulting state. Ordinary entry/exit uses no mandatory wipe or replacement battleground. Dramatic staging remains available where a scene warrants it.

## 12. Exploration, construction and management

Dungeons and wilderness support light, senses, secrets, locks, traps, vertical movement, archaeology, tools, logistics and alternative solutions. Abilities can open routes without rendering all geography irrelevant. Supported destruction and construction alter access, hazards and ownership in the same world. Content must state what happens when an objective is destroyed or bypassed.

Generation carries forward the local roguelike topology lessons and Jaquays-style nonlinear design. Loops, multiple entrances, connected elevations, landmarks, hidden approaches and distinct social/skill routes should create practical choices: scouting, avoiding a patrol, supplying allies, returning by a shortcut or approaching a defended place from another direction. Towns and castles derive routes from land, access, use and accumulated rebuilding; dungeons and crypts have founding purposes, later occupations and changed connections. A graph with extra edges is insufficient if embedded routes merge into the same corridor or the choices have identical costs and information. Validate actual geometry, reachability, route differences and changed terrain. Deliberate gates remain possible where their purpose and alternatives are legible.

Real-time field action supports puzzles inspired by Sea of Stars and Zelda, extended through the game's shared capabilities. Push, pull, climb, balance, jump where supported, cast, redirect flow/light, manipulate a mechanism, lure a creature, perform a role or coordinate several actors. Puzzles bind to physical state, perception, knowledge and explicit triggers, with cooperative and interdisciplinary combinations. The world retains opened routes, expended materials and damaged mechanisms. Combat remains Wait-style; entering an encounter must couple relevant moving mechanisms and hazards so a held actor is not killed by another clock. Timing aids, readable cues and alternative approaches preserve accessibility without requiring every discipline to become a reflex minigame.

Sherlock-style mysteries arise from actual incidents, actors, motives and opportunities. Physical traces, documents, materials, testimony, timelines and contradictions become evidence acquired through supported investigation. The journal distinguishes observed facts, reported claims, interpretations and player/NPC hypotheses. Witnesses can be mistaken, deceptive, absent or dead; skilled inspection improves available deductions without exposing the server's hidden answer. Correct explanations require supported links between evidence and conclusions, not arbitrary exhaustive dialogue clicking.

Author procedural mysteries from a consistent incident model and a validated clue/solution graph. Important deductions need usable alternative evidence channels, including appropriate physical, social, scholarly, magical or historical approaches. Permitted destruction, altered history and autonomous NPC resolutions must produce a truthful continuation, an alternate route or a clearly explained changed outcome; they cannot leave an impossible quest silently active. Wrong accusations and invasive methods can affect relationships, law and future evidence. Content should allow uncertainty and unresolved cases rather than fabricating guilt to reward an accusation. Dream impressions retain their subjective provenance unless independently corroborated.

Players can build and operate homes, workshops, towns, fortresses, infrastructure and dungeons. Construction plans reserve space, materials, labor and authority. Workers perform jobs; structures require valid support/access and can change through damage, weather, neglect or rebuilding. Planning views operate on the same assets walked through in the field.

The world supports persistent vertical volume: tunnels beneath occupied ground, cellars and subfloors, multiple stories, stairs, ladders, ramps, shafts, bridges and compatible lifts. Physical elevation is distinct from realm or interior identity. Other planes and unusual connected interiors use explicit topology and transitions; “multidimensional” does not silently promise arbitrary continuous higher-dimensional physics. A roof is a real supported component, not just an overlay; underground construction changes collision, routes, light, water paths and structural risks where applicable. Floor selection, cutaways and occlusion keep stacked spaces readable in the established pixel-art presentation.

Mining and construction modify this authoritative terrain and occupancy. Excavation can expose deposits, chambers, water and hazards; support removal or destructive events can invalidate floors and routes. Builders can shore, reinforce, repair, drain or reroute using supported tools and materials. Tunneling vehicles use the same excavation and clearance rules. Persistent edits cross chunk boundaries and survive absence, save/load and history branching. Concurrent workers reserve real space and supplies; stale plans are revalidated before work commits.

Fortress building is integrated with ordinary exploration, NPC work, logistics, defense and society. A player can mine personally, cooperate with friends, hire workers or issue legitimate delegated plans while remaining in the same world. The resulting halls, homes, farms, workshops, walls and defenses are inhabited and used by the same actors. Blueprint and management views change interaction scale, not simulation mode or resource accounting. Practical management includes stockpile filters and minimum/maximum supply targets, job priorities, shifts, district responsibilities and delegation to stewards, foremen or quartermasters. These roles reserve actual goods/labor, respect capacity, fatigue, custody and NPC commitments, and report shortages or exceptions. They do not create supplies or force every player into a management dashboard.

The same [cross-system contracts](CROSS_SYSTEM_SYNERGIES.md) connect construction, production, households and politics to local actions and presentation. Sparse directed relationships retain established ties without allocating every possible pair. District navigation, transport and practical delegation keep credible settlement scale usable within an ordinary day; a population count or an idle crowd does not prove a living city.

Settlement institutions include markets, schools, temples, libraries, hospitals, taverns, guilds, courts and offices. They create work, information, obligations, identity and services rather than only abstract buffs. Housing, supply, security, care and migration interact. Delegation makes a functioning settlement possible while its owner adventures.

Towns and cities need credible settlement scale, neighborhoods, housing, work, roads and supply rather than a few shops implying an entire society. Residents, temporary visitors, migrants and road travelers are actual persistent individuals once realized; merchant stocks arrive through accountable supply. Population and active-simulation profiles must be qualified with the same NPC depth promised elsewhere. A candidate large-city test uses roughly 20,000 realized residents with selected detailed activity and recoverable individual distant state; this is a capacity and presentation target to prove, not an already measured shipping guarantee.

Sewers, caves, cellars, crypts, chapel foundations and buried older construction belong beneath and within these places, with actual entrances, elevation, ownership, water paths and history. A shadow-plane correspondence or another realm can supply a necessary route or solution. These spaces must be coherent across maps and time: the drain, chapel and tomb should have a plausible relationship, and a historical alteration can change access or meaning. Layer selection and quiet visual landmarks make that depth navigable without overwhelming an early character.

Player-built dungeons, mechanisms, encounter grammars and eligible creations can become shared blueprints/packages. They obey the same collision, ownership, resources and authority rules. A blueprint is not a free copy of its inhabitants or treasures. The creator interface exposes supported capabilities with validation and explanations.

Water, lava and other supported liquids use bounded conserved transfers with material/mixture, heat, viscosity, pressure/head and reaction profiles. Pumps need work/power; reservoirs and channels have finite capacity; mixing, cooling and phase changes preserve declared material sources and occupancy. Flowing lava, freezing/thawing water, floating ice and slow glaciers have different movement and hazard rules. Steam/trapped air and underwater exposure use defined game-scale approximations rather than an unrestricted chemistry or global fluid solver.

A tunnel breach, dam, melting crossing or erupting passage changes actual terrain, navigation, hazards and resources. Fluid waiting at a held encounter boundary stays in real finite storage, with legal backpressure/overflow or a wider hold; an invisible infinite pending reservoir is invalid. Rendered splashes and effects express committed state, while conservation, exposure and historic outcomes remain in the portable authority. The [fluid/environment contract](ECOLOGY_AND_FLUIDS.md) ties this to farming, travel, mining, construction, society and time travel.

## 13. Economy, politics, war and competition

Trading is a meaningful career. Shops, caravans, ships, warehouses, production contracts and routes move actual goods. Demand, supply, distance, risk, policy, quality and knowledge influence bounded local pricing. Currency/barter, debts, ownership and duties are explicit. Markets are not globally omniscient optimization routines.

Inter-town trade and caravan protection are early-to-mid-game dynamics. A player can notice a local surplus, buy a manageable load, learn of demand elsewhere, travel or join a caravan, protect its people/cargo and sell on arrival. Prices respond to local stock, production, consumption, shortages, route disruption and applicable policy; item condition and qualities matter. Travel time, provisions, tolls, guards, damage, capacity and price change can make an apparently profitable route risky. Known quotes and rumors have place, source and age; another town's exact current market is not free omniscient information.

Caravans employ actual carriers, guards, workers and companions and reserve or own real cargo. Escort agreements define participants, destination, obligations, reward, losses and interruption. Ambushes, weather, broken parts, camps, conflicting allegiances and unexpected demand can generate linked encounters and stories. NPC merchants trade through the same applicable rules; an escort event cannot conjure cargo or erase a caravan death. The early playable economy fixture proves two markets, a changing supply condition, a real journey and a guarded outcome before the later full economic and political catalog expands.

Nations, towns, guilds, factions, religious bodies and covert organizations have memberships, offices, goals, resources, claims, laws and obligations. Their directed relations include alliances, hostility, tribute, access, embargo, dependence and clandestine cooperation. Individuals retain personal motives and relationships alongside institutional allegiance.

Power structures distinguish formal office, jurisdiction and delegated authority from wealth, influence, reputation and personal loyalty. Hierarchies support defined chains of command, councils, competing institutions and covert relationships. Appointment, election, hereditary succession, removal, regency and contested claims follow supported institutional rules. Births, marriages, deaths, wars and material shortages can change eligibility, alliances, leadership and actual orders. Succession does not simply rename a leader while leaving the old actor's permissions or obligations active. NPCs act on what they know and the authority they recognize; a formal decree is not automatic personal obedience.

Castle, town, fortress, crypt and dungeon construction can reshape political geography. Founding a site creates actual buildings and occupancy; establishing a polity requires people, resources, institutional rules and recognized or contested claims. Growth can bring roads, trade, guilds, defense, taxation or tribute, diplomacy, competing jurisdiction and war. A crypt can become a sacred institution, contested ancestry claim or source of danger; a dungeon can alter security, access and local interests. Structures and inhabitants stay in the same world as political events. No separate nation-building mode resets assets, time or NPC depth, and building a throne alone does not make its owner universally recognized as sovereign.

Good, evil, neutral and lawful/chaotic alignment concepts use coherent original rules. Separate care/harm ethics and attitudes toward order/autonomy from cultural norms, public reputation, private motives and explicit divine or cosmic commitments. These dimensions can conflict and change through acts, reflection and supported transformations. Communities judge from available evidence and their own values; a reputation display does not expose secret objective morality. Powers or vows that depend on alignment declare their actual conditions and consequences. Species, lineage or a faction label does not predetermine an individual's moral choices.

Secrets have restricted knowledge and evidence. Spies, testimony, forged claims, interception and messengers can change beliefs and political action. Public UI and network projections cannot reveal secret membership or routes merely because they are authoritative facts. Intrigue, succession, negotiation and law require actors, means and consequences.

Armies use people, supplies, orders, morale, routes and territorial outcomes. Strategic resolution and detailed encounters reconcile casualties, named lives, resource use and committed outcomes. A story flag cannot create an unlimited army. Occupation changes services, authority, migration and daily life. Captivity and enslavement have actual captors/constraints and playable continuations such as escape, rescue, negotiation, revolt or succession rather than an unexplained permanent input lock.

Coliseums, tournaments, player-built arenas, competition and fame participate in society/economy. Their entry, rules, custody, rewards, injury and reputation are explicit, including the host's permissions for player conflict. They are full-scope deliverables, not omitted because the main slice focuses on adventure.

## 14. Adventurelets, encounter decks and grand narratives

Combine three sources of activity: actual systemic happenings; contextual random encounter selection; and authored adventurelets or arc fragments. Every selectable piece declares valid place/actor/history predicates, footprint, resource needs, knowledge exposure, outcomes, repetition rules and ties to existing facts. Selection can surprise the player while still fitting the world.

Decks/pools maintain deterministic draw state, weights, exhaustion or cooldown rules and accepted instance IDs. Retrying, unloading or reconnecting cannot reroll a committed draw. Existing people/resources are reserved, and newly created incidents follow explicit population/resource/arrival rules. If nothing is eligible, ordinary travel remains valid. A deck does not get to fabricate an army or reveal a secret to preserve pacing.

Adventurelets provide authored voice, spatial design and satisfying local structure. Participants bind to actual compatible people, objects and places. Reuse varies causes, constraints, relationships and available solutions—not just names. Significant outcomes become durable world history and can feed later events.

A seed establishes multiple narrative potentials rather than one immutable future. Examples include a claimant's rise, a divine schism, ecological change, rediscovered knowledge, migration, a trade struggle or a dark lord. Templates are content families to author and validate; not every seed needs every family. Potentials become active through prerequisites and actual events, may share actors/resources, and can strengthen, compete, diverge, merge or end.

The player may defeat a threat, help someone else defeat it, join it, negotiate with it, ignore it or face its expansion. Another player or NPC can resolve it independently. A former antagonist may join a party if motives, relationships and accountability permit it. No forced replacement villain is spawned solely because the player has not performed a designated final battle.

The narrative system selects truthful presentation and choices from world state. It can pace invitations and scenes, but cannot retroactively postpone a fact, duplicate rewards or erase independent outcomes. Authored relationship arcs retain participants, memories, commitments, phases and continuation rules across absence, relocation, death or branch changes. Dialogue and optional generated descriptions respect individual knowledge and authored identity.

## 15. Portals, history travel and alternate worlds

Spatial portals, other planes, historical/future states and alternate histories are native capabilities. Some quests, puzzles, materials, lifeforms, skills and creation routes depend on them; these are required product domains. Stable lineage and place anchors make differences understandable: a changed bridge, descendant, profession, monument or artifact has traceable causes.

**Reaching any point in world history is a cornerstone.** Players with the appropriate means can target a requested historical instant across generated prehistory and recorded lived history, rather than only a curated list of eras. Historical checkpoints are reconstruction accelerators. Versioned history rules, causal records and stable identities must produce a coherent place at the requested time, including dates never previously visited. Lazy realization must agree with known facts and remain independent of the order in which dates or places are explored. Preparing a distant epoch can require bounded loading work, but the system must not quietly substitute a nearby checkpoint or erase prior modifications to make the query succeed.

The generated past and the recorded lived past require distinct evidence: the former demonstrates consistent reconstruction from history rules and causal anchors; the latter preserves actual committed actions and their outcomes. Neither is permission to claim every ancient moment was originally simulated at full detail. Historical intervention creates an explicit branch with protected custody and identity, leaving other players' committed history intact. Archive/storage policy must retain the historical access promised by the game. Future travel is governed separately by supported advancement/branch rules, not a claim that unknown future player choices can be predicted.

Distinct planes can have their own geography, inhabitants, materials, visibility, hazards, environmental laws and supported travel mappings. Minecraft's Nether and D&D's ethereal concepts are inspirations for meaningful otherworlds, not content to reproduce. Crossing a plane is different from climbing a floor or visiting another historical branch. A spatially overlapping ethereal-like plane requires explicit correspondence and sensing/interaction rules; a distant hostile realm may have independent terrain and entry constraints. Plane-specific exceptions must use declared capabilities and boundary rules so ordinary movement, vehicles, effects, saving and co-op remain coherent.

Dream realms are a supported special case. A sleeping NPC's remembered experience, perceived world, desires, fears and imagination can shape a subjective space and its inhabitants. Eligible skills, powers and classes can enter, perceive, guide or interact under defined rules; dreams also have value as private characterization when no visitor enters. A dream is not a reliable database query: a remembered false belief can appear convincingly, and an unknown secret does not become known merely because its subject is dreaming. Interpretations and waking recollections retain their source and uncertainty.

Dream entry preserves the dreamer's sleeping body and each visitor's actual identity. An avatar is a controlled manifestation with explicit tether, loadout and permitted effects, not a free second actor or inventory. Sleep interruption, bodily danger, death, disconnect, an unavailable return location and dream closure require safe defined outcomes. Dreamed objects remain representations and grant no right to export physical cargo. A supported dream-derived discovery or effect may enable a separately validated waking creation or transformation, with its own real costs and material provenance; entering a dream cannot duplicate equipment or conjure symbolic ore into the waking inventory. Changed memories, bonds, recovery or learned powers commit once under supported rules; nightmares cannot apply hidden wall-time damage during held Wait decisions. Nested entry is bounded and visible. The first real tether/interruption fixture precedes full dream and planar content production.

Use the explicit branch/custody policy in the technical design. Moving a person or unique artifact between states is an accountable transfer, not a free clone. Other players' histories and assets are protected by host permissions and immutable prior records. Supported future projection is not a guarantee of predicting an unbounded autonomous future.

Persist active and archived branch identities with declared clocks and retention. A finite number of concurrently progressing branches can be a qualification envelope without erasing saved alternatives. The first retained temporal fixture appears before broad content/schema hardening; full temporal/planar gameplay arrives through named phases before desktop production completion.

## 17. Art, animation, audio and interface contract

Every gameplay domain has a shared [presentation and narrative integration contract](PRESENTATION_CONTRACTS.md): authoritative state, observable cues, valid actions, performance hooks, narrative consequences, interruption and acceptance evidence. Engine and content teams define the state and action; artists stage its readable expression; writers give the actual situation voice. Concept images establish direction, while native scenes and animated interactions establish whether the mechanic is understandable and feels alive. Acceptance has two independent bars: pixel-art finish comparable to Sea of Stars, and animation, graphical response and everyday world interaction comparable to Stardew Valley. A beautifully composed still does not satisfy the second bar. Apply the accepted restraint in detail, meaningful variation and accumulated history to both.

### Simple entry, earned depth

The default experience remains as approachable as a classic Chrono Trigger-style field and command interface. Present immediate goals, readable people and places, relevant actions, understandable costs and useful feedback. Players do not need to study the simulation's full model to travel, converse, fight, gather or make a simple item.

Skills and acquired knowledge progressively reveal relevant mechanisms. A novice miner recognizes workable rock and visible danger; an experienced miner can inspect hardness, tool suitability and support; an expert can compare a learned excavation plan's costs and risks. Herbalists learn identification, preparation and potency; smiths learn alloy/process tradeoffs; mages learn facets, interactions and supported formulae; diplomats learn evidence-based relationships and recognized institutional rules. These are useful gameplay capabilities with new decisions, explanations and informed forecasts.

Distinguish knowing a general rule from knowing its hidden inputs. Expertise cannot expose an undiscovered deposit, secret allegiance or unknown enchantment through an exact tooltip, score or forecast. Show uncertainty and the evidence behind a conclusion. NPCs reason through their own acquired skills and knowledge under the same principle. Essential controls, accessibility, explicit action costs and known serious dangers are available at every skill level; lack of expertise must not turn routine play into unexplained punishment.

Advanced inspection is optional. Mastery improves comprehension, delegation and control without forcing every expert into a dense permanent interface. Simple and advanced views issue the same authoritative actions. Developer diagnostics and unrestricted internal algorithms remain separate from character knowledge. Human validation compares novice, growing and expert characters completing the same meaningful activities, and checks that returning to a concise view remains comfortable.

Preserve the accepted restrained 16-bit+ direction and all earlier options. The five reference scenes remain positively received with a repetition refinement. Actual wealth, taste, occupation, weather, customs and provenance shape possessions. Readable silhouettes, a few meaningful variants and visible repair/history take precedence over repeated motifs or sensory clutter. Interactive objects have real state and custody. Dedicated artists work from the same cultural/process/body contracts as engine and narrative builders.
