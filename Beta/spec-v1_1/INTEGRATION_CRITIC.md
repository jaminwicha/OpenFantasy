## Integration conclusions

**Scope and a usable beginning coexist.** Original classes, quests, places, vehicles and other Alpha content remain required. All RQ-001–150 texts are unchanged. Culture/body/technology additions have real mechanics and native obligations. Teaching selects grounded opportunities; it cannot force NPC decisions, supply free items or grant powers when a player skips guidance.

**Time is an explicit abstraction.** Required-decision Wait, outside co-op progression, fixed-world appointments, biological exposure and consented advancement remain distinct. Relevant bodies, vegetation, fluids, vehicles and supports cannot gain hidden costs or free work from another clock. The 40-minute day and isolated no-companion/refused-acceleration rest remain early UX qualifications. Existing ages and near-due family events support slow ordinary aging without promising a human generation during one adventure.

**Environmental history preserves identity.** Planar macro provinces and finite dependencies avoid an implicit infinite global solve. Stacked terrain differs from other planes; aquifers differ from traversable caves. Sources, phases, cohorts and realized individuals have accountable ownership. Ancient refinement and recorded lived history require different evidence, but neither may substitute checkpoint dates or erase committed interventions. Capacity remains unproved.

**Culture and death use shared actors and materials.** Comprehension differs from truth, permission and localization. Learned norms, nationality, inherited tendencies and directed personal relations remain distinguishable and changeable. Layouts, equipment lineages and civilizational methods depend on actual space, bodies, supplies and work. All eleven named species families require native coverage. Death revokes living-body control without forbidding a separately granted eligible spirit form; return reconciles one person and custody without cloning an estate or undoing succession. Conditional hauntings and delegated care avoid compulsory memorial chores.

**Presentation is part of completion.** Pixel finish and responsive native animation are independent human gates. Tool contact, anatomy, equipment, paired acting, cultural spaces, ecological cues and interruption reach manifests and fixtures. Sound requires semantic triggers, listener scope, intentional quiet, restrained mixing and long-session acceptance. Decorative or musical cues cannot expose secrets or invent huntable actors. Accepted concept stills close none of these native gates.

**Staging preserves the full production obligation.** P00 enumerates finite definitions/generated families; P11 reconciles semantic migration and native art/audio/UI/accessibility rather than only source archives. P12 requalifies complete-world load and experience, P13 requires every desktop domain, and P14 completes mobile interoperability. Estimates follow measured accepted-content throughput. Selective ecosystem use, one authority, separately owned art/audio and interchangeable Codex/Claude leads agree across the documents.
