# Archive-to-current design continuity audit

8 September 2026. Audited against the living 181-requirement specification, after the explicit chat, management and additive-species corrections described below. This is a semantic document audit, not a playable-content migration result.

**Finding:** no wanted feature family reviewed here remains orphaned solely in the archives. The current GDD, technical/focused contracts, requirements and full production plan carry those families forward. Three details had become too implicit during consolidation; they are now explicit in the living documents and have retained validation obligations. Old implementation proposals that conflict with later decisions remain preserved as history, without becoming competing current requirements.

This supports beginning the retained P00 work in the [implementation handoff](IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md). It does not certify that every sentence in every historical document has been converted into an executable feature, that the 201-file Alpha snapshot has been fully semantically migrated, or that any Beta runtime/asset/playtest gate has passed.

## Evidence and method

The [archive index](../archive/index.json) identifies seven snapshots containing 92 manifested files. Their [archive policy](../archive/README.md) preserves editions and source paths without editing historical wording. The separate [Alpha migration inventory and plan](../content-migration/README.md) covers source, assets, finite definitions, code-defined behavior and generated families. These are distinct preservation scopes: the 92 design/archive files are not a backup of the complete running game and all artwork.

The coordinator's mechanical pass checked the 92 archived payloads and 201 Alpha source payloads, source hashes and original requirement preservation. Those checks establish file integrity and traceability. This audit adds a different kind of evidence:

1. Read the archive index/manifests and the older product/system sections, owner-direction addenda, specialist designs, adopted combat decision and architecture synthesis.
2. Follow their player-facing and architectural obligations into current GDD headings, technical owners, focused contracts, requirement rows and completion phases.
3. Compare archived specification 1.0 with current GDD/TDD/plan/decisions, inspecting replaced passages rather than assuming a copied directory preserves meaning.
4. Distinguish required outcomes from illustrative scenarios, tuning hypotheses, unadopted alternatives and superseded engineering constraints.
5. Check particularly vulnerable details: ordinary careers, operated dungeons/arenas, legacy identities and routes, social nonhumans, management usability, player communication, artistic options, optional AI, creator packages and later platform scope.
6. Report missing explicit carry-forward to the coordinator, then inspect the actual corrections and their unrun validation cases.

The 1.0-to-1.1 GDD comparison is especially strong evidence of continuity: its replaced passages concerned the title/baseline, predecessor naming, the expanded audio contract and the research summary. The earlier substantive system prose remains, with additions. Technical changes extend geological history, ecological state and death/form semantics; the older generated-baseline-plus-overlay wording is refined because a moving, eroding world needs ordered process history. Keeping the older sentence verbatim would have preserved an architectural defect rather than the intended historical continuity.

## Remaining evidence boundaries

The living main spec is no longer dependent on recovering the three identified details from archived prose. No additional known orphan family was found in the reviewed sources. The current GDD still points to the full requirement register, migration plan, contracts and phases; a shorter main document is not a content cut when its detail remains normative, reachable and assigned.

There is nevertheless real work remaining, explicitly required by the handoff:

- **P00 semantic inventory:** classify the 187 Alpha documentation headings, code-only behavior, intended/superseded concepts and later source deltas into concrete migration records. This audit is not a substitute for that field-by-field/content-level work.
- **P02/P11 playable incorporation:** preserve the faithful authored-map realization and integrate every required identity, route, asset and generated family into supported Beta play. A ZIP, database row or museum-only map does not pass MIG-04.
- **Compatibility reconciliation:** record original and Beta behavior, transforms, aliases, dependencies, content placement, artistic mapping and valid starting fixtures. Do not revive a dead quest target to force an old script; retain the original reachable route and truthful later consequences.
- **Empirical gates:** prove portable execution, exact-time history, recovery, co-op timing, NPC depth, native bodies/art/audio and sustained enjoyment in retained builds. Documentation continuity cannot establish any of these results.

Archive integrity, semantic continuity, complete source migration and gameplay quality are four different claims. The first has mechanical evidence; this document supplies bounded evidence for the second; the latter two remain planned work with explicit completion gates. Further revisions must keep the same distinction and preserve both original records and the reasons for intentional changes.
