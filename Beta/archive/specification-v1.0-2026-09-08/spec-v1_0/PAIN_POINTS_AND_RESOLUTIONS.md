# Pain points, contradictions and recommended resolutions

This review follows the completed [expanded research synthesis](../research-v0_8/README.md). It evaluates the integrated GDD, architecture, full production plan and cross-system contracts. Recommendations preserve the required breadth while making unavoidable tradeoffs explicit. They are design judgments with named proof gates; they are not measured claims about an unbuilt game.

## Overall recommendation

Build a new Beta authority with Godot C# presentation, selectively reuse proven original algorithms and tests, and fully incorporate original OpenFantasy content. Deepen RogueFiction's sensing, contextual narrative and consequences through shared actors and history. Combining the concepts is worthwhile; retaining incompatible old state owners is not. The [original review](../architecture-decision-review-2026-09-08.md) provides executable evidence for the persistence gap that motivates this choice.

Retain C# provisionally for the core and close the language qualification gate before broad P04 production. Whole Rust behind a coarse in-process C ABI is a credible alternative if representative workloads or a concrete embedding need warrant it. It requires native integration but not IPC. Do not maintain two complete cores or choose a language as a substitute for proving determinism, scheduling and archives. See the [full reassessment](../research-v0_8/csharp-rust-core-reassessment.md).

The design can be specified rigorously before implementation. It cannot be proved fun, performant or affordable entirely on paper. Readiness means named ownership and contracts, preserved scope, and early tests that can stop an unsuccessful approach before bulk work. “No doubts” must mean no concealed decision or unsupported assurance, rather than pretending uncertainty disappears.

## Foundational tensions

| Concern | Viable alternatives | Recommended resolution and cost | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arbitrary time travel versus finite computation | Simulate every ancient detail up front; restrict travel to authored eras; or lazily reconstruct exact requested instants under stable historical constraints | Choose consistent lazy ancient refinement plus lossless recorded lived history. Do not substitute checkpoints for destinations. The past is generated at defined fidelity, not a claim that eons ran at full detail. Preparation may visibly take time. | P03 unprepared-instant/query-order/restart and same-instant cursor proof; P10 chronology qualification |
| Historical access versus save growth | Delete old intervals; retain only summaries; or preserve sufficient ordered state changes with compact indexes/shared prefixes | Preserve every promised lived interval and versioned interpretation. Compression and checkpoint rebuilding must be lossless for that contract. Storage grows with realized consequential activity; capacity/backpressure must be visible. No finite machine supports literally unlimited recording. | LANG/TERR archive bytes per world hour, reconstruction latency, storage-pressure recovery |
| Wait decisions versus independently progressing co-op | Pause everyone; impose timers; or accept local tactical stasis | Shared pause remains solo/coordinated default. Independent play explicitly uses local stasis with admission and whole dependency closure. No retroactive hunger/damage or free outside supply. This is a declared fictional discontinuity, not uniform physical chronology. | P02/P03 long holds, moving vessels, hazards, outsiders, deadlines and closure overload |
| NPC equality versus realistic cities | Shallow decorative residents; fully detail every decision continuously; or persist deep individuals with sparse state and scheduled cognition | Choose the third. Intrinsic capabilities/history stay equal; cadence and representation vary. Candidate 20,000-resident qualification must include work, travel, relationships and news—not idle sprites. Complexity is in relevance, storage and backlog, not only raw CPU. | LANG/SOC; P07 full-city native and scheduler evidence |
| “Infinite” frontier versus authored quality and travel | Dense repeated events; long empty loading corridors; or coherent macro-worlds with authored grammars and selective density | Use meaningful regional variation, historical constraints and landmarks, with wilderness supporting actual travel/care/building. No compulsory fight exists merely to disguise a load. The finite authored grammar and art catalog can still become repetitive and need expansion. | FRONT/PLACE/PX; long unfamiliar-seed journeys and second-region production |
| C# versus Rust | Portable C#; whole Rust authority; or measured kernels | C# is selected now. Rust's ownership/GC advantages are real but not yet shown to dominate this workload. Keep language-neutral archives/commands, test tails and allocation, and change early only with whole-workflow evidence. | Q-07/LANG before broad P04; requalify later full workloads |
| Full import versus stronger Beta semantics | Copy old runtime behavior literally; drop awkward content; or preserve identities/intents with explicit rule reconciliation | Fully import content, authored routes and source provenance; adapt timing, quantities and state ownership with documented mappings. Preserve faithful fixtures and truthful changed-world continuations. Byte archives alone are not migration completion. | P00 inventory/P02 original loop/P11 semantic and native catalog |

## Player-experience tensions

| Concern | Viable alternatives | Recommended resolution and cost | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smooth advancement versus destructive freedom | Indestructible critical paths; omnipotent repair quests; or viable starts with accountable later consequences | Select constructive supported-start progression witnesses, diverse learning/supply routes and revalidation after events. Preserve harder optional gates. A destroyed teacher or clue can make a specific chain impossible; offer truthful recovery/continuation where feasible, not fabricated victory. Stronger invulnerable-path protection would be a different product rule requiring explicit revision. | PLACE/P03/P12; poverty, lost supplies, missing teacher and changed-event cases |
| Mysteries versus mutable worlds, dreams and history | Protect every clue; conjure replacement evidence; or allow uncertain/cold cases | Preserve incident truth, evidence provenance and multiple clues for supported solvable states. Dreams are subjective leads; another branch does not automatically prove this branch's crime. Destruction may produce an unresolved case with meaningful consequences. | PLACE/SK/SYN-05/14 |
| Forty-minute days versus generations and eons | Accelerate lifespans; force time skips; or keep life stages credible with existing ages, near-due events and optional advancement | Keep species-appropriate development and a tunable 40-minute complete day. At that rate, 60–80 hours gives roughly 90–120 days before pauses or acceleration, not a human generation. Generational play uses consented advancement, continued worlds and historical travel; it must not silently age children in a few days. | SOC/REST/P12 ordinary-career and historical campaigns |
| Sleep versus split-party attention | Force everyone to sleep; make recovery instant; or retain real rest with consented acceleration and useful control options | Preserve actual exposure/recovery, watches, care, interruption and consent. Companion/delegated work helps when available. A lone actor whose peers refuse acceleration remains a specific UX risk; qualify it before broad production. If boring, revise the recovery/control design openly while preserving clock integrity. | P03/P04 no-companion/refused-acceleration human and actual-build tests |
| Autonomous crises versus a polished adventure | Protect one main plot; force the player into events; or build responsive arcs and independent resolutions | Provide a substantial adventure path through many grounded arcs and continuations, including no-dark-lord seeds. Preserve NPC agency and ordinary careers. A 60–80-hour quality target cannot guarantee one identical sequence or survival of every favorite character. | SW/SOC/P09/P12 attention, pacing and independent-resolution runs |
| Deep simulation versus simple JRPG presentation | Hide rules permanently; expose dashboards immediately; or reveal justified detail through progression | Use practical intentions and readable feedback first. Separate learning a rule from observing its inputs and gaining control. Expertise can explain more without revealing secrets, and experts may retain the concise view. Delegation must be practical enough to support adventuring alongside institutions. | PX/SK/P02 novice/growing/expert comparison; SYN-16 |
| Realistic settlement scale versus walking and chores | Miniature towns; meaningless fast teleportation; or navigable districts with actual transit and delegation | Build credible connected neighborhoods and inhabitants. Provide landmarks, district navigation, transport and services whose time/cost consequences remain real. Measure travel friction across a normal day; physical size alone does not establish immersion. | PLACE/P07/P12 city errands, work and departures |
| Rare injuries versus cumulative attrition | Cosmetic scars only; common limb loss; or low-frequency serious injury with reliable declared restoration | Preserve scars as the common lasting mark and rare functional losses. Calibrate per-character campaign incidence and community burden, not merely a small per-hit probability. Show practical adaptations and accessible recovery; the appropriate spell heals its supported injury without hidden immunity exceptions. | P05/P12 anatomy, economy, frequency and native motion |

|---|---|---|---|

| Time/planes versus economic duplication | Unrestricted copied cargo; no meaningful transit; or explicit origin claims and transfer eligibility | Keep actual travelers, unique artifacts and material claims accountable; branch-local use can be broader than cross-branch export. If a gate fails, revise transparently rather than marking empty scaffolds complete. | P02/P12/P13 full coverage, acceptance and operations |

## What must be proven first

The earliest retained work should resolve the risks that could invalidate broad production: portable authority and replay; full-world durability; one real original-content loop; mixed-clock custody/hazards; mutable stacked terrain; deep NPC work; and exact historical reconstruction/branch transfer. The plan also brings native animation, simple versus expert UI and two-town trade into the early slices. This ordering prevents a polished showcase from hiding absent foundations.

P00–P03 may begin against the selected contracts. Broad P04 expansion is conditional on the language and foundational evidence gates, while bulk art requires actual native-motion acceptance. Neither a completed document nor a critic's closed wording issue closes those executable gates. Mobile remains a required later phase, and the C# front end's actual export/device limits need their own proof regardless of core language.

The remaining choices are explicit design defaults with qualification triggers, rather than hidden gaps. The largest owner-facing tradeoffs are local stasis during split-party Wait, truthful failure after destructive world changes, generated ancient-history fidelity, visible historical preparation/storage costs, and rare injuries with restoration. Changing one of those defaults is possible, but its authority, progression, save and production consequences must be reflected across the specifications before dependent work expands.
