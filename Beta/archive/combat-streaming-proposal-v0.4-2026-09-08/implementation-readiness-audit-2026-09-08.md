# GDD synthesis and implementation readiness

8 September 2026. This audit incorporates the owner's latest art, Godot/C# and Wait-style combat decisions. It reviews the design package and existing evidence; it does not claim a new runtime, import, benchmark or playtest has been completed.

## Recommendation

**Synthesize one authoritative GDD now, alongside a technical design and a decision-closure register. Review the implementation plan against that consolidated specification before beginning the first retained implementation increment.** Further research should answer specific remaining decisions; another general survey of reference games is unlikely to settle our principal uncertainties.

Do not label a compilation of the existing documents “final” while unresolved contracts remain buried inside it. The first synthesis should be a release-baseline candidate. Resolve the decisions needed to code its foundations, then implement small, permanent increments with their regression tests. Their evidence earns the next production commitment. No disposable engine, competing combat implementation or second-language prototype is planned.

We can aim for no untracked requirements, contradictory rules, unowned decisions or untested claims presented as facts. We cannot establish enjoyment, cross-platform correctness, performance or production throughput entirely through documents. “No doubts” must mean explicit evidence and controlled revision, rather than a promise that no future learning will require a refactor.

## Decisions now established

- Godot with C# is the selected stack. The authoritative core remains engine-independent, deterministic and seeded. Godot APIs, rendering, transport and platform storage stay in adapters.
- Initial code must contribute to the intended implementation. Build proofs in the real modules and retain fixtures, importers and tests. Improve a failing design in place; do not preserve defective code merely to claim nothing was discarded.
- The lead combat direction is on-field command/timeline combat with cooperative techniques and Wait-style decisions, described by the owner as “like chrono without real time mode on.” Direct real-time action is no longer a competing flagship implementation. Exact pause boundaries and shared-world interactions still need specification; this statement does not claim exact historical Chrono Trigger timing semantics.
- Complete original OpenFantasy content assimilation, deeper selected RogueFiction narrative/quest concepts, seeded history and portable core boundaries remain required.
- The six style candidates and five new scenes remain preserved. The owner likes all five scenes and identifies excessive repeated sprites. Keep the accepted visual treatment while improving variety within and across compositions. Native assets and motion remain unvalidated.
- Expressive personality/emotion/action animation, interactive people/animals/objects, scene choreography and deep persistent NPC-to-NPC/player relationships are required. Their state and performance contracts are developed in the linked narrative and animation designs.
- Dedicated artists own visual production; either Codex or Claude can lead design or implementation. Antigravity remains assigned to bounded maintenance.

The exact launch device/OS list, minimum hardware and supported simultaneous-player count are still unspecified. “Godot multiplatform” selects the technology and portability intent; it does not prove every export or establish a release-support promise for every device.

## What the existing evidence does and does not settle

The [v0.2 GDD](persistent_seed_jrpg_gdd_v0_2.md) contains 79 requirements, 32 named acceptance scenarios and ten explicit architecture decisions. The [direction delta](requirements-delta-v0_3.json) adds the subsequent owner requirements. The broad domain coverage is valuable, but many tests are only descriptions and several key rules remain proposals.

The [earlier code review](review-evidence/README.md) recorded a successful original-game build, 40 self-tests, an engine-free simulation compilation and a two-client connectivity smoke test. It also demonstrated incomplete state hashing and missing durable world/generated-quest state. Those findings support selective algorithm reuse with stronger Beta state contracts. They do not establish Beta persistence, networked combat, temporal safety or scalability. The two-client test recorded no damage events.

The [migration inventory](content-migration/README.md) preserves 201 source files and indexes 532 structured records, ten code-defined entries and 187 documentation sections. Its source-to-Beta mappings and playable evidence are still empty. File preservation is complete for its stated snapshot scope; semantic reconciliation and executable import are unfinished.

The five art scenes prove that we can communicate the desired atmosphere with existing content references. They do not yet prove a usable native sprite grid, stable camera scale, animation, editable exports, tiling, dynamic weather, variation quality or repeatable asset throughput. The latest repetition feedback is a concrete production concern to resolve in the asset family and placement pipeline.

## Targeted research still worth doing

**Platform/export support is a concrete constraint.** The Godot 4.6 C# documentation lists Windows, Linux and macOS support, with Android and iOS marked experimental. Browser export is not currently supported for C# projects in that documentation. This selects an export-validation matrix, not a reason to replace the owner's chosen stack. The installed original project references Godot.NET.Sdk 4.4.1 and .NET 8; do not silently upgrade it or presume that reading newer documentation upgrades Beta. Pin the intended successor toolchain and verify it. [Godot C# platform support](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/tutorials/scripting/c_sharp/index.html), [C# web-export limitation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/tutorials/export/exporting_for_web.html).

Godot documents headless/dedicated exports; the portable core must still have its own engine-free runner so domain correctness does not depend on a particular Godot host. The first implementation plan should choose one production hosting path and reuse the same core for local play. [Godot dedicated-server exports](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/tutorials/export/exporting_for_dedicated_servers.html).

Other research should be bounded by an implementation decision: package execution and mobile ahead-of-time compilation if mobile is in scope; storage/replay behavior under our transaction model; the art editor/export workflow actually available to artist agents; and chronology/aggregation techniques for the selected temporal contract. Each investigation must finish with a choice, a retained verification case or a stated constraint.

The earlier [reference research](reference-architecture-research-2026-09-08.md) identifies limits to proprietary-engine evidence and incomplete Starbound package research. Those limits do not block Beta unless we propose adopting the undocumented behavior. Deeper reference studies can support specific mechanics later; attempting to prove their unavailable internals will not settle our own combat clocks, import coverage or art throughput.

## Order of work

1. **Consolidate the GDD candidate.** Integrate the complete baseline, accepted owner changes, mandatory assimilation and latest art feedback. Preserve requirement IDs; archive previous editions and companion files before writing a new version, then replace superseded decision language in that active edition. Keep exact algorithms, formats and host APIs in a companion technical design.
2. **Close the contracts needed for implementation and review the plan.** Resolve the register into decisions or explicitly bounded evidence gates. Review spatial/temporal identity, Wait combat, imports, platforms and art together. Give every required domain a delivery stage; no plan may quietly reduce the vision to the first region.
3. **Implement retained foundations.** Build the real pure C# core, command/receipt/persistence path, headless host and minimal Godot presentation. Use the actual content package and one connected original-content import. Tests, fixtures, adapters and source mappings remain in the project.
4. **Prove the expensive interactions early.** Exercise two clients with Wait combat, restart/retry, a tiny temporal branch, coarse/local transitions, one sanctioned extension and native art variation/motion. These are narrow increments of the selected design, not competing disposable games.
5. **Review evidence before scaling.** Complete a coherent adventure/community/repair loop, then produce a second region using the same tools. Revise budgets and the remaining implementation plan from observed results before bulk assets and campaign production.

The resulting “final GDD” should be a versioned baseline for implementation, with controlled changes when evidence warrants them. The immediate deliverable is synthesis and contract closure, not another five reference scenes or an unbounded research phase.
